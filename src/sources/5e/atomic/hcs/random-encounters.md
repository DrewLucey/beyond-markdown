# Appendix E: Random Encounters {#hcs:random-encounters:appendixerandomencounters}

test your heroism

Here are a few random encounter tables to both provide inspiration for any encounter you may wish to build, and to flesh out the encounters in your game.

## City Encounters {#hcs:random-encounters:cityencounters}

(Levels 2-4)

| d8  | Encounter                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | A [**mapach tinkerer**](#ref:monster:4237041-mapach-tinkerer "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4237041-mapach-tinkerer") with a hand cart of goods distracts the party with his marvelous wares while his [**hedge bard**](#ref:monster:4237032-hedge-bard "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4237032-hedge-bard") partner attempts to pickpocket them.                                   |
| 2   | An innocent [**cervan priest**](#ref:monster:4237021-cervan-priest "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4237021-cervan-priest") is accosted by 1d4 [**birdfolk guards**](#ref:monster:4237014-birdfolk-guard "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4237014-birdfolk-guard"). These birdfolk are criminals who’ve stolen Perch Guard uniforms, and the party will be rewarded for their capture. |
| 3   | A visiting [**vulpin noble**](#ref:monster:4237053-vulpin-noble "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4237053-vulpin-noble") is interested in seeing the sights. Show them around town by rolling again on this table. They will accompany the party for this encounter, and should they survive, will reward the party 20 gp for the invigorating time.                                       |
| 4   | A gathering of acrobatic street performers. If high enough level, a party member with the Sharp Mind trait, or who makes a DC 14 Intelligence check, will recognize a [**gallus monk**](#ref:monster:4237029-gallus-monk "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4237029-gallus-monk") among them from a wanted poster.                                                                          |
| 5   | A citywide parade to honor Gaspard, the Champion. There are jousts, fencing matches, and other contests in which the party can participate. Defeat a [**jerbeen swashbuckler**](#ref:monster:4237034-jerbeen-swashbuckler "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4237034-jerbeen-swashbuckler") in a duel to win a magic item.                                                                  |
| 6   | A mysterious death. If players are high enough level, this could lead to a fight with the [**corvum assassin**](#ref:monster:4237023-corvum-assassin "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4237023-corvum-assassin") who was paid to perform the murder.                                                                                                                                       |
| 7   | A bar fight breaks out between 1d4 [**mapach bandit**](#ref:monster:4237039-mapach-bandit "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4237039-mapach-bandit") patrons and 1d4 [**birdfolk militia**](#ref:monster:4237015-birdfolk-militia "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4237015-birdfolk-militia").                                                                                           |
| 8   | A pair of [**jerbeen thieves**](#ref:monster:4237035-jerbeen-thief "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4237035-jerbeen-thief") steal from the party. This leads back to their [**vulpin captain**](#ref:monster:4237052-vulpin-captain "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4237052-vulpin-captain") leader, who runs a pickpocketing ring in town.                                           |

## Coast Encounters {#hcs:random-encounters:coastencounters}

(Level 4)

| d8  | Encounter                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | The party finds a small shrine to Gesme within a hidden grotto. The first player to pray here gains the ability to cast [**clairvoyance**](#ref:spell:2028-clairvoyance "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2028-clairvoyance") once, without material components.                                                              |
| 2   | 2 [**raptor explorers**](#ref:monster:4237044-raptor-explorer "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4237044-raptor-explorer") are leading a [**strig tracker**](#ref:monster:4237049-strig-tracker "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4237049-strig-tracker") bounty hunter to the party.                                      |
| 3   | 1d4 giant petrels circle overhead (use the stat block for a [**giant eagle**](#ref:monster:16881-giant-eagle "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16881-giant-eagle"), but with a 30 ft. swim speed).                                                                                                                          |
| 4   | A violent storm rolls in. If the party chooses not to take shelter, they must make a DC 14 Constitution saving throw while traveling, suffering a level of [**exhaustion**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#exhaustioncondition) on a failure.                                                |
| 5   | A mass of seaweed turns out to be a [**shambling mound**](#ref:monster:17011-shambling-mound "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/17011-shambling-mound").                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 6   | A ship is stolen from the docks by 1d4 +1 [**birdfolk sailor**](#ref:monster:4237016-birdfolk-sailor "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4237016-birdfolk-sailor") pirates, led by a [**jerbeen swashbuckler**](#ref:monster:4237034-jerbeen-swashbuckler "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4237034-jerbeen-swashbuckler"). |
| 7   | A sinking ship is spotted with its crew aboard. There are 1d3 [**hunter sharks**](#ref:monster:16928-hunter-shark "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16928-hunter-shark") circling in the water.                                                                                                                             |
| 8   | A crashing wave sweeps in, and the party must make a DC 14 Strength saving throw or be swept 80 feet into the sea, where a [**water elemental**](#ref:monster:17051-water-elemental "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/17051-water-elemental") lies in wait.                                                                 |

## Forest Encounters {#hcs:random-encounters:forestencounters}

(Level 1-2)

| d8  | Encounter                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | 2d4 [**mapach bandits**](#ref:monster:4237039-mapach-bandit "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4237039-mapach-bandit") ambush the party.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| 2   | 1d2 [**swarms of emberbats**](#ref:monster:4237050-swarm-of-emberbats "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4237050-swarm-of-emberbats") attack the party.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 3   | 1d4 [**magmin**](#ref:monster:16949-magmin "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16949-magmin") can be spotted burning foliage through the trees. They haven’t started a fire yet, but they will if left unchecked.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 4   | The party finds a moss covered shrine to Reya. The first character that stops to pray here gains the ability to cast [**elevated sight**](#ref:spell:2294144-elevated-sight "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2294144-elevated-sight") once.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 5   | 1d4 [**mapach bandits**](#ref:monster:4237039-mapach-bandit "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4237039-mapach-bandit") and a [**jerbeen thief**](#ref:monster:4237035-jerbeen-thief "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4237035-jerbeen-thief") spring from the forest, offering safe passage for a road toll of 3 gp per party member. They will attack if the toll is not paid immediately.                                                                           |
| 6   | 1d3 [**wolves**](#ref:monster:17062-wolf "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/17062-wolf") led by a [**dire wolf**](#ref:monster:16841-dire-wolf "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16841-dire-wolf") attack the party.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 7   | A [**birdfolk skirmisher**](#ref:monster:4237018-birdfolk-skirmisher "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4237018-birdfolk-skirmisher") on patrol is struggling in a large spider web and calling for help. He has been bound in silk, requiring a DC 15 Strength check or one round using a cutting tool to free him. There are 1d2 +1 [**giant spiders**](#ref:monster:16895-giant-spider "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16895-giant-spider") hidden in the trees. |
| 8   | A bandit’s net trap has been laid across the trail and covered with leaves. It can be spotted with a DC 15 Wisdom ([**Perception**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills)) check. If players walk into it, the trap springs, and players must make a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw to avoid getting caught. On a failure, you can roll on this table again to complicate the encounter.                                               |

| 8   | A field of geysers lays ahead, each intermittently spraying scalding gases into the air. Pass or fail, after 3 saving throws they’ve made it across. Alternatively, party members can choose to go around. If so, roll again on this table. |
