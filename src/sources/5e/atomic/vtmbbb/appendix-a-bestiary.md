# Appendix A: Bestiary {#vtmbbb:appendix-a-bestiary:appendixabestiary}

This appendix provides stat blocks for creatures that characters may encounter in the _Vampire: The Masquerade_ setting. The following stat blocks are presented in alphabetical order.

## Highland Wolf {#vtmbbb:appendix-a-bestiary:highlandwolf}

Large packs of wolves hunt across the highlands, rarely choosing to interact with humans. When food becomes scarce or when travelers become separated from their groups, these thickly furred canines become a deadly threat.

## Lupine {#vtmbbb:appendix-a-bestiary:lupine}

[![A lupine with an axe under a hail of arrows. Black and white illustration (Artist: Ron Spencer)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/vtmbbb/FIPjWHwuc1dxehRt/03-002.lupine.jpg)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/vtmbbb/FIPjWHwuc1dxehRt/03-002.lupine.jpg)

Werewolves, or what Kindred call the lupines, are another powerful denizen of the supernatural world. Lupines are overwhelmingly strong, terrifyingly fast, immensely resilient, and have held a frenzied level of hatred for the Kindred for as long as either side can remember. When one is not taking the shape of a human or a wolf, it stands on two legs to become a ten-foot-tall killing machine of fur, claws, and fangs who can split stone with a single slash and endure horrific amounts of damage. A single lupine is more than a match for a group of younger Kindred, and even powerful Kindred are wise to give pause before engaging unprepared.

**Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)

#### Traits {#vtmbbb:appendix-a-bestiary:lupine:6631104-lupine:Traits}

**_Rage._** The lupine can give itself [**Advantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#advantage) on its first [**D20 Test**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#d20test) each turn. If it does so and fails that D20 Test, it’s overcome with rage. On each of its turns while raging, the lupine attacks the nearest creature it can see. If no creature is near enough to move to and attack, its rage ends. A lupine’s ally, if within 60 feet of the raging lupine, can try to calm it by taking an action to make a DC 15 Charisma ([**Persuasion**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills)) check; the lupine must be able to hear its ally. If this check succeeds, the lupine ceases its rage.

**_Regeneration._** The lupine regains 10 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns. If the lupine takes Fire damage or damage from silver objects, this trait doesn’t function at the start of its next turn. The lupine dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn’t regenerate.

#### Actions {#vtmbbb:appendix-a-bestiary:lupine:6631104-lupine:Actions}

**_Multiattack._** The lupin makes two attacks, using Battleaxe, Rend, or Longbow in any combination. If it hits with a Rend attack, the lupine can make one Bite attack against the same target as a Bonus Action.

**_Battleaxe (Humanoid or Hybrid Form Only)._** _Melee Attack Roll:_ +8, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 10 (1d10 + 5) Slashing damage.

**_Bite (Wolf or Hybrid Form Only)._** _Melee Attack Roll:_ +8, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 16 (2d10 + 5) Piercing damage.

**_Rend (Wolf or Hybrid Form Only)._** _Melee Attack Roll:_ +8, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 12 (2d6 + 5) Slashing damage.

**_Longbow (Humanoid or Hybrid Form Only)._** _Ranged Attack Roll:_ +6, range 150/600 ft. _Hit:_ 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage.

#### Bonus Actions {#vtmbbb:appendix-a-bestiary:lupine:6631104-lupine:BonusActions}

**_Horrifying Howl (Recharge 6)._** _Wisdom Saving Throw:_ DC 14, each creature within 60 feet that can hear the howl and isn’t a lupine. _Failure:_ The target has the [**Frightened**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#frightenedcondition) condition and repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. After 1 minute, it succeeds automatically.

**_Shape-Shift._** The lupine [**shape-shifts**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#shapeshifting) into a Large wolf-humanoid hybrid form or a Medium wolf, or it returns to its true humanoid form. Its game statistics, other than its size, are the same in each form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying isn’t transformed.
