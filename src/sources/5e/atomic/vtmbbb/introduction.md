# Introduction {#vtmbbb:introduction:introduction}

## A World of Darkness {#vtmbbb:introduction:aworldofdarkness}

You are already intimately familiar with our world. The sun rises, the sun sets, and, in between, people go about the business of living. Most of them never look too closely at what moves in the dark. The World of Darkness is a mirror of our world, our history, our cities. The horror of what you see in this mirror lives in the margins of the familiar.

Here, vampires have walked among humanity since before recorded history, moving through every age and empire, every era of plague and plenty. They are not a modern invention but an ancient entity, a continuous secret underpinning civilization as a whole.

That secret sounds rather different depending on the century.

In the Dark Ages, the world was lit only by fire. Most of Europe was forest. Old Roman roads cut through it in straight lines that represented a distant civilization’s expression of confidence. Outside the cities, the landscape is largely unmapped and ungoverned, full of things that have not yet been named and are certainly not waiting to be. It is a world of faith and superstition, of feudal courts and a wilderness that starts twenty yards past the last hut in the hamlet and does not end in any particular location.

This is the world of _Vampire: The Dark Ages_, and the setting of the included adventure “[One Last Goodbye](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/vtmbbb/one-last-goodbye).” It is a fundamentally different world for vampires to inhabit than the modern nights their descendants will eventually learn to navigate. The political machinery that defines later vampiric society gets built here. Right now, it is still very raw.

In the modern era, vampires maintain what they call the Masquerade: a centuries-old tradition of concealment, a collective agreement to stay invisible to a mortal population that would destroy them if it truly believed. Vampires in the modern world exist inside that agreement, are shaped by it, constrained by it.

The Masquerade does not exist yet in the Dark Ages. Its foundation is still being built. Vampires have not yet collectively codified how they will interface with mortal civilization. Every peasant already believes in demons. The monstrous man in the castle who has not aged since the lord’s father was a boy is, to the village below, a basic fact of local life. The stakes of discovery are real, but faith does the work that secrecy will later have to do, and faith is much less precise. A vampire lord can be exposed by a suspicious priest or a child who saw the wrong thing at the wrong hour. The game of concealment is messier, more immediate, and more personal.

## Vampire and D&D {#vtmbbb:introduction:vampireanddnd}

Games like _Vampire_, set within the World of Darkness, are built on the Storyteller system, a ruleset designed around collaborative narrative and moral complexity. Combat is certainly present, but it is not the engine. This system does what its name promises.

D&D is a different kind of game. It often utilizes maps, miniatures, structured encounters, and tactical combat as parts of its core pleasures. Adventures are built around objectives pursued through dangerous terrain, confrontations that play out in initiative order, and a party that solves problems by moving through space together. Adaptations of the World of Darkness to other systems are not unprecedented. GURPS produced _World of Darkness_ supplements in the 1990s, and the lesson those adaptations demonstrated holds here: when you have a great setting, different systems simply open different doors into it.

What the 5E format offers a Kindred party is tactical weight. The dungeon is sometimes literally a dungeon. The rival is something you have to fight through a burning building to reach. The wilderness has teeth and the map ends before the danger does.

Nosferatu moving through tunnels beneath a dying city, carrying information three factions will kill to possess. Gangrel tracking rivals through wilderness, far past the last torch on the road. Ventrue navigating a court where the mortal prince is someone else's pawn and the negotiation was designed to fail. A coterie arriving at a remote village where something has been feeding, and strong indication that the locals have already decided it must be them.

**_Welcome to the Dark Ages._**
