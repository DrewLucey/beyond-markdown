# Character Options {#vtmbbb:character-options:characteroptions}

## Kindred Class {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredclass}

_A Vampiric Survivor Empowered by Blood_

KindredKindred are an ancient line of vampires who have existed since the first days of humanity. These vampires are innately social creatures who form their own socio-political sects and divisions, often based on clans and bloodlines. They gather for camaraderie and safety among the only beings who can understand the eternal hunger within.

Kindred draw their abilities, collectively known as vampiric Disciplines, from the supernatural power in their blood, which they more eloquently call vitae. The older and more powerful Kindred become, the more blood they can consume and concentrate within themselves, calling upon it to fuel their undead bodies and powers. These Disciplines allow them to do unbelievable things. They can possess intense strength, durability, or speed. They can transform into creatures of the night, or even mist. They can leash the wills of others. They can manipulate minds and perceptions. And they can reshape the darkness in which they are at home. Kindred can call upon truly terrifying powers, and they are all powered by vitae.

Kindred can develop in different ways, but as the centuries of their existence have proven, the clans and bloodlines carry specific aspects of their vampirism throughout the generations, from sire to childe. These manifest as each clan’s ancient bane, the cost to be paid beyond just the endless hunger for blood and the struggle to keep at bay the Beast inside. There are Kindred in every walk of life, it seems, hiding in plain sight, acting like mortals, and maintaining their eternal Masquerade.

### Becoming a Kindred … {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredclass:becomingakindred}

#### As a Level 1 Character {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredclass:becomingakindred:asalevel1character}

- Gain all the traits in the [Core Kindred Traits table](#corekindredtraits).
- Gain the Kindred’s level 1 features, which are listed in the [Kindred Features table](#kindredfeatures).

#### As a Multiclass Character {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredclass:becomingakindred:asamulticlasscharacter}

- Gain the following traits from the [Core Kindred Traits table](#corekindredtraits): Hit Point Die, proficiency in one skill of your choice from the Kindred’s skill list, and training with Light Armor and Breastplate.
- Gain the Kindred’s level 1 features, which are listed in the [Kindred Features table](#kindredfeatures).

> #### Kindred and Other Species {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredclass:becomingakindred:KindredandOtherSpecies}
>
> In the _Vampire: The Masquerade_ (and the collective _World of Darkness_) setting, the vast majority of Kindred are human. While there might be the occasional plot-based variation, all Kindred characters are made using the human species.
>
> In fifth edition games, however, since Kindred is a class, other species can be Kindred if GMs want to create vampiric options in their campaigns.

#### Level 1: Feed {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredclass:kindredclassfeatures:level1featurefeed}

You feed on the blood of other creatures. This is most often accomplished through your sharp fangs. As an action, you can feed on another creature within 5 feet of yourself if the target is willing, [**Charmed**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#charmedcondition) by you, or has the [**Incapacitated**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#incapacitatedcondition), [**Grappled**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#grappledcondition), [**Paralyzed**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#paralyzedcondition), [**Restrained**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#restrainedcondition), [**Stunned**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#stunnedcondition), or [**Unconscious**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#unconsciouscondition) condition. You can feed only once per turn.

When you feed, roll up to your maximum Feed Dice plus your Constitution modifier (minimum of 1). The creature takes Necrotic damage equal to the roll’s total. This damage only reduces the creature’s Hit Point maximum by an amount equal to the roll’s total. The number of Feed Dice increases as you gain Kindred levels, as shown in the Feed Dice column of the [Kindred Features table](#kindredfeatures). If you roll a 6 on a d6 on a Feed Die, you can regain 1 expended Blood Point. If a creature’s Hit Point maximum is reduced to 0, you can regain 1 expended Blood Point.

**_Feeding from the Willing._** If you feed on a willing creature or a creature [**Charmed**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#charmedcondition) by you, it can spend 1 Hit Point Die. It rolls the Hit Point Die and reduces its Hit Point maximum by the amount rolled, and you can regain 1 expended Blood Point. Feeding in this way doesn’t end the Charmed condition.

**_Hit Die Deficit._** If a creature has no remaining Hit Point Dice, the creature takes Necrotic damage equal to a roll of your Feed Dice plus your Constitution modifier, and the creature’s Hit Point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the roll’s total.

**_Maximum Successful Feedings._** A creature can provide a number of Blood Points based on its size. A Small creature provides 1 Blood Point, a Medium provides 3, a Large provides 5, a Huge provides 7, and a Gargantuan provides 10. A creature regains all lost Blood Points when it finishes a Long Rest.

**_Creature Susceptible to Feed._** You can feed on any creature with blood or other life-giving fluid. Aberrations, Beasts, Dragons, Fey, Giants, Monstrosities, and Humanoids are the most common targets. Constructs, Elementals, Oozes, and Plants usually don’t have blood. Work with your GM to determine the types of creatures you can feed on.

> #### The Blood Bond {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredclass:kindredclassfeatures:TheBloodBond}
>
> A mysterious power in the blood of Kindred causes creatures to become Blood Bound to that Kindred when:
>
> - They drink from the same Kindred at least three times within one year.
> - At least one Long Rest occurs between each of the three drinks.
>
> While Blood Bound to a Kindred, a creature is considered [**Charmed**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#charmedcondition) by that Kindred and has [**Advantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#advantage) on saving throws to avoid being Charmed or [**Frightened**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#frightenedcondition) by other creatures. This is in addition to the effects from the [**Kindred Vitae**](#ref:magic_item:11796006-kindred-vitae "https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/11796006-kindred-vitae") consumable (see “[New Equipment](#newequipment)”).
>
> A Blood Bond can be suppressed for 1 minute if the creature succeeds on a DC 25 Charisma saving throw. The bond is only permanently removed if the creature doesn’t drink from that Kindred again for a year, or if the Kindred in question is killed.

#### Level 1: The Beast {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredclass:kindredclassfeatures:level1featurethebeast}

You have a terrible Beast within that can only be sated with blood. As a Bonus Action, you can use this Beast’s predatory instincts to sharpen your senses and abilities. Until the start of your next turn, you have [**Advantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#advantage) on [**D20 Tests**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#d20test) you make.

You can use this feature a number of time equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Short or Long Rest.

**_Hunger._** If a [**D20 Test**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#d20test) benefiting from your Beast feature fails, you go into a bloodlusted, hunger-driven state. This bloodlusted state ends after 1 minute or after you have successfully used your [Feed](#level1featurefeed) feature, whichever comes first.

While bloodlusted, you regard the creature nearest to you that you can see or hear and that you can feed on as your prey. If there are multiple possible creatures, choose one at random. On each of your turns, you must move as close to the creature as possible and take the [**Attack**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#attackaction) action to make an [**Unarmed Strike**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#unarmedstrike) to grapple the target. If you’re unable to get close enough to the creature to grapple it, your turn ends after you’ve used up all your available movement. If the creature dies or can no longer be seen or heard by you, the next nearest creature that you can see or hear and that you can feed on becomes your new target. If the target is in range and has the [**Grappled**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#grappledcondition) condition at the start of your turn, you use your action to feed on it.

#### Level 1: Blood Potency {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredclass:kindredclassfeatures:level1featurebloodpotency}

You are fueled by blood, represented by Blood Points. Your Kindred level determines the number of points you have, as shown in the Blood Points column of the [Kindred Features table](#kindredfeatures). You can expend Blood Points in the following ways:

**_Heal Wounds._** As a Bonus Action, you can expend 1 Blood Point to regain Hit Points equal to 1d10 plus your Kindred level.

**_Hunger Sated._** If you fail a [**D20 Test**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#d20test) that is affected by your [Beast](#level1featurethebeast) feature, you can expend 1 Blood Point to ignore the effects of Hunger.

**_Vitae Powers._** There are [Kindred feats](#kindredfeats) and [subclass](#kindredsubclasses) features fueled by expending Blood Points.

#### Level 1: Kindred Biology {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredclass:kindredclassfeatures:level1featurekindredbiology}

You are a specific kind of vampire known as a Kindred, which brings physical benefits and drawbacks.

**_Awaken._** You don’t regain expended Blood Points when you finish a Short or Long Rest. To gain the benefits of a Long Rest, you must have at least 1 Blood Point in your pool or you only gain the benefits of a Short Rest.

**_Darkvision._** You have [**Darkvision**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#darkvision) with a range of 60 feet.

**_Sanguivore._** You can’t eat food or drink. If you consume more than a nibble or sip, you have the [**Poisoned**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#poisonedcondition) condition that ends once you expel what you consumed. While Poisoned in this way, you must succeed on a Constitution saving throw every 10 minutes to avoid having the [**Incapacitated**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#incapacitatedcondition) condition, which lasts for 1 minute as you vomit up what you’ve consumed. The initial DC of this saving throw is 10, and it increases by 5 with each successive save.

Instead, you must consume a number of Blood Points per day equal to the number of pounds of food a creature your size normally requires per day (usually, 1 per day for Small and Medium creatures). You can expend 1 Blood Point to automatically succeed on the DC 10 Constitution saving throw to avoid gaining 1 level of [**Exhaustion**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#exhaustioncondition) from [**Malnutrition**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#malnutritionhazard).

Expending Blood Points to activate your Kindred features or [Kindred Feats](#kindredfeats) does not count towards the Blood Points you need to expend for sustenance.

**_Sunlight Vulnerability._** You take 5 Radiant damage if you start your turn in sunlight.

**_Supernatural Spellcasting._** Charisma is your spellcasting ability for spells from your Kindred subclass and Kindred feats, your spells require no Verbal or Somatic components, and they require no Material components unless they are consumed by the spell or have a cost specified in it, taking damage can’t break your [**Concentration**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#concentration).

**_Torpor._** If your [**Exhaustion**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#exhaustioncondition) level is 6, you aren’t killed. Instead, you drop to 1 Hit Point, become [**Unconscious**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#unconsciouscondition), and can’t remove the condition until you have expended a number of Blood Points equal to your Proficiency Bonus over any length of time. Another creature can feed you while you are Unconscious to recover Blood Points but the details of how that occurs is left for you and your GM to determine.

**_Undead._** You have the Undead (Kindred) creature type in addition to your normal creature type. You don’t age.

**_Vampiric Resilience._** You automatically succeed on Death Saving Throws. Being reduced to 0 Hit Points by Fire or Radiant damage, or being beheaded kills you outright.

**_Vampiric Vulnerabilities._** You have Vulnerability to Fire and Radiant damage.

**_Wooden Stake Through the Heart._** When you are attacked with a wooden weapon that deals Piercing damage and you are reduced to 0 Hit Points or the attack roll is a [**Critical Hit**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#criticalhit), you are staked through the heart and have the [**Paralyzed**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#paralyzedcondition) condition until the weapon is removed.

#### Level 2: Kindred Feat {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredclass:kindredclassfeatures:level2featurekindredfeat}

You gain a [Kindred feat](#kindredfeats) of your choice for which you qualify. You gain this feature again at Kindred levels 7, 10, 13, and 17.

#### Level 3: Kindred Subclass {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredclass:kindredclassfeatures:level3featurekindredsubclass}

You gain a [Kindred subclass](#kindredsubclasses) of your choice. A subclass is a specialization that grants you features at certain Kindred levels. For the rest of your career, you gain each of your subclass’s features that are of your Kindred level or lower.

> #### Creating Kindred Characters in the World of Darkness {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredclass:kindredclassfeatures:CreatingKindredCharactersintheWorldofDarkness}
>
> In the _Vampire: The Masquerade_, Kindred are usually aware of their clan or bloodline soon after they are sired. In order to maintain game balance with other classes, levels 1 and 2 of the Kindred class are designed to represent the learning curve of becoming Kindred, a process that can take longer for some.
>
> In a campaign set wholly in the _World of Darkness_, it is suggested that Kindred characters be created at level 3. This allows them to choose their clan or bloodline from the beginning of the game, and this may allow them to create lengthier backstories that can bring their _Vampire: The Masquerade_ characters to life, proverbially speaking.

#### Level 4: Ability Score Improvement {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredclass:kindredclassfeatures:level4featureabilityscoreimprovement}

You gain the [Ability Score Improvement](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/feats#abilityscoreimprovement) feat or another feat of your choice for which you qualify. You gain this feature again at Kindred levels 8, 12, and 16.

#### Level 5: Bestial Fury {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredclass:kindredclassfeatures:level5featurebestialfury}

Once per turn when you hit a target with a Melee weapon or [**Unarmed Strike**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#unarmedstrike), you can expend a use of your [Beast](#level1featurethebeast) feature to roll the weapon’s damage dice twice and use either roll against the target. If this damage reduces it to 0 Hit Points, you can’t choose to leave the creature at 1 Hit Point as per the “[**Knocking Out a Creature**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#knockingoutacreature)” rule.

**_Bestial Flurry._** While affected by your Beast feature, you can attack twice instead of once whenever you take the [**Attack**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#attackaction) action on your turn.

#### Level 5: Improved Feed {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredclass:kindredclassfeatures:level5featureimprovedfeed}

You can use the [Feed](#level1featurefeed) feature as a Bonus Action. In addition, a creature [**Grappled**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#grappledcondition) by you has [**Disadvantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#disadvantage) on its checks to escape the grapple.

#### Level 10: Vampiric Will {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredclass:kindredclassfeatures:level10featurevampiricwill}

You have [**Advantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#advantage) on saving throws to avoid or end the [**Charmed**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#charmedcondition) condition, and against effects that turn undead.

#### Level 14: Efficient Feeding {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredclass:kindredclassfeatures:level14featureefficientfeeding}

Whenever you roll a die for your [Feed](#level1featurefeed) feature, you can reroll the die, and you must use the new roll.

#### Level 19: Epic Boon {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredclass:kindredclassfeatures:level19featureepicboon}

You gain an Epic Boon feat or another feat of your choice for which you qualify.

#### Level 20: Ancient Awakening {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredclass:kindredclassfeatures:level20featureancientawakening}

The essence of the original vampiric Antediluvians has awoken in you. Pick four Ability Scores. Increase each chosen Ability Score by 2, to a maximum of 25.

If a Kindred subclass feature allows for an Ability Score to be increased beyond 25, Ancient Awakening can increase that Ability Score up to the maximum noted in that other feature.

> Kindred or Cainite?
>
> In the Dark Ages setting, vampires do not yet call themselves Kindred. That term belongs to a later era, born from the political institutions that will eventually organize vampiric society across the modern nights. In the Dark Ages, vampires more commonly call themselves Cainites, tracing their lineage to Caine, the first of their kind.
>
> The Kindred class is designed to work across any era in which vampires have existed. In modern nights, “Cainite” carries specific weight as the preferred term of the Sabbat sect, and using it as a catch-all can create confusion. Kindred is used here as more neutral term for the class.
>
> If you are playing in a Dark Ages campaign, feel free to have your character use Cainite at the table. The rules work the same either way. What you are, regardless of the century, is something that drinks blood and does not die easily.

## Kindred Subclasses {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredsubclasses}

A Kindred subclass is a specialization that grants you features at certain Kindred levels, as specified in the subclass. These subclasses represent your vampiric clan or bloodline. This section includes six of the original thirteen: [Brujah](#brujah), [Gangrel](#gangrel), [Lasombra](#lasombra), [Nosferatu](#nosferatu), [Toreador](#toreador), and [Ventrue](#ventrue).

**Note:** Kindred subclasses give you a subclass-specific Bane feature. This is a thematic weakness attached to that specific clan or bloodline.

### Brujah {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredsubclasses:brujah}

_Stand Up, Stand Firm, and Fight for Something_

[![Clan Brujah Crest, a helmet with a flaming crown atop a shield bearing an hourglass image (Artist: Richard Thomas)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/vtmbbb/FIPjWHwuc1dxehRt/01-002.brujah-crest.png)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/vtmbbb/FIPjWHwuc1dxehRt/01-002.brujah-crest.png)

The Brujah are a clan of freethinkers and revolutionaries who thrive on their own liberty and fight against oppression and stagnation. They find a power in themselves that is reflected as strength, speed, and a force of personality that makes them formidable in the varied conflicts they often find themselves in.

#### Level 3: Brujah Bane {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredsubclasses:brujah:level3featurebrujahbane}

Your blood simmers with the rage of your vampiric ancestors. If you fail a [**D20 Test**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#d20test) that is affected by your [Beast](#level1featurethebeast) feature and choose to expend Blood Points to ignore the effects of Hunger, you must expend a number of Blood Points equal to your Proficiency Bonus to ignore the effects, instead of 1.

#### Level 3: Fighting Fury {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredsubclasses:brujah:level3featurefightingfury}

You can channel the rage simmering below the surface, funneling it into your physical strength and speed. You can expend 1 Blood Point to gain the following benefits for 1 minute.

**_Brawn._** You have [**Advantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#advantage) on the first attack roll you make with a Melee weapon or [**Unarmed Strike**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#unarmedstrike) on your turn.

**_Swiftness._** You can take the [**Dash**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#dashaction) or [**Dodge**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#dodgeaction) action as a Bonus Action.

**_Weapon Training._** You gain proficiency with Martial weapons, and your training with weapons allows you to use the mastery properties of two kinds of Simple or Martial Melee weapons of your choice.

#### Level 6: Fearsome {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredsubclasses:brujah:level6featurefearsome}

Your vampiric presence exudes menace from the rise of your inner Beast.

**_Dread Gaze._** You bare your fangs, your eyes flash with predatory hunger, and you let out a hiss that strikes terror in the hearts of others. You can expend 1 Blood Point to cast the [**Fear**](#ref:spell:2618872-fear "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618872-fear") spell.

**_Predatory Aura._** You have [**Advantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#advantage) on Charisma ([**Intimidation**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills)) checks.

#### Level 9: Fleetness {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredsubclasses:brujah:level9featurefleetness}

Your celerity grants you bursts of incredible speed.

**_Dizzying Speed._** You can expend 3 Blood Points to cast the [**Haste**](#ref:spell:2619141-haste "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619141-haste") spell on yourself. When you cast the spell in this way, when the spell ends, you aren’t [**Incapacitated**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#incapacitatedcondition) and your Speed isn’t reduced to 0.

**_Call the Wretched._** You can expend 2 Blood Points to cast the [**Blight**](#ref:spell:2618934-blight "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618934-blight") or [**Insect Plague**](#ref:spell:2619183-insect-plague "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619183-insect-plague") spell.

**_Distending Jaws._** You can roll one additional Feed Die when you [feed](#level1featurefeed). In addition, you can use your jaws to make [**Unarmed Strikes**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#unarmedstrike). When you hit with your Unarmed Strike and deal damage, you can deal Piercing damage equal to 2d6 plus your Strength modifier instead of the normal damage of an Unarmed Strike.

**_One with the Night._** Your [**Blindsight**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#blindsight) is increased to 90 feet, and you can see normally in [**Dim Light**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#dimlight) and [**Darkness**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#darkness)—both magical and nonmagical—within 120 feet of yourself.

**_Skin of the Chameleon._** You can expend 2 Blood Points to cast the [**Greater Invisibility**](#ref:spell:2618958-greater-invisibility "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618958-greater-invisibility") spell. In addition, you have [**Advantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#advantage) on Dexterity ([**Stealth**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills)) checks.

**_Stony Flesh._** In response to taking damage, you can use your Reaction to expend 1 Blood Point to gain Resistance for 1 minute to two damage types, other than Fire, Psychic, and Radiant, including against the triggering damage.

**_Wings of the Gargoyle._** While you aren’t wearing Heavy armor, your Speed increases by 10 feet, and you gain a Fly Speed equal to your Speed.

#### Level 18: What Goes Bump in the Night {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredsubclasses:nosferatu:level18featurewhatgoesbumpinthenight}

You have fully come to embrace the nature of your existence and thrive as a creature of the night. You gain the following benefits.

**_Lord of Vermin._** As a [**Magic**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#magicaction) action, you can expend 3 Blood Points to summon 1d4 + 1 [**Swarms of Bats**](#ref:monster:5195222-swarm-of-bats "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195222-swarm-of-bats"), [**Swarms of Insects**](#ref:monster:5195224-swarm-of-insects "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195224-swarm-of-insects"), or [**Swarms of Rats**](#ref:monster:5195228-swarm-of-rats "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195228-swarm-of-rats"). These swarms arrive at the end of your next turn and are under your control for 1 hour. The swarms are an ally to you and your allies. In combat, the swarms share your Initiative count, but they take their turn immediately after yours. The swarms obey your verbal commands (no action required by you). If you don’t issue any, they take the [**Dodge**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#dodgeaction) action and use their movement to avoid danger.

Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

**_Stalk the Shadows._** You can cast [**Greater Invisibility**](#ref:spell:2618958-greater-invisibility "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618958-greater-invisibility") on yourself without expending a spell slot a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Short or Long Rest.

### Toreador {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredsubclasses:toreador}

_See the World Like No One Else Can_

#### Level 6: Depth of Feelings {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredsubclasses:toreador:level6featuredepthoffeelings}

The following are example questions.

- Is the creature’s creature type X? (Where X is a creature type specified by the player character, such as Monstrosity or Undead)

- Is the creature [**Hostile**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#hostileattitude) toward me?
- Does the creature believe what it is saying?

The GM has final say as to whether the question can be answered, but the answers must be truthful.

**_Entrancement._** You can expend 1 Blood Point to cast the [**Calm Emotions**](#ref:spell:2618959-calm-emotions "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618959-calm-emotions"), [**Charm Monster**](#ref:spell:2618963-charm-monster "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618963-charm-monster"), or [**Charm Person**](#ref:spell:2618964-charm-person "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618964-charm-person") spell.

#### Level 9: Live Fast, Be a Good Looking Corpse {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredsubclasses:toreador:level9featurelivefastbeagoodlookingcorpse}

You experience the world, in all its wonders, with vampiric speed. You gain the following benefits.

**_Ability Score Increase._** Your Dexterity score increases by 2, to a maximum of 25.

**_Rapidity._** As a Bonus Action, you can expend 1 Blood Point to gain an additional action on your turns. That action can be used to take only the [**Attack**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#attackaction) (one attack only), [**Dash**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#dashaction), [**Disengage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#disengageaction), [**Hide**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#hideaction), or [**Utilize**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#utilizeaction) action. This effects lasts for a number of turns equal to your Proficiency Bonus.

#### Level 11: Visionary {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredsubclasses:toreador:level11featurevisionary}

You have honed your skills and your perceptions to a razor’s edge. You gain the following benefits.

**_Practiced Perfection._** You gain [**Expertise**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#expertise) in three of your skill proficiencies of your choice.

**_Spirit’s Touch._** As a [**Magic**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#magicaction) action, you can expend 2 Blood Points to touch an object and perceive emotional residues on it for 10 minutes. You can ask the GM a number of questions equal to your Proficiency Bonus, which must be answered truthfully. You must ask your questions before the effect ends. The following are examples of questions that can be asked.

- What did the last creature that touched this look like?
- What was the emotional state of the last creature that used the object?
- Is this object magical?
- Is this object cursed?
- Is this object trapped?
- What are any physical details of the object that aren’t readily visible?
- How many creatures have touched the object in the last 24 hours?

#### Level 15: Truly Majestic {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredsubclasses:toreador:level15featuretrulymajestic}

You exude vampiric allure and have learned how to charm others. You gain the following benefits.

**_Eerily Convincing._** You have [**Advantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#advantage) on Charisma ([**Deception**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills) and [**Persuasion**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills)) checks. #### Level 18: Magnum Opus {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredsubclasses:toreador:level18featuremagnumopus}

You have mastered what it means to be one of the greatest vampiric artisans, which defines you as much as you define it. Choose two of the following benefits.

**_Flicker._** As a Bonus Action, you can expend 3 Blood Points to dance between the beats of a mortal’s heart. Until the start of your next turn, you have a +3 bonus to AC, your Speed is doubled, your movement doesn’t provoke [**Opportunity Attacks**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#opportunityattack), and you have [**Advantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#advantage) on attack rolls.

**_Open Mind._** You can use a [**Magic**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#magicaction) action to expend 1 Blood point to choose any number of creatures you know within 5 miles of yourself or any number of creatures you can see within 120 feet of yourself. You and the chosen creatures can communicate telepathically with each other while the creatures and you are within 5 miles of each other for 8 hours. You and the targets can instantly share words, images, sounds, and other sensory messages with each other through the link, and the targets recognize you as the creature they are communicating with. This effect enables creatures to understand the meaning of your words and any sensory messages you send to them.

**_Star Magnetism._** You can expend 3 Blood Points to cast the [**Mass Suggestion**](#ref:spell:2619029-mass-suggestion "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619029-mass-suggestion") spell. When you cast the spell in this way, you can target any creature you can communicate with, you don’t need line of sight, the range becomes 1 mile, and you can modify it so that it doesn’t require [**Concentration**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#concentration).

### Ventrue {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredsubclasses:ventrue}

_Control Others with Influence and Guile_

[![Clan Ventrue Crest. Lions holding axes and a quartered shield bearing images of lions, fleur-de-lis, axes, and maces, atop which sits a crown and a crowned lion holding a mace. Color Illustration. The Ventrue have elevated themselves to be the “Clan of Kings,” imposing their will on the clans and standing contemptuously against those who would defy them. After lifetimes of prudent rule, the Ventrue have earned their figurative throne.

#### Level 3: Voice of Authority {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredsubclasses:ventrue:level3featurevoiceofauthority}

You can impose control over others while keeping others from imposing control over you. You gain the following benefits.

**_Do as I Say._** You can cast the [**Command**](#ref:spell:2618985-command "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618985-command") and [**Suggestion**](#ref:spell:2619101-suggestion "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619101-suggestion") spells. You can cast either spell without expending a spell slot a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Short or Long Rest.

**_Unshakably Confident._** You have [**Advantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#advantage) on Wisdom saving throws.

#### Level 3: Ventrue Bane {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredsubclasses:ventrue:level3featureventruebane}

You have an arguably refined palate for blood. Choose a specific physical aspect (such as natural blondes or the elderly) or a social aspect (such as employees, soldiers, or the willing). It cannot be a wide generalization, such as creature type. When you use your [Feed](#level1featurefeed) feature on a creature that doesn’t have your preferred aspect, you roll half the number of Feed Dice.

#### Level 6: Dare Not Falter {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredsubclasses:ventrue:level6featuredarenotfalter}

You effortlessly shrug off harm to your body and mind. You gain the following benefits.

**_Toughened._** Your Hit Point maximum increases by an amount equal to your Kindred level when you gain this feature. Whenever you gain a Kindred level thereafter, your Hit Point maximum increases by an additional 1 Hit Point.

**_Unswayable Mind._** If you fail a saving throw to avoid or end the [**Charmed**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#charmedcondition), [**Frightened**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#frightenedcondition), or [**Stunned**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#stunnedcondition) condition, you can reroll it. You must use the new roll.

#### Level 9: Domineering Control {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredsubclasses:ventrue:level9featuredomineeringcontrol}

With a glance, you can steer the emotions and thoughts of intelligent creatures.

**_Befuddle._** You can expend 1 Blood Point to cast the [**Hypnotic Pattern**](#ref:spell:2619168-hypnotic-pattern "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619168-hypnotic-pattern") spell. When you cast the spell in this way, a creature with an Intelligence of 3 or lower automatically succeeds on its saving throw against the spell while a creature with an Intelligence of 4 or higher has [**Disadvantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#disadvantage) on the saving throw.

**_Entrance._** As a [**Magic**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#magicaction) action, make a Charisma ([**Persuasion**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills)) check and expend 2 Blood Points to choose a creature you can see within 30 feet of you. The target must make a Wisdom saving throw with a DC equal to the result of your Persuasion check. On a failed save, the creature has the [**Stunned**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#stunnedcondition) condition until the end of your next turn. At the end of each of the Stunned creature’s turns, the creature repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. A creature with an Intelligence of 3 or lower automatically succeeds on its saving throw against the effect.

**_Persuade._** You can expend 1 Blood Point to cast the [**Charm Monster**](#ref:spell:2618963-charm-monster "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618963-charm-monster") spell.

**_Terrify._** As a [**Magic**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#magicaction) action, make a Charisma ([**Intimidation**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills)) check and expend 1 Blood Point to choose a creature you can see within 30 feet of you. The target must make a Wisdom saving throw with a DC equal to the result of your Intimidation check. On a failed save, the creature has the [**Frightened**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#frightenedcondition) condition for 1 minute. At the end of each of the Frightened creature’s turns, the creature repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. A creature with an Intelligence of 3 or lower automatically succeeds on its saving throw against the effect.

#### Level 11: Mass Manipulation {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredsubclasses:ventrue:level11featuremassmanipulation}

Your ability to command and sway others grows to encompass multiple minds at once. You can expend 3 Blood Points to cast the [**Mass Suggestion**](#ref:spell:2619029-mass-suggestion "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619029-mass-suggestion") spell. At level 15, you can expend 4 Blood Points to cast the spell as its level 7 version; at level 18, you can expend 5 Blood Points to cast it as its level 8 version; and at level 20, you can expend 6 Blood Points to cast it as its level 9 version.

#### Level 15: Sanguine Fortification {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredsubclasses:ventrue:level15featuresanguinefortification}

Your vitae has resilience-enhancing properties. You gain the following benefits.

**_Draught of Endurance._** When a creature within 60 feet of you drinks your vitae, you can take a Reaction and expend 2 Blood Points to grant that creature a number of [**Temporary Hit Points**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#temporaryhitpoints) equal to twice your Kindred level.

**_Flesh of Marble._** When you take damage except Fire and Radiant, you can take a Reaction and expend 2 Blood Points to reduce the damage you take by half (round down) or 4 Blood Points to reduce the damage to 0.

#### Level 18: Lordly Power {#vtmbbb:character-options:kindredsubclasses:ventrue:level18featurelordlypower}

Your will over others becomes ironclad and staggeringly powerful. You gain the following benefits.

**_Imposing Aura._** When a creature within 15 feet of you targets you with an attack roll or a damaging spell, it must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw (DC 8 plus your Strength modifier and Proficiency Bonus) or either choose a new target or lose the attack or spell. On a successful save, the target isn’t affected, and it’s immune to Imposing Aura for 24 hours.

**_Penetrating Dominance._** Creatures you can see within 60 feet of yourself have [**Disadvantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#disadvantage) on saving throws to avoid or end the [**Charmed**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#charmedcondition) condition.

**_Summon._** As a [**Magic**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#magicaction) action, you can expend 3 Blood Points to urge a Beast, Humanoid, or Undead you have touched and is on the same plane of existence as you to make a Wisdom saving throw (DC 8 plus your Charisma modifier and Proficiency Bonus). On a failed save, the creature is compelled to come to you. The creature must use as much of its movement as possible to move in your direction on its turns, taking the safest route, and stopping only to eat and rest. A compelled creature repeats the save at the end of each day at dusk, ending the effect on itself on a success. You know if the compulsion ends early. You can’t have more than one creature under this effect at a time. If you use this feature again, the effect on another creature ends.

When the creature appears, it is under no compulsion to behave a particular way. You can ask it to perform a service in exchange for payment, but it isn’t obliged to do so. The requested task could range from simple (fly us across the chasm, or help us fight a battle) to complex (spy on our enemies, or protect us during our foray into the dungeon). You must be able to communicate with the creature to bargain for its services. Payment can take a variety of forms.

The GM can adjust these payments based on the circumstances under which you called upon it. If the task is aligned with the creature’s ethos, the payment might be halved or even waived. Nonhazardous tasks typically require only half the payment, while especially dangerous tasks might require a greater gift. Creatures rarely accept tasks that seem suicidal.

A [**Remove Curse**](#ref:spell:2618943-remove-curse "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618943-remove-curse"), [**Greater Restoration**](#ref:spell:2618961-greater-restoration "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618961-greater-restoration"), or [**Wish**](#ref:spell:2619213-wish "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619213-wish") spell ends this effect.

## Feats {#vtmbbb:character-options:feats}

The following feats are designed for the [Kindred class](#kindredclass) or classes adventuring in the World of Darkness.

### Origin Feats {#vtmbbb:character-options:feats:originfeats}

These feats are in the Origin category.

#### Healthy {#vtmbbb:character-options:feats:originfeats:healthy}

_Origin Feat_

You have, for whatever reason, maintained a physically healthy lifestyle. You gain the following benefits.

**_Hit Die Use._** The first time you would spend a Hit Point Die after completing a Short or Long Rest, you gain the benefit of spending the Hit Die but do not spend it.

**_Saving Throw Proficiency._** Choose one ability in which you lack saving throw proficiency. You gain saving throw proficiency with the chosen ability.

#### Nocturnal {#vtmbbb:character-options:feats:originfeats:nocturnal}

_Origin Feat_

You have spent many formative years in the darkness of night. You gain the following benefits.

**_Darkvision._** You have [**Darkvision**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#darkvision) with a range of 60 feet. If you already have Darkvision, its range increases by 30 feet.

**_Slink in Shadows._** Whenever you make a Dexterity ([**Stealth**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills)) check while in [**Dim Light**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#dimlight) or [**Darkness**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#darkness), you can treat a roll of 9 or lower on the d20 as a 10.

#### Protected {#vtmbbb:character-options:feats:originfeats:protected}

_Origin Feat_

You have someone or something guarding you for as long as you can remember, even if you don’t know why. You gain the following benefits.

**_Luck Points._** You have a number of Luck Points equal to your Proficiency Bonus and can spend the points on the benefits below. You regain your expended Luck Points when you finish a Long Rest.

**_Reroll._** Whenever you make a [**D20 Test**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#d20test) and roll 9 or lower on the d20, you can spend 1 Luck Point to reroll the d20. You must accept the second result.

**_Stabilize._** When you are reduced to 0 Hit Points and not killed outright, you can spend 1 Luck Point to drop to 1 Hit Point instead.

#### Thin-Blooded {#vtmbbb:character-options:feats:originfeats:thinblooded}

_Origin Feat (Can Be Chosen in Place of Any Origin Feat)_

You have a portion of a vampire’s blood flowing in your veins. You are a thin-blood, often destined for a place among the clanless. You gain the following benefits.

**_Able to Feed._** You feed on the blood of other creatures. This is most often accomplished through your sharp fangs. As an action, you can feed on another creature within 5 feet of yourself if the target is willing, [**Charmed**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#charmedcondition) by you, or has the [**Incapacitated**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#incapacitatedcondition), [**Grappled**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#grappledcondition), [**Paralyzed**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#paralyzedcondition), [**Restrained**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#restrainedcondition), [**Stunned**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#stunnedcondition), or [**Unconscious**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#unconsciouscondition) condition. You can feed only once per turn.

You have 2d6 Feed Dice. When you feed, roll up to your maximum Feed Dice plus your Constitution modifier (minimum of 1). The creature takes Necrotic damage equal to the roll’s total. This damage only reduces the creature’s Hit Point maximum by an amount equal to the roll’s total. If you roll a 6 on a d6 on a Feed Die, you can regain 1 expended Blood Point. If a creature’s Hit Point maximum is reduced to 0, you can regain 1 expended Blood Point.

**_Feeding from the Willing._** If you feed on a willing creature or a creature [**Charmed**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#charmedcondition) by you, it can spend 1 Hit Point Die. It rolls the Hit Point Die and reduces its Hit Point maximum by the amount rolled, and you can regain 1 expended Blood Point. Feeding in this way doesn’t end the Charmed condition.

**_Hit Die Deficit._** If a creature has no remaining Hit Point Dice, the creature takes Necrotic damage equal to a roll of your Feed Dice plus your Constitution modifier, and the creature’s Hit Point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the roll’s total.

**_Maximum Successful Feedings._** A creature can provide a number of Blood Points based on its size. A Small creature provides 1 Blood Point, a Medium provides 3, a Large provides 5, a Huge provides 7, and a Gargantuan provides 10. A creature regains all lost Blood Points when it finishes a Long Rest.

**_Creature Susceptible to Feed._** You can feed on any creature with blood or other life-giving fluid. Work with your GM to determine the types of creatures you can feed on.

**_Kindred, but Not Kindred._** You ignore the Kindred class prerequisite for choosing [Kindred feats](#kindredfeats). You can’t gain levels in the [Kindred class](#kindredclass).

**_Kindred Vitae._** You have a number of Blood Points equal to your Proficiency Bonus minus one.

**_Supernatural Spellcasting._** Charisma is your spellcasting ability for spells from your Kindred feats. Your spells require no Verbal or Somatic components, and they require no Material components unless they are consumed by the spell or have a cost specified in it. Taking damage can’t break your [**Concentration**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#concentration) for spells cast using this feature.

**_Vampiric Vulnerabilities._** You have Vulnerability to Fire and Radiant damage.

**_Wooden Stake Through the Heart._** When you are attacked with a wooden weapon that deals Piercing damage and you are reduced to 0 Hit Points or the attack roll is a [**Critical Hit**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#criticalhit), you are staked through the heart and have the [**Paralyzed**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#paralyzedcondition) condition until the weapon is removed.

#### Well-Read {#vtmbbb:character-options:feats:originfeats:wellread}

_Origin Feat_

You grew up with your nose buried in books. You gain the following benefits.

**_Creature Lore._** When you roll Initiative, you can recall lore about a creature you can see within 30 feet of yourself; you know one Immunity, Resistance, Vulnerability, or Trait of that creature. You decide when you use this ability.

**_Font of Useful Knowledge._** When you roll a 1 on an Intelligence ([**Arcana**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills) or [**History**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills)) check, you can reroll the die, and you must use the new roll.

**_Rare Knowledge._** If you succeed on an Intelligence ([**Arcana**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills) or [**History**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills)) check, you learn additional lore. The GM has final say on what you learn, if anything. The lore might consist of important details, amusing revelations, or even secret lore that has never been widely known. The more information you already know about the thing, the more precise and detailed the information you receive is. That information is accurate but might be couched in figurative language or poetry, as determined by the GM.

#### Lethal Body {#vtmbbb:character-options:feats:kindredfeats:lethalbody}

_Kindred Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Kindred Class)_

You possess potent vampiric strength, turning your bare hands into deadly weapons.

**_Crushing Blows._** You can deal 1d8 extra damage when you hit a target with an [**Unarmed Strike**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#unarmedstrike). The extra damage is the same type dealt by that strike.

**_Iron Grip._** A creature has [**Disadvantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#disadvantage) on ability checks and saving throws to escape your grapple.

#### Loyal Servant {#vtmbbb:character-options:feats:kindredfeats:loyalservant}

_Kindred Feat (Prerequisite: Level 7+, Kindred Class)_

You have a loyal companion who has gained power from your blood. You gain the following benefits.

**_Companion._** A loyal Beast or Humanoid is [Blood Bound](#thebloodbond) to you. Choose a Beast or Humanoid stat block that has a maximum Challenge Rating of up to half your Proficiency Bonus. When choosing a companion, you may look in the monster section of source books or elsewhere for an eligible Beast or Humanoid if the Game Master permits you to do so.

**_Power in the Blood._** Your Loyal Servant has one [Kindred feat](#kindredfeats) of your choice, and it counts as a level 2 Kindred for the purpose of selecting a Kindred feat. In addition, your Loyal Servant can spend 1 Hit Point Die in place of 1 Blood Point for the purpose of its feat.

**_Replacing a Servant._** If your servant is killed or otherwise leaves, you can choose a new Loyal Servant after 30 days. The GM can lengthen or shorten this time based on in-game circumstances.

#### Mind Tricks {#vtmbbb:character-options:feats:kindredfeats:mindtricks}

_Kindred Feat (Prerequisite: Level 2+, Kindred Class)_

You can dominate others with your mental powers. You gain the following benefits.

**_Cloud Memory._** You can cast the [**Command**](#ref:spell:2618985-command "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618985-command") spell without expending a spell slot a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest. In addition, you gain access to a special command.

**_Forget._** The target has only a hazy and unsure recollection of the last 5 minutes.

#### Self-Control {#vtmbbb:character-options:feats:kindredfeats:selfcontrol}

_Kindred Feat (Prerequisite: Level 2+, Kindred Class)_

You manage your Beast better than many. You gain the following benefits.

**_Ability Score Increase._** Your Wisdom score increases by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**_Reining the Beast._** When you would be affected by the Hunger of your [Beast](#level1featurethebeast) feature, you can take a Reaction to immediately end the effect.

You can use this benefit a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once). You regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

#### Superior Discipline Acquisition {#vtmbbb:character-options:feats:kindredfeats:superiordisciplineacquisition}

_Kindred Feat (Prerequisite: Level 12+, Greater Discipline Acquisition Feat)_

The vampiric Discipline of another clan or bloodline is now second nature to you. Gain one of the following benefits for which you already possess the matching Discipline from the [Greater Discipline Acquisition](#greaterdisciplineacquisition) feat.

**Animalism.** You can expend 1 Blood Point to cast the [**Charm Monster**](#ref:spell:2618963-charm-monster "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618963-charm-monster") or [**Dominate Beast**](#ref:spell:2619146-dominate-beast "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619146-dominate-beast") spell. When you cast the spell in this way, the target has [**Disadvantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#disadvantage) on the saving throw against the spell.

**Auspex.** You can cast [**Detect Magic**](#ref:spell:2619097-detect-magic "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619097-detect-magic") but only as a ritual. You can cast [**Clairvoyance**](#ref:spell:2618972-clairvoyance "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618972-clairvoyance") and [**Telepathy**](#ref:spell:2619159-telepathy "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619159-telepathy"). You can cast each of these spells without expending a spell slot. Once you cast either spell in this way, you can’t cast that spell in this way again until you finish a Long Rest.

**Celerity.** As a Bonus Action, you can expend 1 Blood Point to gain an additional action. That action can be used to take only the [**Attack**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#attackaction) (one attack only), [**Dash**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#dashaction), [**Disengage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#disengageaction), [**Dodge**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#dodgeaction), [**Hide**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#hideaction), or [**Utilize**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#utilizeaction) action.

**Dominate.** You can expend 3 Blood Points to cast the [**Mass Suggestion**](#ref:spell:2619029-mass-suggestion "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619029-mass-suggestion") spell. When you cast the spell in this way, creatures have [**Disadvantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#disadvantage) on the saving throw against the spell.

**Fortitude.** When you take damage except Fire and Radiant, you can take a Reaction to reduce the damage taken by your Proficiency Bonus plus your Constitution modifier (minimum reduction of 1).

**Obfuscate.** You can cast the [**Invisibility**](#ref:spell:2619116-invisibility "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619116-invisibility") spell without expending a spell slot a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest. In addition, when you gain the [**Invisible**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#invisiblecondition) condition, you can expend up to 3 Blood Points to grant up to three other creatures that you can see within 30 feet of yourself the Invisible condition for 1 minute. The number of Blood Points expended in this way equals the number of creatures affected by this feat.

**Oblivion.** You can expend 2 Blood Points to cast the [**Black Tentacles**](#ref:spell:2619187-evards-black-tentacles "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619187-evards-black-tentacles") spell. When you cast the spell in this way, the Black Tentacles deal 1d6 Bludgeoning damage and 2d6 Cold damage (instead of 3d6 Bludgeoning damage).

**Potence.** As a Bonus Action, you can expend up to 6 Blood Points to increase your Strength score by an amount equal to the number of Blood Points expended in this way for 1 hour.

**Presence.** You can cast the [**Suggestion**](#ref:spell:2619101-suggestion "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619101-suggestion") spell without expending a spell slot.

**Protean.** You can expend 1 Blood Point to cast the [**Meld into Stone**](#ref:spell:2619031-meld-into-stone "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619031-meld-into-stone") spell on yourself. When you cast the spell in this way, the object or surface must be dirt, sand, or clay, and the spell’s duration becomes 24 hours for that casting.

**_Repeatable._** You can take this feat more than once, but you must choose a different Discipline each time.

#### Supreme Discipline Acquisition {#vtmbbb:character-options:feats:kindredfeats:supremedisciplineacquisition}

_Kindred Feat (Prerequisite: Level 16+, Superior Discipline Acquisition Feat)_

You have learned all you can about a vampiric Discipline of another clan or bloodline. Gain one of the following benefits for which you already possess the matching Discipline from the [Superior Discipline Acquisition](#superiordisciplineacquisition) feat.

**Animalism.** You cast the [**Dominate Beast**](#ref:spell:2619146-dominate-beast "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619146-dominate-beast") spell as its level 7 version without expending a spell slot, and you can modify it so that it doesn’t require [**Concentration**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#concentration). Once you cast the spell with this feat, you can’t do so in this way again until you finish a Long Rest.

**Auspex.** You can expend 2 Blood Points to cast the [**True Seeing**](#ref:spell:2619201-true-seeing "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619201-true-seeing") spell on yourself.

**Celerity.** You can expend 2 Blood Points to cast the [**Haste**](#ref:spell:2619141-haste "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619141-haste") spell on yourself. When you cast the spell in this way, when the spell ends, you aren’t [**Incapacitated**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#incapacitatedcondition) and your Speed isn’t reduced to 0.

**Dominate.** You cast the [**Dominate Person**](#ref:spell:2619148-dominate-person "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619148-dominate-person") spell as its level 7 version without expending a spell slot, and you can modify it so that it doesn’t require [**Concentration**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#concentration). Once you cast the spell with this feat, you can’t do so in this way again until you finish a Long Rest.

**Fortitude.** When you take damage except Fire and Radiant, you can take a Reaction and expend 2 Blood Points to reduce the damage you take by half (round down) or 4 Blood Points to reduce the damage to 0.

**Obfuscate.** You can expend 1 Blood Point to cast the [**Greater Invisibility**](#ref:spell:2618958-greater-invisibility "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618958-greater-invisibility") spell. When you cast the spell in this way, you can modify it so that it doesn’t require [**Concentration**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#concentration). In addition, you have [**Advantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#advantage) on Dexterity ([**Stealth**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills)) checks.

**Oblivion.** When a creature that you can see within 10 feet of you misses an attack roll, you can take a Reaction and expend 1 Blood Point to deal 2d8 Bludgeoning damage and 2d8 Cold damage to that creature.

**Potence.** As a Bonus Action, you can expend 2 Blood Points to give yourself [**Advantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#advantage) on attack rolls using Strength for 1 minute. In addition, whenever you have Advantage on an attack roll using Strength, you can reroll one of the dice once.

**Presence.** You can expend 3 Blood Points to cast the [**Sanctuary**](#ref:spell:2619003-sanctuary "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619003-sanctuary") spell on yourself. When you cast the spell in this way, the spell doesn’t end if you make an attack roll, cast a spell, or deal damage.

**Protean.** You can expend 3 Blood Points to cast [**Polymorph**](#ref:spell:2618876-polymorph "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618876-polymorph") on yourself, [**shape-shifting**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#shapeshifting) into a [**Bat**](#ref:monster:4775803-bat "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4775803-bat"), [**Cat**](#ref:monster:4775808-cat "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4775808-cat"), [**Rat**](#ref:monster:4775836-rat "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4775836-rat"), [**Raven**](#ref:monster:4775837-raven "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4775837-raven"), or [**Wolf**](#ref:monster:4775850-wolf "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4775850-wolf"). You can expend 1 additional Blood Point to shape-shift into a [**Dire Wolf**](#ref:monster:4775812-dire-wolf "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4775812-dire-wolf"), [**Giant Bat**](#ref:monster:5195012-giant-bat "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195012-giant-bat"), [**Giant Rat**](#ref:monster:5195025-giant-rat "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195025-giant-rat"), or [**Panther**](#ref:monster:4775832-panther "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4775832-panther"). While in that form, you retain your Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma scores.

**_Repeatable._** You can take this feat more than once, but you must choose a different Discipline each time.

#### Touch of Darkness {#vtmbbb:character-options:feats:kindredfeats:touchofdarkness}

**_Shadowplay._** You have [**Advantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#advantage) on Dexterity ([**Stealth**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills)) checks.

**_Wooden Melee Weapons._** When you make an attack roll with this Melee weapon and roll a 1 on the d20, the weapon splinters and is destroyed.

**_Wooden Ammunition._** Wooden ammunition is destroyed after hitting or missing a target.

#### Leather Undervest [(10 GP)](#ref:equipment:881-leather-undervest "https://www.dndbeyond.com/equipment/881-leather-undervest") {#vtmbbb:character-options:newequipment:equipment:881-leather-undervest}

These layered leather patches are worn under clothing or armor. The vest fits over the upper chest and back, protecting several internal organs from puncture, particularly the heart. While wearing a leather undervest, you gain the following benefits.

**_Underarmor._** The leather undervest can be worn with any type of armor.

**_Limited Flexibility._** The undervest allows a max Dexterity modifier of 3.

**_Pierce Protection._** When you’re hit by an attack while you’re wearing a leather undervest, any Piercing damage dealt to you by that attack is reduced by 2. In addition, Intelligence ([**Medicine**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills)) checks to stake you in the heart have [**Disadvantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#disadvantage).

### Magic Items {#vtmbbb:character-options:newequipment:magicitems}

#### [Kindred Vitae](#ref:magic_item:11796006-kindred-vitae "https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/11796006-kindred-vitae") {#vtmbbb:character-options:newequipment:magicitems:11796006-kindred-vitae}

_Potion, (Uncommon)_

Vitae, blood from within a Kindred, has powerful properties that are passed on when imbibed by a non-Kindred. A single Blood Point can be stored in a drinkable vessel for up to 48 hours without alchemical treatment. When you drink this potion, you gain the following effects.

**_Increased Strength and Speed:_** Your Strength score increases by 2, and your Speed increases by 5 feet for 1 week.

**_Increased Vitality:_** You gain 2d6 [**Temporary Hit Points**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#temporaryhitpoints).

**_Reduced Aging:_** You physically ignore the next year of natural aging.

However, mortals who drink this potion are more susceptible to the powers of the Undead. For the period of 1 year, you have [**Disadvantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#disadvantage) on saving throws against being [**Charmed**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#charmedcondition) or [**Frightened**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#frightenedcondition) by Undead.
