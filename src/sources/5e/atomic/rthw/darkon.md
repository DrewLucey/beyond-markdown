# Darkon {#rthw:darkon:darkon}

Domain on the Brink of Destruction

> #### **Darklord:** None {#rthw:darkon:DarklordNone}

>

Darkon is a sprawling realm of somber gothic cities, vast wildernesses, hidden secrets, and the dead. The ruins of forgotten magical empires scar the land. Undead creatures haunt the domain’s lonely reaches, and the living acknowledge that the land’s morbid magic might one day reanimate their corpses.

Yet, for all its mysteries, the domain of Darkon has failed. Across the realm, the Mists close in, inexorably overwhelming the land. These Mists turned ravenous are known as the Shroud, and what they consume doesn’t reemerge.

Darkon’s dissolution ties to the disappearance of its Darklord, [**Azalin Rex**](#ref:monster:6491340-azalin-rex "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491340-azalin-rex"). During a disastrous event known as the Hour of Ascension, Azalin’s citadel, Castle Avernus, was destroyed, and a mysterious golden star called the King’s Tear appeared in the heavens. Since then, no one has seen Azalin, and the Shroud has eroded the domain.

Despite the encroaching annihilation, life in Darkon hasn’t changed. The residents of towns and cities far from the domain’s borders dismiss reports of vanishing frontiers as fearmongering. As Darkon splits into crumbling islands, ambitious individuals vie for Azalin’s power, each touting that they alone can save the land by becoming its tyrant.

## Domain Features {#rthw:darkon:domainfeatures}

Darkon is known for the following features:

**Divided Domain.** The Mists consuming Darkon have divided the land into four separate regions.

**Hour of Ascension.** [Azalin Rex](#azalinrex) vanished during an event his servants call the Hour of Ascension.

**Inevitable Undeath.** The night after any Humanoid dies, its corpse reanimates and shambles into the night. Locals swiftly burn bodies to prevent this.

**King’s Tear.** Since the Hour of Ascension, a golden star called the King’s Tear hangs in the sky. The sun and moon pass behind this star in their orbits.

**Secret Police.** The Kargat, the domain’s secret police, remains active in Darkon’s largest cities: Il Aluk and Martira Bay.

## Locations in Darkon {#rthw:darkon:locationsindarkon}

Darkon is a place of dark wonders and apathy. The ruins of impossible structures litter the landscape, but the domain’s jaded people ignore these marvels to focus on daily concerns.

The regions of Darkon are described below, and other notable locations are summarized on the [Darkon Locations table](#darkonlocations).

### Regions of Darkon {#rthw:darkon:locationsindarkon:regionsofdarkon}

The Shroud has divided Darkon into four parts (see “[Fractures of Darkon](#fracturesofdarkon)” for details):

**Jagged Coast.** Dense forests and shattered cliffs compose the [Jagged Coast](#jaggedcoast), skirting the Sea of Souls. Undead within the forests prey on isolated communities here.

**Lychgate.** [Lychgate](#lychgate) is ruled by the dead. Aimless undead creatures wander the forest, milling around amid the time-locked ruins of Castle Avernus and many of Azalin’s powerful servants.

**Mistlands.** Scattered communities dot the [Mistlands](#mistlands), which were once Darkon’s wild eastern reaches. Strange monsters emerge from the forests and mountains to prey on the isolated lands.

**Rexcrown.** The heart of Darkon, [Rexcrown](#rexcrown) has become a sagging bog. From it rise the spires and grim wonders of Il Aluk, Darkon’s capital, and the homes of the domain’s indifferent elite.

## Azalin Rex {#rthw:darkon:azalinrex}

Darklord Who Won’t Be Caged

[![(Artist: Guilherme Motta)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/05-003.azalin-rex.jpg)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/05-003.azalin-rex.jpg)

> #### “It is customary to kneel before your dread lord.” {#rthw:darkon:azalinrex:Itiscustomarytokneelbeforeyourdreadlord}
>
> —Azalin Rex

In Darkon, Azalin was a tyrannical wizard-king obeyed by the living and the dead. When he first appeared before his people, he adopted an illusion of the form he possessed in life: that of a middle-aged human man with dark hair and a superior demeanor. However, his true form was that of a moldering lich, eyes ablaze with blasphemous energy.

Since arriving in the Domains of Dread, Azalin has been unable to learn new magic. For a wizard who forsook his mortality to pursue arcane studies, this was the ultimate frustration. Despite the limits on his studies, Azalin numbered among the few Darklords who understood the mysterious nature of the Domains of Dread. For generations, Azalin struggled to escape Darkon. His magical atrocities reshaped Darkon and the Domains of Dread time and again. However, these struggles gave Azalin only a glimpse of freedom, and his metaphysical violations inevitably reverted to the Dark Powers’ original designs. Nevertheless, Azalin refused to stay a prisoner. His most recent plot culminated in the Hour of Ascension and the destruction of [Castle Avernus](#castleavernus). Since then, no one in Darkon has seen the Darklord, and his realm has begun deteriorating.

The stat block in this section presents the Darklord as he was prior to his disappearance and how he would be should he return. Azalin’s current spirit jar is a golden amulet he carries. If this amulet is destroyed, Azalin’s original spirit jar—an immovable dragon skull in the ruins of Castle Avernus—reactivates, and the lich’s spirit returns to Darkon if his body is destroyed.

### Azalin’s Tale {#rthw:darkon:azalinrex:azalinstale}

Firan Zal’honan was a noble and heir to the city-state of Knurl on the world of Oerth. A student of magic, Firan adopted the title “Azal’Lan,” meaning “wizard-king,” when he came to power. Firan’s rule was marked by harsh laws and violent expansion, but his people flourished, as their lives were filled with magical wonders.

As Firan aged, he looked toward his succession. His son, Irik, was generous and charming but, in Firan’s eyes, soft and unfit to rule. No matter how Firan tried to teach his son of the world’s harsh realities, Irik’s kind spirit wouldn’t bend to his father’s cynical outlook. This difference came to a head when Firan caught Irik freeing political prisoners. Firan could have ignored the crime or pardoned his son. Instead, Firan personally wielded the axe during Irik’s execution.

In the years that followed, Firan was tormented by his son’s death, but he also became convinced that only he was fit to rule. Researching the mysteries of death, he discovered the secret to becoming a lich. After embracing the transition into undeath, Firan ruled for decades more, increasing Knurl’s power and earning himself many foes. Eventually, tales of magic that might restore Irik lured Firan from his home. During his search, a band of mercenaries ambushed the ruler. Firan fled into the mist to avoid the assassins. When the mist cleared, Firan found himself in [Barovia](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/barovia).

In Barovia, peasants Firan encountered mistook his title and called him Azalin. Soon, Azalin encountered Barovia’s ruler, [**Strahd von Zarovich**](#ref:monster:6491390-strahd-von-zarovich "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491390-strahd-von-zarovich"). The two came to respect each other, and Azalin taught Strahd much of the way of magic. Together, the pair learned a great deal about the nature of the Domains of Dread and made attempts to escape. Their plots failed, though, and resulted in other realms—such as the domain of [Mordent](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/mordent)—being drawn into Barovia’s orbit. Eventually, the two tyrants’ respect soured into loathing. Before Azalin and Strahd could destroy each other, Azalin entered the Mists, and the domain of Darkon spread before him.

### Azalin’s Domain {#rthw:darkon:azalinrex:azalinsdomain}

Darkon is altered by Azalin’s presence, creating the following effects:

**Closing the Borders.** While in his domain, Azalin can close or open the borders to Darkon at will. While the borders are closed, undead terrors fill the Mists surrounding Darkon. Whenever a creature enters the Mists, one [**Hostile**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#hostileattitude) [**Zombie Clot**](#ref:monster:6491405-zombie-clot "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491405-zombie-clot") (see [chapter 5](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/denizens-of-the-mists#zombieclot)) appears in a random unoccupied space within 30 feet of that creature.

**Lord of the Dead.** While in his domain, Azalin can cast [**Clairvoyance**](#ref:spell:2618972-clairvoyance "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618972-clairvoyance"), requiring no spell components and using the same spellcasting ability as his Spellcasting action. When Azalin casts this spell in this way, he can place the sensor in any Undead in Darkon with an Intelligence of 6 or less, even if it is beyond 1 mile of Azalin. In addition, Azalin can speak through the Undead he placed the sensor in.

**No Secrets.** Whenever a creature in Darkon casts a spell using a level 5+ spell slot, Azalin becomes aware of that creature’s name and location. A [**Nondetection**](#ref:spell:2619122-nondetection "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619122-nondetection") spell blocks this effect.

If Azalin dies, these effects end immediately.

### Azalin’s Lair {#rthw:darkon:azalinrex:azalinslair}

During the Hour of Ascension, Castle Avernus—Azalin’s seat of rule and magical laboratory—erupted in a magical conflagration. Now, Castle Avernus stands frozen in time at the instant of its destruction, blasted to magically floating chunks of rubble. Its towers pitch wildly and crumble to pieces amid arcane flames, yet they don’t fall. Although time has halted in the ruins, Azalin’s servants who avoided the disaster have returned, unaffected by the castle’s frozen time. Meanwhile, the fortress’s magic attempts to heal the wreckage, slowly creating new halls and winding stairs between the fractured floors.

#### Castle Avernus {#rthw:darkon:azalinrex:azalinslair:castleavernus}

Amid its magical destruction, Castle Avernus is a vast structure filled with Azalin’s arcane creations and summoned servants. Prominent locations from this ruin are depicted on [map 2.7](#map207castleavernus) and detailed here:

**Outer Bailey and Gallery.** The castle’s entry, called the Maw by Azalin’s servants, remains partially intact. Undead from the surrounding wilds lurk here, waiting for their lord’s commands.

**Hall of Records.** A column of stationary but still deadly magical fire blasts through Avernus’s library. Scattered on ruined islands of rubble are the remains of a collection said to hold the biographies of all who have died in Darkon.

**Tomb of Irik Zal’honan.** Here stands the tomb of Azalin’s son, Irik. The [**Ghost**](#ref:monster:5195008-ghost "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195008-ghost") of Irik appears to those with benevolent intentions. Despite his tragic fate, he forgives his father—but he doesn’t want Azalin to harm anyone else. He can’t leave the crypt and knows little of Azalin’s plots.

**Lair of Ebbasheyth.** The [**Shadow Dragon**](#ref:monster:5195201-shadow-dragon-huge "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195201-shadow-dragon-huge") Ebbasheyth served as Azalin’s adviser and steed. While she has moved much of her hoard to Mount Nyid, she still watches over the treasures here.

**Hall of Life.** The Hall of Life has largely crumbled and is accessible only via gaps in the ceiling of the floor below. Within survives a circle of glass and emerald cylinders. These hold the remains of Azalin’s magical cloning experiments. A gigantic dragon skull made of gold has fallen into the midst of these failed experiments. This is Azalin’s original spirit jar, but the skull’s eyes, which once glowed with unholy flames, now look empty.

**Heart of Avernus.** Amid the tower’s wreckage glows the magical anomaly called the Heart of Avernus. This is the epicenter of the disaster that destroyed the castle during the Hour of Ascension. This mysterious force draws droves of the undead to feed on the ongoing magical reaction.

## Fractures of Darkon {#rthw:darkon:fracturesofdarkon}

The domain of Darkon has fractured into four regions: the Jagged Coast, [Lychgate](#lychgate), the [Mistlands](#mistlands), and [Rexcrown](#rexcrown). In Azalin’s absence, ambitious villains unleash terrors as they strive to dominate their piece of the domain. This section explores the horrors unfolding in each region of Darkon.

### Jagged Coast {#rthw:darkon:fracturesofdarkon:jaggedcoast}

Stark vistas of sheer cliffs, mysterious islands, and knotted forests bleed from one to the next along the Jagged Coast. The shadow of Castle Avernus once loomed over the southern part of the region, enforcing grim peace on the small communities there. In the north, the thriving city of Martira Bay prides itself on its inhabitants’ wealth and reason.

Since Azalin’s disappearance, the Darklord’s secret police have maintained order here—obeying not the commands of the missing king, but rather the will of their regional leader, Alcio Metus.

[![The Treacherous Harbor of Martira Bay (Artist: Titus Lunter)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/05-004.martira-bay.png)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/05-004.martira-bay.png)

#### Kargat {#rthw:darkon:fracturesofdarkon:jaggedcoast:kargat}

Azalin’s secret police, the Kargat, has long enforced the Darklord’s edicts and smothered any ember of rebellion in Darkon. Those “disappeared” by the Kargat are never seen again.

While all Darkon fears these dark-cloaked spies, few realize the Kargat is largely composed of vampires and other shape-shifting creatures. Members operate in cells assigned to particular tasks or locations, with each reporting to a regional leader. The highest-ranking members reported to Azalin. Serving the Kargat are the Kargatane, a cultlike rank of living informants and initiates who spy on their neighbors in hopes of earning supernatural power and induction into the larger organization.

The Hour of Ascension threw the Kargat into disorder. Taking advantage of this opportunity, Alcio Metus (Medium, Neutral Evil [**Vampire**](#ref:monster:5195251-vampire "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195251-vampire")), a Kargat leader and criminal kingpin in Martira Bay, slew her peers and took over the western branch of the group. From the Black Tower in Martira Bay, Alcio spreads her Kargat spies across region and beyond.

#### Martira Bay {#rthw:darkon:fracturesofdarkon:jaggedcoast:martirabay}

A foggy port on the Sea of Souls, Martira Bay is a cosmopolitan hub of trade and ideas. The populace prides itself on its worldliness and believes that the best of Darkon and lands beyond passes through the city. Martira Bay’s people appreciate reason and tangibility, and many have never left the city. This makes many residents incredulous of magic and the supernatural, even as séances and spiritualist practices flourish in posh salons.

Martira Bay is infamous for sensationalist murders. Newspapers such as _The Bullseye_ update their devoted readership on the grisly details of sprees committed by killers such as the Midnight Slasher, the Spider, and the Weeping Woman. The overwhelmed Martira Bay constabulary works to counter these killers, as do professional detective agencies including the Ray Detectives (founded by Alanik Ray; see [chapter 3](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/ravenloft-adventures#alanikray)) and the Dusklight Detectives.

### Lychgate {#rthw:darkon:fracturesofdarkon:lychgate}

Lychgate hides the magically shattered remains of Castle Avernus. Whatever ongoing magical disaster befell the castle during the Hour of Ascension has locked its ruins in time, freezing it in a semiexploded state. While Castle Avernus remains the lair of some of Azalin’s most dangerous servants, the surrounding wilderness has become overrun by shuffling corpses and mournful spirits drawn to the castle’s strange magic. The region is ruled by Ebbasheyth, Azalin’s [**Shadow Dragon**](#ref:monster:5195201-shadow-dragon-huge "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195201-shadow-dragon-huge") ally who maintains a lair in Castle Avernus.

Adventures in Lychgate likely relate to the mystery of Azalin’s disappearance. Roll on or choose a result from the Doom in Lychgate table to inspire adventures in this ruined realm.

### Mistlands {#rthw:darkon:fracturesofdarkon:mistlands}

Even before the Hour of Ascension, western Darkon was a land of magical mysteries and strange creatures. No longer fearing the Darklord’s magic, ancient evils have crept from the shadows to terrorize and control the scattered communities.

In the town of Nevuchar Springs, an elf mystic and leader of the Eternal Order, Cardinna Artazas, works to turn back the Shroud. To do this, she sought to resurrect a being bonded to the land and capable of bending the Mists to its will. This resulted in the corruption of her order’s sacred pools and unleashed Darcalus Rex, a [**Necrichor**](#ref:monster:6491378-necrichor "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491378-necrichor") (see [chapter 5](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/denizens-of-the-mists#necrichor)) that claims to be the first king of Darkon. Uncertain of what it has unleashed but desperate for salvation, the Eternal Order serves the whims of Darcalus Rex as the strange being grows in power.

#### Rexcrown {#rthw:darkon:fracturesofdarkon:mistlands:rexcrown}

Residents of Rexcrown consider themselves the only true Darkonians due to their proximity to the capital, Il Aluk. Despite that prestige, Rexcrown is covered in deadly swamps, depleted mountains, and fields that produce disturbingly malformed crops.

The city of Il Aluk is built in a bend in the Vuchar River and rises in countless gothic spires. These mark the homes of the city’s elite, severe governmental buildings, and temples dedicated to dozens of grim deities. Serving the city are the vassal villages of Decimus, Desolatus, and Despondia, where peasants struggle to feed the city’s people and the decadent lifestyles of local nobles.

As the Shroud spreads fear among traders, Il Aluk suffers from shortages in food and other supplies. The city’s residents have grown desperate and resentful of the exploitative upper class. This has resulted in violence and the ransacking of wealthy homes. The city’s elite have rallied behind one of their eldest members, Lady Talisveri Eris.

Eris is a vain human who uses elaborate fashions and makeup to disguise the fact that her body is permanently imperceptible. Once each moon, she incites her fearful peers to go out at night and commit violence while using her high-priced [**Potions of Greater Invisibility**](#ref:magic_item:9228934-potion-of-greater-invisibility "https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/9228934-potion-of-greater-invisibility"). These magical attacks and Eris’s pompous, detached rhetoric exacerbate tensions in the city while enriching her nearly bankrupt family.

## Adventures in Darkon {#rthw:darkon:adventuresindarkon}

Adventures set in Darkon’s disconnected regions often involve the mystery of Azalin’s disappearance, the domain’s dissolution, or the metaphysical nature of the Domains of Dread. Use this section to develop horror adventures in this domain.

### Horror in Darkon {#rthw:darkon:adventuresindarkon:horrorindarkon}

Beyond the adventures you might develop in Darkon’s disconnected regions, the following elements persist across the domain.

#### Azalin’s Fate {#rthw:darkon:adventuresindarkon:horrorindarkon:azalinsfate}

The events of the Hour of Ascension and the fate of Azalin loom over adventures in Darkon. The details of these mysteries are yours to decide. You can take inspiration from the following possibilities when determining Azalin’s fate.

**_Severed Darklord._** During the Hour of Ascension, Azalin’s body was destroyed as he tore open portals to the Negative and Positive Energy Planes. The former portal lies at the heart of Castle Avernus, while the latter is known as the King’s Tear. The opposed planar energies are disrupting Darkon. Once the domain is destroyed, Azalin theorizes, his spirit jar will be released into the Shadowfell.

**_Shattered Darklord._** The Hour of Ascension was Azalin’s failed attempt to become one of the Dark Powers. His essence is now trapped within his spirit jar, scattered pieces of his iron crown, and the floating amber sarcophagus known as the King’s Tear. Whoever frees Azalin’s essence will become the new Darklord.

**_Unmoored Darklord._** During the Hour of Ascension, Azalin split his soul, leaving a piece in Darkon while the greater part escaped through a metaphysical rent between domains—the King’s Tear. Now, disguised as the scholar Firan Zal’honan, Azalin searches for secrets of the Dark Powers in other domains. He avoids entering the Mists without Mist-wandering guides, fearing the Dark Powers will find him otherwise.

#### The Dead Serve {#rthw:darkon:adventuresindarkon:horrorindarkon:thedeadserve}

In Darkon, Humanoid corpses reanimate as [**Zombies**](#ref:monster:4775851-zombie "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4775851-zombie") 24 hours after death. These corpses obey Azalin. While he remains missing, they roam in a winding path toward Castle Avernus. These zombies avoid other creatures, but they fight anything that blocks their path.

#### The Shroud {#rthw:darkon:adventuresindarkon:horrorindarkon:theshroud}

During the day, one can traverse the Mists surrounding Darkon as normal, allowing travel between domains or regions of Darkon. At night, the Mists surrounding the domain turn deadly and encroach on the land—sometimes by infinitesimal degrees, other times in unstoppable floods. A creature that touches the Shroud takes 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage and is [**Frightened**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#frightenedcondition) until the end of its next turn. If a creature is slain by the Shroud or enters it entirely, the creature vanishes. What becomes of it is a mystery.

### Darkon Campaign Arc {#rthw:darkon:adventuresindarkon:darkoncampaignarc}

Campaigns set in Darkon likely involve the mystery of the Hour of Ascension. A campaign here might follow this outline:

**Levels 1–3.** The characters arrive in Darkon near Il Aluk, Martira Bay, or Nevuchar Springs. They soon make an ally among those who oppose the villains rising to replace Azalin: Alcio Metus, Darcalus Rex, or Talisveri Eris. The characters help defend their allies and learn of these villains’ methods.

**Levels 4–15.** The characters confront the region’s villain. Rather than fighting the characters, the villain entreats the group to help them establish control over the domain, which they believe will reunite Darkon’s regions and end the threat of the Shroud. To do so, they must defeat the domain’s other villains or recover the scattered remnants of Azalin’s power. The characters work to save Darkon while undermining their wicked patron.

**Levels 16–18.** The characters’ adventures take them to Castle Avernus, where they must either claim a remnant of Azalin’s might or learn his fate. In doing so, they can restore Azalin and bring his wrath down on those who would claim his throne.

### Secrets of Avernus {#rthw:darkon:adventuresindarkon:secretsofavernus}

Adventure for Level 16 Characters

**Situation.** The characters must learn the secret of Azalin’s disappearance.

**Hook.** The threat of the Shroud and those who would claim Azalin’s throne has grown too great to ignore. An ally directs the characters to the only place they can find answers regarding Azalin’s disappearance: Castle Avernus.

**_Into the Maw._** Droves of animate corpses fill the outer bailey. A [**Spirit Naga**](#ref:monster:5195215-spirit-naga "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195215-spirit-naga") named Lossa lurks amid the rubble. If she spots the characters, she impersonates Azalin and commands the corpses to fight. They rise as three [**Zombie Clots**](#ref:monster:6491405-zombie-clot "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491405-zombie-clot") (see [chapter 5](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/denizens-of-the-mists#zombieclot)).

**_The Archive._** Elzarath, an [**Indifferent**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#indifferentattitude) [**Ghost**](#ref:monster:5195008-ghost "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195008-ghost") with a floating severed head, laments the state of the archives. Elzarath shares that they once held the biographies of all who died in Darkon, but now the books are scattered. If asked about Azalin’s biography, the ghost searches and finds nothing. Elzarath doesn’t know Azalin’s actual name, but if specifically asked about the biography of Firan Zal’honan, the ghost finds the book. This biography relates the information in the “[Azalin’s Tale](#azalinstale)” section and key details of the Hour of Ascension.

**_Crypt of Irik Zal’honan._** The [**Ghost**](#ref:monster:5195008-ghost "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195008-ghost") Irik Zal’honan appears to any Good-aligned creature or those who call out to him. Irik can’t leave this area, but he aids those who seek to restore his father. He knows Azalin’s true name is Firan Zal’honan and shares that with anyone who destroys the corrupted experiment in the Hall of Life in the ruins above. Irik reveals that he is Azalin’s son only if asked directly.

**_Hall of Life._** This room holds a dragon skull made of gold and a circle of glass and emerald cylinders. The dragon skull remains magically anchored so only Azalin can move it. The cylinders are cracked and filled with bubbling decay. If someone smashes all the cylinders, the cylinders fuse into a Hostile abomination with the same stat block as a [**Lesser Star Spawn Emissary**](#ref:monster:6491369-lesser-star-spawn-emissary "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491369-lesser-star-spawn-emissary") (see [chapter 5](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/denizens-of-the-mists#lesserstarspawnemissary)).

**_Ebbasheyth’s Lair._** Ebbasheyth the [**Shadow Dragon**](#ref:monster:5195201-shadow-dragon-huge "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195201-shadow-dragon-huge") is in her lair, attended by a Kargat messenger (Medium [**Vampire Nightbringer**](#ref:monster:5195248-vampire-nightbringer "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195248-vampire-nightbringer")). The monsters are [**Hostile**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#hostileattitude), but any character who takes an [**Influence**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#influenceaction) action and succeeds on a DC 16 Charisma ([**Persuasion**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills)) check can make them [**Indifferent**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#indifferentattitude). Characters who mention their desire to restore Azalin have [**Advantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#advantage) on this check. If made Indifferent, Ebbasheyth tells the characters the archives might hold details of Azalin’s schemes. If asked what name to search for, she doesn’t know but relates that the spirit in the room below might.

**_Conclusion._** The biography of Firan Zal’honan in the archive reveals the secret of Azalin’s disappearance. From this, the characters should know how to find or restore Azalin or how to recover his power. Each character also earns a bonus 9,800 XP.
