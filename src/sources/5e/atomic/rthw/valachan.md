In the wild domain of Valachan, only the deadliest predators survive. Bestial hunters, carnivorous plants, and ravenous monsters stalk the dense rainforests, but the land’s greatest threat is its Darklord: the devious, undying hunter [**Chakuna**](#ref:monster:6491347-chakuna "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491347-chakuna").

Chakuna stalks the jungle, testing herself against the domain’s deadliest beasts. Yet little delights her more than discovering strangers to her realm—visitors who soon realize they’ve become the Darklord’s newest quarry. Chakuna forces her victims to participate in the Trial of Hearts, a race through the wilderness to remote sanctuaries. Participants face deadly traps and all the dangers of the wilds, including Chakuna herself. Those who reach the goal at the end of the trial are permitted to leave Valachan. Those who don’t reach it die, either due to the deadly wilderness or at the hands of Chakuna herself.

## Domain Features {#rthw:valachan:domainfeatures}

Valachan is known for the following features:

**Displacer Beast Packs.** Packs of [**Displacer Beasts**](#ref:monster:5194970-displacer-beast "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194970-displacer-beast") roam the jungle, led by Yana, a preternaturally large and cunning displacer beast that serves Chakuna.

**Natural Hunters.** Wild plants and creatures in Valachan usually seem hostile toward strangers. Most creatures here are carnivorous even if elsewhere they aren’t.

**Trial of Hearts.** The deadly [Trial of Hearts](#trialofheartsrules) is a battle royale that starts on the night of the full moon. Chakuna chooses participants.

**Wilderness Domain.** Valachan is covered by a fantastically dangerous rainforest. Visitors face constant perils, but the domain’s residents flourish.

## Locations in Valachan {#rthw:valachan:locationsinvalachan}

Valachan is lush and wild. Rare creatures and plants reign here, as do villages of people who understand and thrive in the rainforest. No roads pass through the land, but ships from other domains sometimes wreck on these shores. The settlement of Oselo and the Pantara Lodge are described below, and other notable locations are summarized on the [Valachan Locations table](#valachanlocations).

### Oselo {#rthw:valachan:locationsinvalachan:oselo}

The former home of Darklord Chakuna, Oselo is a prosperous village that welcomes visitors. When night falls, the residents change, shifting from people into panthers, ocelots, tigers, and other hunting cats. These shape-shifters hunt animals for food and do battle with other peoples only to defend their home. Most fear Chakuna and want no involvement with the Trial of Hearts, fearing the Darklord’s wrath. However, in rare instances, Oselo’s shape-shifters might be convinced to guide, hide, or otherwise aid participants in the Trial. This displeases Chakuna, but she respects the decisions of her people’s elders. The villagers might adopt strangers deemed worthy, but outsiders first must prove themselves in an initiation ceremony focused on surviving unarmed in the forest for a week.

### Pantara Lodge {#rthw:valachan:locationsinvalachan:pantaralodge}

The Pantara Lodge is a series of thatched structures strung together high in the forest canopy, suspended above the overgrown ruins of a burned castle. This is Chakuna’s home. Its interconnected treehouses include a great hall, the Darklord’s personal quarters, an armory, an infirmary, and several guest lodges. The Darklord’s [**Displacer Beast**](#ref:monster:5194970-displacer-beast "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194970-displacer-beast") and [**Panther**](#ref:monster:4775832-panther "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4775832-panther") allies prowl the trees amid the lodge’s structures and can enter them freely.

## Chakuna {#rthw:valachan:chakuna}

Darklord Who Hunts to Survive

[![(Artist: Karina Refrynn (WeirdUndead))](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/18-003.chakuna.jpg)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/18-003.chakuna.jpg)

> #### “You seem capable, but do you have the heart of a hunter?” {#rthw:valachan:chakuna:Youseemcapablebutdoyouhavetheheartofahunter}
>
> —Chakuna

Chakuna is Valachan’s deadliest hunter, a youthful werepanther with the intensity and calm of a veteran executioner. Few can elude her cold cunning, deadly traps, and packs of feline allies.

Chakuna is no mere murderer. She hunts because she must to protect the people of Oselo from a more terrible fate. To that end, she prefers to target strangers in her land, forcing them to participate in the Trial of Hearts. She relishes the chance to hunt the proud and disrespectful, as well as the skilled and unpredictable. Chakuna knows no mercy, though, and even those she comes to respect eventually face her blade. She spares the people of Oselo alone, even as her peoples’ fear of her grows.

#### Traits {#rthw:valachan:chakuna:6491347-chakuna:Traits}

**_Darklord Restoration._** If Chakuna dies and her heart remains intact, she revives with all her Hit Points in 1d10 weeks somewhere in Valachan. Chakuna’s heart is a Tiny object that has AC 17, HP 10, and Immunity to all damage except Fire.

**_Experienced Hunter._** [**Difficult Terrain**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#difficultterrain) composed of undergrowth doesn’t cost Chakuna extra movement.

**_Legendary Resistance (3/Day, or 4/Day in Domain)._** If Chakuna fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.

#### Actions {#rthw:valachan:chakuna:6491347-chakuna:Actions}

**_Multiattack._** Chakuna makes three attacks, using Scratch or Barbed Spear in any combination. She can replace one attack with a Bite attack.

**_Bite (Panther or Hybrid Form Only)._** _Melee Attack Roll:_ +8, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 20 (3d10 + 4) Piercing damage. If the target is a Humanoid, it is subjected to the following effect. _Constitution Saving Throw:_ DC 15. _Failure:_ The target is [**cursed**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#curses). If the cursed target drops to 0 Hit Points, it instead becomes a werepanther under the DM’s control and has 10 Hit Points (use the [**Weretiger**](#ref:monster:5195266-weretiger "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195266-weretiger") stat block). _Success:_ The target is immune to Chakuna’s curse for 24 hours.

**_Scratch._** _Melee Attack Roll:_ +8, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 13 (2d8 + 4) Slashing damage.

**_Barbed Spear (Humanoid or Hybrid Form Only)._** _Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:_ +8, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft. _Hit:_ 8 (1d8 + 4) Piercing damage and the target receives a dire wound if it doesn’t have one. While the target is wounded, its speed is reduced by 10 feet, and it loses 5 (1d10) Hit Points at the start of each of its turns. The wound closes after 1 minute, after a spell restores Hit Points to the target, or after the target or a creature within 5 feet of it takes an action to staunch the wound, doing so by succeeding on a DC 15 Wisdom ([**Medicine**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills)) check. _Hit or Miss:_ The spear magically returns to Chakuna’s hand immediately after a ranged attack.

#### Bonus Actions {#rthw:valachan:chakuna:6491347-chakuna:BonusActions}

**_Prowl (Panther or Hybrid Form Only)._** Chakuna moves up to her Speed without provoking [**Opportunity Attacks**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#opportunityattack). At the end of this movement, she can take the [**Hide**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#hideaction) action.

**_Shape-Shift._** Chakuna [**shape-shifts**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#shapeshifting) into a Large panther-humanoid hybrid or a Large panther, or she returns to her true humanoid form. Her game statistics, other than her size, are the same in each form. Any equipment she is wearing or carrying isn’t transformed.

#### Legendary Actions {#rthw:valachan:chakuna:6491347-chakuna:LegendaryActions}

Legendary Action Uses: 3 (4 in Domain). Immediately after another creature’s turn, Chakuna can expend a use to take one of the following actions. Chakuna regains all expended uses at the start of each of her turns.

**_Pounce._** Chakuna moves up to half her Speed and makes one Scratch attack.

**_Strike from Shadows._** Chakuna makes one Barbed Spear attack. If Chakuna has the [**Hide**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#hideaction) action’s [**Invisible**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#invisiblecondition) condition, this attack doesn’t end that condition. Chakuna can’t take this action again until the start of her next turn.

**_Sudden Bite._** Chakuna uses Shape-Shift to turn into her hybrid form or panther form and then makes one Bite attack. Chakuna can’t take this action again until the start of her next turn.

### Chakuna’s Tale {#rthw:valachan:chakuna:chakunastale}

For untold generations, Darklord Urik von Kharkov ruled Valachan from Castle Pantara and hunted the people of Valachan for sport. Yearly, the Darklord’s hunts grew in carnage, with his favorite prey being the shape-shifters of Oselo. Von Kharkov viewed himself as the domain’s greatest hunter, a title he confirmed every time he slaughtered one of Oselo’s supernatural warriors.

Chakuna was a werepanther warrior of Oselo and, like most of her people, was a skillful hunter. Seeing that von Kharkov eventually would slay her entire village, she swore to save Oselo. Realizing it would take a monster to slay a monster, she left her people and sought the deepest secrets of her deadly homeland. When she returned, she volunteered to let von Kharkov hunt her. During the murderous game that followed, Chakuna burned Castle Pantara and took the former Darklord’s head.

Valachan is no mundane land, though. A Domain of Dread demands a Darklord, and Valachan will be ruled only by its greatest hunter. Refusing to let von Kharkov return, Chakuna made a pact with the domain itself. Within the depths of the mazelike Yaguara Mountains, Chakuna ripped her own heart from her chest and consumed the heart of Urik von Kharkov. Doing so, she stole von Kharkov’s powers. Yet to retain them, she must feed the land constantly with the blood of the hunted. If she doesn’t, the plants and animals of Valachan grow murderous and howl for von Kharkov’s return. So Chakuna continues a cycle of bloodshed, embracing the violence of her peoples’ onetime oppressor to ensure her bitter domination of her vicious realm.

### Chakuna’s Domain {#rthw:valachan:chakuna:chakunasdomain}

Valachan is altered by Chakuna’s presence, creating the following effects.

**Closing the Borders.** While in her domain, Chakuna can close or open the borders to Valachan at will. While the borders are closed, shadowy beasts skulk or soar amid the Mists surrounding Valachan. A creature that enters the Mists for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there takes 1d10 Piercing damage.

**Heightened Senses.** Beasts and Monstrosities have [**Advantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#advantage) on Wisdom checks.

**Whispering Shadows.** Humanoids subtract 1d8 from any ability checks they make when they take the [**Hide**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#hideaction) action.

If Chakuna dies, these effects end immediately.

### Chakuna’s Lair {#rthw:valachan:chakuna:chakunaslair}

Built above the overgrown ruins of von Kharkov’s Castle Pantara, the Pantara Lodge is a maze of rope bridges, mighty boughs, and structures built among the branches of gigantic trees. Chakuna leaves those who die in the [Trial of Hearts](#trialofheartsrules) without demonstrating skill or honor dangling from the branches of her home.

#### Pantara Lodge {#rthw:valachan:chakuna:chakunaslair:pantaralodge}

The Pantara Lodge rises 80 feet above the rainforest floor. The site is depicted on [map 2.35](#map235pantaralodge) and has the following features:

**Chakuna’s Chambers.** In her quarters, Chakuna studies lore from other realms, fruitlessly seeking ways to placate her domain without further bloodshed. Secret doors hidden here lead to her meditation chamber and a room where the undying head of Urik von Kharkov spits curses and plots his return.

**Great Hall and Banquet Room.** Chakuna welcomes guests in the lodge’s great hall. Prior to the Trial of Hearts every moon, she hosts a lavish meal for her prey.

**Trophy Gallery.** The trophy gallery holds the skulls of deadly beasts, the weapons of those who failed to murder Chakuna, and the stuffed corpses of the Darklord’s rivals—animals and people alike.

## Adventures in Valachan {#rthw:valachan:adventuresinvalachan}

Adventures set in Valachan involve merciless wilderness, predatory animals, and deadly hunts. Use this section to develop horror adventures in this domain.

### Horror in Valachan {#rthw:valachan:adventuresinvalachan:horrorinvalachan}

Adventures in Valachan often include the following elements.

#### Trial of Hearts Rules {#rthw:valachan:adventuresinvalachan:horrorinvalachan:trialofheartsrules}

Visitors to Valachan likely are forced to participate in the Trial of Hearts, a dangerous game where the domain’s Darklord hunts them. On the night of the full moon, [**Chakuna**](#ref:monster:6491347-chakuna "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491347-chakuna") selects those she considers worthy to participate. At the Pantara Lodge, she shows her guests every courtesy, provides them with a map of the domain, and sets out these rules for the trial:

- Participants can divide into small groups or go it alone.
- Once groups are established at the trial’s start, alliances between contenders aren’t permitted.
- Participants must reach one of two shrines: either on Kiru Island or between the lakes called the Scars atop Anquara Plateau.
- Participants can leave the Pantara Lodge at dawn. The following dusk, Chakuna pursues the contenders with hunting partners of her choice, attempting to slay any participants she encounters outside a shrine.
- Only one person or group can occupy a shrine.
- Participants can kill one another for any reason—particularly to take a shrine.
- The trial doesn’t stop for any reason until Chakuna arrives at both shrines and acknowledges any winners there.
- The Darklord’s servants escort winners to the village of Shuaran, and from there, out of Valachan.
- Losers rot where they fall.
- Rule breakers are killed.

Chakuna wants the trial to be challenging. If she catches a competitor too soon, she might release them if they sacrifice an ally, a limb, or a valuable weapon. Anyone who dies in the trial feeds the domain’s bloodlust—even those who fall to a threat other than the Darklord herself.

#### Valachan Exploration {#rthw:valachan:adventuresinvalachan:horrorinvalachan:valachanexploration}

Valachan is a dense and deadly wilderness. When creating adventures here, refer to [chapter 2](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/running-the-game#runningexploration) of the [Dungeon Master’s Guide](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024) for details on travel and journeys with stages.

Characters use the rules in the “[Travel Pace](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/playing-the-game#travelpace)” section of the [Player’s Handbook](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024). Valachan’s forests prevent a party from traveling at faster than a Normal pace, and their density makes them [**Difficult Terrain**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#difficultterrain), reducing travel pace by half. This means most characters can move through the domain at a Normal pace of 1½ miles per hour and 12 miles per day.

**_Flying._** Difficult Terrain slows flying creatures in Valachan, due to the strong winds above the forest canopy. Flying creatures are also easy to spot, attracting the attention of [**Chimeras**](#ref:monster:5194943-chimera "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194943-chimera"), [**Pteranodons**](#ref:monster:5195173-pteranodon "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195173-pteranodon"), and condor-like [**Rocs**](#ref:monster:5195184-roc "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195184-roc"). Chakuna might stipulate that flight is off-limits during the Trial of Hearts. If she does, the Mists aid in enforcing this rule.

**_Navigation._** It’s easy to get lost in Valachan’s jungles. Unless they use gorges and waterways for guidance, characters must make DC 15 Wisdom ([**Survival**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills)) checks to avoid getting lost. Those who fail spend 1d4 hours wandering and risk encountering deadly creatures (see “[Trial of Hearts](#trialofhearts)” for suggestions). Characters should make this check once during each stage of a journey, or after dramatic or disorienting encounters.

**_Trial Route: Kiru Island._** An approximately 20-mile route from the Pantara Lodge to Kiru Island runs along the gorge south of the lodge to the Eirubamba River, then along the coast to Kiru Island. At a Normal pace, this route takes a day and a half (13 hours) to complete. Roughly 10 miles of this route follows the river. Characters who use a raft can move at Fast pace along the river. Rapids make the river [**Difficult Terrain**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#difficultterrain), but even so, moving at this pace reduces the journey to just over a day (11 hours).

**_Trial Route: The Scars._** Participants in the Trial of Hearts who travel from the Pantara Lodge to the Scars on Anquara Plateau have a route of approximately 20 miles if they follow the Shadowtaker Lakes east to the river called Killer’s Road, then ascend Anquara Plateau. Characters can follow most of this route at a Normal pace, but 2 miles involve climbing into the mountains—a Slow pace through Difficult Terrain, reducing movement to 1 mile per hour. This route takes over a day and a half (14 hours) to complete.

### Valachan Campaign Arc {#rthw:valachan:adventuresinvalachan:valachancampaignarc}

Campaigns set in Valachan often feature the Trial of Hearts and Chakuna’s connection to her domain. A campaign here might follow this outline:

**Levels 1–3.** Consider starting this campaign in another domain or world. During their travels, the characters become shipwrecked.

**Level 4–6.** The characters wash up on the shores of Valachan. They must find ways to survive the rainforest and fend off a pack of [**Displacer Beasts**](#ref:monster:5194970-displacer-beast "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194970-displacer-beast") that stalks them. They eventually find shelter in Oselo or Shuaran.

**Levels 7–9.** Having heard of their exploits, [**Chakuna**](#ref:monster:6491347-chakuna "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491347-chakuna") invites the characters to the Pantara Lodge, offering to help them leave if they participate in the Trial of Hearts (see the adventure below). Before the trial ensues, the characters might learn secrets from the head of Urik von Kharkov.

**Levels 10+.** If the characters survive the trial, Chakuna helps them leave the domain. As they characters travel out of Valachan, locals from Shuaran or Oselo beg the characters to stay and slay Chakuna. Should the characters kill Chakuna, chaos erupts and Urik von Kharkov strives to retake power in Chakuna’s absence.

### Trial of Hearts {#rthw:valachan:adventuresinvalachan:trialofhearts}

Adventure for Level 8 Characters

**Situation.** To escape Valachan, the characters must participate in the Trial of Hearts.

**Hook.** The hunter [**Chakuna**](#ref:monster:6491347-chakuna "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491347-chakuna") (see earlier in [this chapter](#chakuna)) invites characters to Pantara Lodge to discuss helping them leave the domain.

[![Map 2.35: Pantara Lodge View Player Version (Artist: Sean Macdonald)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/map-18-02.pantara-lodge.jpg)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/map-18-02.pantara-lodge.jpg)

#### Encounters {#rthw:valachan:adventuresinvalachan:trialofhearts:encounters}

The adventure uses [map 2.35](#map235pantaralodge) and consists of these encounters.

**_Pantara Lodge._** Chakuna seems welcoming and polite, inviting characters to relax before a feast where she’ll explain how to leave Valachan. Note the [**Indifferent**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#indifferentattitude) [**Displacer Beasts**](#ref:monster:5194970-displacer-beast "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194970-displacer-beast") that prowl the lodge—Chakuna’s guards and hunting companions. The characters receive a comfortable guest room where they can rest before dinner.

**_Chakuna’s Guests._** The characters aren’t Chakuna’s only guests. There are three other groups:

**Lizardfolk.** Tolocwa ([**Lizardfolk Sovereign**](#ref:monster:5195108-lizardfolk-sovereign "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195108-lizardfolk-sovereign")) and three lizardfolk (Medium [**Warrior Veterans**](#ref:monster:5195260-warrior-veteran "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195260-warrior-veteran")) are their tribe’s best hunters.

**Pirates.** The human Rakengel (Medium [**Pirate Captain**](#ref:monster:4904838-pirate-captain "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4904838-pirate-captain")) and three [**Pirates**](#ref:monster:4904830-pirate "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4904830-pirate") from his crew were recently shipwrecked.

**Thieves.** Aian, Citana, and Lesar are [**Weretigers**](#ref:monster:5195266-weretiger "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195266-weretiger") from Oselo who were caught stealing a rare herb.

**_Trial Rules._** After Chakuna’s guests have all gathered for a meal of vegetarian dishes and mineral water, Chakuna does the following:

- Explains the trial, outlining the rules from the “[Trial of Hearts Rules](#trialofheartsrules)” section.
- Insists this is the only way to leave Valachan.
- Notes that the group could attack her, but they would face her [**Displacer Beast**](#ref:monster:5194970-displacer-beast "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194970-displacer-beast") allies. If they want to fight, they should wait for the trial.

The pirate group speaks up in protest, but growls from Chakuna’s allies silence them. The Darklord answers questions, then encourages her guests to rest, as the trial starts at dawn. Those who leave before then violate the rules.

**_Cheating._** Chakuna catches anyone who tries to leave the lodge early or otherwise cheat. She encourages them to obey the rules and calls on six [**Displacer Beasts**](#ref:monster:5194970-displacer-beast "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194970-displacer-beast") to enforce them. She calls the monsters off before anyone dies.

**_Choosing a Route._** Have the characters choose a shrine as their destination and determine their route (see “[Valachan Exploration](#valachanexploration)”).

**_Trial Encounters._** The trial is divided into three phases, including a night camping in the jungle. As you narrate the characters’ journey through the wilderness, roll on the following table for an encounter that happens during each of the first two phases and another that occurs while camping.

**_Chakuna During the Trial._** Chakuna might appear and menace characters, but don’t track her travels or have her fight the characters directly.

**_Kiru Island Event._** During the last phase of their journey, groups traveling to Kiru Island must find a way to cross Sangui Cove. While crossing, they are attacked by a [**Giant Shark**](#ref:monster:5195027-giant-shark "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195027-giant-shark") and a [**Giant Crocodile**](#ref:monster:5195016-giant-crocodile "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195016-giant-crocodile").

**_Scars Event._** During the last phase of their journey, groups traveling to the Scars must climb 120 feet up Anquara Plateau. While they do, a [**Giant Ape**](#ref:monster:5195010-giant-ape "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195010-giant-ape") attacks them from above.

**_Trial Shrine._** At both destinations, the trial shrine is a wooden platform bearing a statue of a panther. It hangs at the end of a 200-foot-long rope bridge suspended 60 feet over a spiked pit. Those who fall into the pit take 21 (6d6) Bludgeoning damage plus 36 (8d8) Piercing damage.

**_Rival._** If the characters reached the shrine in less than 15 hours, they are the first ones there. After 2d4 hours, the pirate group arrives at the Kiru Island shrine, and the lizardfolk group arrives at the Scars shrine. Both groups are missing any members who died in previous encounters, but they’re [**Hostile**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#hostileattitude) and fight the party. If the characters took longer than 15 hours, this rival group arrives first.

**_Conclusion._** An hour after the group contends with its rivals, Chakuna arrives. If the characters followed the rules of the Trial of Hearts, she pronounces them victors and upholds her end of the bargain—helping them leave Valachan. If they broke the trial’s rules in even the slightest way, Chakuna knows it and fights them. In either case, each character earns a bonus 2,100 XP.
