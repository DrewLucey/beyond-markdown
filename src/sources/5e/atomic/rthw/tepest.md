Domain of Nature’s Cruel Secrets

All is well in Tepest. Fields shimmer with bountiful harvests, and horned village children recite sing-song rhymes. But those who linger among this land’s pastures and colorful cottages can’t escape the feeling of being watched or the impression that strange forms move amid the fields. Locals dismiss such worries as the work of fey tricksters, but their smiles fail to mask the desperation in their eyes.

Brutality wears a welcoming face in Tepest, a truth embodied by the ancient hag Lorinda, who betrayed her coven in pursuit of adoration. Taking the guise of a deity called Mother, [**Lorinda**](#ref:monster:6491377-mother-lorinda "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491377-mother-lorinda") adopted the village of Viktal, protecting its people from nature’s whims so they can worship her even as they sacrifice themselves to feed her monstrous offspring. In the wilderness beyond, resentful fey watch and plot, offering cruel bargains to those who stray beyond Mother’s sight. The people of Viktal do what they must to survive, using tradition to cloak their fear and complicity in a cycle of murder. Strangers become symbols of hope for Viktal’s citizens—welcome alternatives to slaughtering their families in Mother’s name.

## Domain Features {#rthw:tepest:domainfeatures}

Tepest is known for the following features:

**Children of the Fey.** Few children are born in Viktal. Would-be parents must bargain with Mother or the fey to bring them a child.

**Fey Threats.** Caves dot the land, connecting to depths where dangerous fey dwell. The people of Viktal fear them and blame them for every ill.

**The Last Village.** Viktal is the only community in Tepest. The others displeased Mother and suffered terrible fates.

**Mother.** Viktal’s residents devoutly follow the optimistic faith of the nature deity called Mother.

**Mother’s Minders.** Mother’s most zealous faithful are called Mother’s Minders. These devotees sacrifice their left eyes to Mother to show their faith.

**The Tithe.** Viktal’s festivals include a fertility celebration called the Tithe that occurs once a season.

## Locations in Tepest {#rthw:tepest:locationsintepest}

The valley of Tepest is rocky, ill-suited to farming, and plagued by dangerous fey. Nevertheless, those who worship Mother are protected from the worst of the land’s dangers. Mother’s Sight and Viktal are detailed below, and other notable locations are summarized on the [Tepest Locations table](#tepestlocations).

### Mother’s Sight {#rthw:tepest:locationsintepest:motherssight}

A mile outside Viktal, in a field apart from the surrounding farms, stands Mother’s Sight: an array of rune-etched standing stones. These stones rise as tall as thirty feet, and several have pieces that defy gravity. Within the central and largest stone floats a disk reminiscent of a great eye.

Here the people come to worship, celebrate, and sacrifice. Colorful crafts adorn long tables, where the villagers play games and feast in celebration of Mother’s bounty. Meals here are tasty, and guests are encouraged to eat and drink beyond their fill.

During rituals here, Viktal’s community leaders give thanks to Mother before a dramatic fire surrounded by an eight-foot scaffold. (See “[The Tithe](#thetithe)” adventure for additional details.)

### Viktal {#rthw:tepest:locationsintepest:viktal}

Mother ensures peace in Viktal. The village consists of a few dozen cottages, a market square, and an inn on the banks of Lake Kronov. Wicker scarecrows guard the rich fields blessed by Mother, and friendly people go about their daily lives. Cheery villagers welcome visitors, show them hospitality, and demonstrate interest in their stories.

## Mother Lorinda {#rthw:tepest:motherlorinda}

Darklord Who Demands Adoration

[![(Artist: Brian Valenzuela)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/17-003.mother-lorinda.jpg)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/17-003.mother-lorinda.jpg)

> “What precocious moppets you are! Little ones who show Mother love get rewarded, but bad children get punished. Which sorts of tots are you?”
>
> —Mother Lorinda

Mother Lorinda desires a family over all else, not realizing she’s destroyed every family she’s ever had. A creator of monstrosities, this ancient green hag obsesses over unnatural maternalistic pursuits.

Over generations, Tepest’s villagers have come to view Lorinda not as a powerful witch, but as a god of the land and fertility called Mother. Lorinda doesn’t dissuade her fragile mortal followers from their baseless cult practices. The villagers provide willing sacrifices for the hag’s flawed magical attempts to create life, and their adoration gradually soothes the violent, magical outbursts that follow her failures.

Lorinda’s walking tower of bones, the Gurgyl, ranges across Tepest. Those who seek her here find a distracted but deceptively cunning hag clinging to grotesque dolls of her family. She laments the loss of her sisters, Laveeda and Leticia, never acknowledging that they remain alive as her prisoners. She often speaks of her daughter, Laoirse (pronounced “LUR-shuh”)—the name she uses over and over again for each of her monstrous creations.

When Lorinda visits Viktal, she appears in her illusory guise as Mother, an ancient, powerful woman wearing a mothlike cloak and bearing a branch with burning limbs. She calls on the people to prove their love for her through sacrifice, whether of food and crafts, their left eyes, or their lives. Sometimes her arrival is sudden and terrifying, such as when she magically appears to crawl from one of her followers’ empty eye sockets.

Lorinda seeks new ways to create a permanent family. This might involve great sacrifices, stolen fey magic, strange adoptions, or all manner of supernatural bargains.

**_Overwhelming Presence._** Lorinda casts [**Slow**](#ref:spell:2619066-slow "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619066-slow"), using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting. Lorinda can’t take this action again until the start of her next turn.

### Mother Lorinda’s Tale {#rthw:tepest:motherlorinda:motherlorindastale}

The sisters Lorinda, Laveeda, and Leticia Mindefisk lived desperate lives at the wooded edge of a terrible land. Their brutish family claimed the girls were unnatural fey in disguise and forced them to toil constantly. After years of harsh treatment from their cruel father and brothers, the hopeless sisters snapped and killed an abusive traveler during a chance encounter. They butchered the body and served the remains to their unwitting family. Thus began a cycle of murder and cannibalism that persisted for years.

The Mindefisk sisters’ crimes were finally revealed when they all fell in love with a charming stranger. They squabbled, and unwilling to relent or share, they murdered the stranger in full view of their horrified family. With nothing left to lose, the sisters killed the rest of their family as the Mists rose around them.

The horror of the Mindefisk sisters only grew in the Domains of Dread. Finding themselves in Tepest, the sisters discovered their new nature as hags. Over generations, mortals and fey alike came to know them as fickle miracle workers. Despairing families came to them often, willing to trade much for hexblood children born from the hags’ magic (see [chapter 1](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/character-options#hexblood)).

Eventually, Lorinda grew fascinated by others’ pursuit of family. She entreated her sisters to help her create a child, but Laveeda and Leticia refused, loathing the idea of any one of them having greater happiness than the others.

Defying her sisters, Lorinda assembled a monstrosity of animal parts and foul magic in secret. With her new daughter’s help, the hag trapped her sisters inside their magic cauldron and stole their powers. Yet Lorinda’s joy with her creation was short—the monster’s magic quickly waned, and it rotted away. Ever since, Lorinda has sought to create a family of her own, producing countless monstrosities in experiments and approaching the villagers of Viktal in her guise as Mother. Over generations, this has given rise to the cult of the Mother in Viktal, but it has brought Lorinda no closer to attaining the lasting family connection she covets.

### Mother Lorinda’s Domain {#rthw:tepest:motherlorinda:motherlorindasdomain}

Tepest is altered by Lorinda’s presence, creating the following effects:

**Closing the Borders.** While in her domain, Mother Lorinda can close or open the borders to Tepest at will. While the borders are closed, a violent storm fills the Mists surrounding Tepest. A creature that enters the Mists for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or take 2d8 Lightning damage and be pushed up to 30 feet straight toward Tepest. A creature makes this save only once per turn.

**Mother’s Watchers.** Creatures that have sacrificed an eye to Mother can take a [**Magic**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#magicaction) action and pray to her. When they do so, Lorinda can perceive through the creature’s senses as well as her own until the end of the creature’s next turn. When perceiving through a creature’s senses, Lorinda benefits from any special senses it has.

**Sun and Storms.** While in Tepest, Lorinda can cast [**Control Weather**](#ref:spell:2619054-control-weather "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619054-control-weather"), requiring no Material components and using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting. When casting the spell this way, Lorinda can control the weather within 5 miles of herself, regardless of whether she is inside or outside.

If Lorinda dies, these effects end immediately.

### Mother Lorinda’s Lair {#rthw:tepest:motherlorinda:motherlorindaslair}

Ranging across the domain is Mother Lorinda’s home, the Gurgyl. This towerlike structure of thorns, wicker, and giants’ bones walks on three skeletal dragon legs.

#### The Gurgyl {#rthw:tepest:motherlorinda:motherlorindaslair:thegurgyl}

The villagers of Viktal know the Gurgyl serves their god in some way, but sighting it portends bad luck. The site has the following features:

**Kitchen-Laboratory.** The Darklord concocts foul creation magic in this central chamber.

**Nursery-Oubliette.** This room is home to whichever monster Lorinda calls her daughter, Laoirse.

**Old Cauldron.** Lorinda’s hag sisters languish in this plugged, oversize pot.

## Adventures in Tepest {#rthw:tepest:adventuresintepest}

Adventures set in Tepest feature rural cults, deceptive fey, and dread of nature’s mysteries. Use this section to develop horror adventures in this domain.

[![Celebrants parade from Viktal to Mother’s Sight to honor their god on the day of the Tithe (Artist: Sylvain Sarrailh)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/17-004.parade-to-mothers-sight.png)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/17-004.parade-to-mothers-sight.png)

### Horror in Tepest {#rthw:tepest:adventuresintepest:horrorintepest}

Adventures in Tepest often include the following elements.

#### Strangeness in Viktal {#rthw:tepest:adventuresintepest:horrorintepest:strangenessinviktal}

The locals seem welcoming and vigorous in their worship of the nature deity, Mother. The village leader, Wyan, guides them in their faith, assisted by elders called Mother’s Minders—all of whom are missing their left eye.

Residents invite visitors to stay at the village’s only inn, the Fisher’s Rest, free of charge for as long as they please. They encourage new arrivals to linger and participate in their upcoming festival, called the Tithe (detailed later in [this section](#thetithe)).

#### Mother’s Children {#rthw:tepest:adventuresintepest:horrorintepest:motherschildren}

Multiple times a year, Lorinda goes through a cycle of trying to magically create a new child. She calls this monstrous being Laoirse and dotes on it for a time. She believes that sacrifices to Laoirse during the Tithe can extend her child’s life, but inevitably, the monster’s magic fades, and it becomes mud and bones once more. For weeks afterward, Lorinda remains inconsolable, and violent storms and strange disasters plague Tepest.

Lorinda’s children take varied forms. To create such a monster, choose a stat block appropriate to challenge your characters, then consider how that creature might appear if it were made from different parts. For example, a [**Minotaur of Baphomet**](#ref:monster:5195126-minotaur-of-baphomet "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195126-minotaur-of-baphomet") or [**Werewolf**](#ref:monster:5195267-werewolf "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195267-werewolf") might be a bipedal mixture of ram, stag, and wolf parts, while a [**Shambling Mound**](#ref:monster:5195203-shambling-mound "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195203-shambling-mound") might be a person made of wicker. These descriptions should seem outlandish but reflective of the creature’s traits and actions. And no matter how bizarre her children are, Lorinda loves them all the same.

### Tepest Campaign Arc {#rthw:tepest:adventuresintepest:tepestcampaignarc}

Campaigns set in Tepest often involve Viktal’s cult and the dangerous spirits of the wilds. A campaign here might follow this outline:

**Levels 1–2.** Characters find themselves welcomed in Viktal and encouraged to take part in the Tithe (see the adventure below). At the rite’s conclusion, the villagers try to sacrifice one or more characters to one of Mother’s children. The characters defeat the monster, earning the enmity of both the villagers and Mother Lorinda.

**Level 3–5.** Fey creatures or druids who have been victims of Lorinda aid the characters; they reveal that she is no god, but a powerful hag who wanders the land in a walking tower called the Gurgyl. They send the group on a quest to learn from a mystical site or elder forest spirit how to infiltrate the hag’s lair.

**Levels 6–8.** After learning how to infiltrate the Gurgyl, the characters locate and break into Lorinda’s lair, finding some of the hag’s bizarre creations and her diminished, imprisoned sisters. When they emerge from the mobile fortress, they find themselves near Viktal, where [**Lorinda**](#ref:monster:6491377-mother-lorinda "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491377-mother-lorinda") confronts them, backed by her fanatical followers.

**Levels 9+.** If the group defeats Lorinda, the land spirals into chaos as fey from Mount Arak run wild. The characters might become involved in a power struggle between the good and evil fey of Arak. Lorinda’s return might drive the fey back—but the Darklord will help only if someone becomes her new child.

### The Tithe {#rthw:tepest:adventuresintepest:thetithe}

Adventure for Level 2 Characters

**Situation.** The characters participate in a religious celebration, at the end of which they are poised to become sacrifices.

**Hook.** The human leader of Viktal, Wyan (Medium, Neutral Evil [**Priest**](#ref:monster:5195170-priest "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195170-priest")) invites the characters to participate in the Tithe, a rite honoring their god, Mother.

#### Encounters {#rthw:tepest:adventuresintepest:thetithe:encounters}

This adventure begins the day of the Tithe, after [**Friendly**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#friendlyattitude) villagers from Viktal ([**Commoners**](#ref:monster:5194951-commoner "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194951-commoner")) lead the characters to Mother’s Sight. It uses map 2.33 and consists of these encounters.

**_Tithe Competitions._** Wyan directs the assemblage in a prayer to Mother, and the day of games begins. Characters each can play three games, participating in each selected game once. During each game, a character makes the ability check listed on the table below three times. Those who succeed on the check two or more times win the game; if they fail, a villager wins.

| Tithe Game                                                                                           | Check                                                                                                        |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Crooked Joust.** Participants use stilts strapped to their arms and legs to topple one another.    | Strength ([**Athletics**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills))         |
| **Powrie Chase.** Pursuers play tag in while wearing tattered red cloaks.                            | Dexterity ([**Stealth**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills))          |
| **Lost Siblings.** Blindfolded participants identify other players by touching their hair.           | Intelligence ([**Investigation**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills)) |
| **Gossamer Glutton.** Whoever eats the most live moths wins.                                         | Constitution                                                                                                 |
| **Never Naughty.** Participants flatter elderly villagers, who decide who wins and who gets paddled. | Charisma ([**Persuasion**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills))        |

Characters who don’t play a game might socialize and see some benign oddity extrapolated from the details presented earlier in this section.

**_Competition Winners._** The day’s games draw to a close at dusk. Wyan names a character who won two or more games as Mother’s Favorite and crowns them with a garland of white camellias. If no character won this many games, a human villager named Lorelei (Medium [**Commoner**](#ref:monster:5194951-commoner "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194951-commoner")) wins the crown and invites them to sit with her at the head table during the feast.

**_Feast._** Wyan begins the feast with another prayer as the Mother’s Minders light a grand bonfire within a tall scaffold. During the feast, villagers encourage the characters eat and drink to excess. A character who does so must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or gain the [**Poisoned**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#poisonedcondition) condition for the next 6 hours.

**_The Sacrifice._** At midnight, standing before the scaffold (see [map 2.33](#map233motherssight)), Wyan announces, “Now we prove our undying love for Mother and beg her for another season of bounty. Bring forth our sacrifice!” Mother’s Favorite receives a bowl of food and tiny crafts and is instructed to take it to the bonfire.

If Lorelei is Mother’s Favorite, she obeys and approaches the bonfire. If a character is Mother’s Favorite, they choose whether to approach. Regardless, 2 rounds later, a biped with wolflike features and stag’s horns (use the [**Minotaur of Baphomet**](#ref:monster:5195126-minotaur-of-baphomet "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195126-minotaur-of-baphomet") stat block) emerges from the field nearest Mother’s Favorite. This is one of Lorinda’s monstrous children. It fights Mother’s Favorite (and anyone else who damages it) to the death. During combat, the villagers raise their voices in a hymn but stay out of the conflict. Lorelei smiles and doesn’t defend herself if attacked.

**_Conclusion._** If the monster dies, Wyan and the villagers are horrified that the Tithe went wrong. They turn [**Hostile**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#hostileattitude), blaming the characters and demanding they leave. Mother Lorinda learns of this and seeks revenge.

If the monster wasn’t killed, the villagers remain [**Friendly**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#friendlyattitude), but their true nature becomes clear. Mother Lorinda also takes an interest in the characters, potentially seeking to adopt them as her new children.

Regardless of how the Tithe concludes, the characters gain 200 XP.
