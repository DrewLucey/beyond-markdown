Domain of Betrayal and Revenge

Deceptively beautiful, Kalakeri is a land of verdant rainforests and a web of rivers and lakes known as the Backwaters. At the height of its prosperity, Kalakeri was a robust center of art, commerce, and religion, with merchants spreading wild tales of the land, using fanciful names such as Sri Raji and the Steaming Lands. Now Kalakeri is a conflict-torn shadow of its past glory. The remnants of the Vasavadan dynasty—three heirs transformed into monsters by their depravity and hatred—trap the land in an endless cycle of war.

The people of Kalakeri live in fear of [**Maharani Ramya Vasavadan**](#ref:monster:6491382-ramya-vasavadan "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491382-ramya-vasavadan"), the domain’s Darklord, as well as her treacherous siblings, Arijani and Reeva. The Darklord’s curse manifests both in her deathless desire to rule Kalakeri and in her siblings’ endless rebellions. Both sides escalate their atrocities against one another, drawing the powerful and the innocent alike into their squabbles. As the maharani’s siblings court allies, the Darklord’s forces hunt and execute renegades, adding skulls to the grim Tower of Traitors.

## Domain Features {#rthw:kalakeri:domainfeatures}

Kalakeri is known for the following features:

**Factions in Conflict.** Two factions claim Kalakeri’s Sapphire Throne: the soldiers and loyalists of [Maharani Ramya Vasavadan](#ramyavasavadan), and the nobles and merchants who support the younger royal siblings, [Arijani and Reeva Vasavadan](#arijaniandreeva).

**Jadurai, a City at War.** The vast city of Jadurai is Kalakeri’s capital, the site of the royal palace, and a battlefield where factions struggle to claim or hold it as a symbol of rule.

**The Backwaters.** Rare creatures and extraordinary magical plants flourish in the Backwaters, the collective name for the wilderness away from Kalakeri’s cities. Scattered refugee groups hide in the wilds and organize dangerous, desperate attacks on the Vasavadan factions.

## Locations in Kalakeri {#rthw:kalakeri:locationsinkalakeri}

Tropical rainforests isolate Kalakeri’s communities. While the domain’s residents are adept at navigating these wildernesses, strangers often fall prey to the domain’s myriad hazards and predators.

The capital of Jadurai is detailed below, and other notable locations are summarized on the [Kalakeri Locations table](#kalakerilocations).

### Jadurai {#rthw:kalakeri:locationsinkalakeri:jadurai}

Ancient structures adorned with the artistry of past dynasties stand alongside modern buildings in Kalakeri’s capital, Jadurai. The city retains its splendor, but the conflict between the Vasavadan siblings takes an enormous toll as they struggle over the Cerulean Citadel, the nation’s capitol, as well as the Sapphire Throne within. Sections of the city lie in ruins.

On the outskirts of Jadurai rises the Tower of Traitors. Rows of skulls taken from the Darklord’s enemies fill depressions in this stone tower. Two skulls hold places of special prominence: the original human skulls of Arijani and Reeva Vasavadan. The Darklord’s skeletal soldiers build the tower as part of her plan to rid Kalakeri of evil and receive the gods’ blessings.

## Ramya Vasavadan {#rthw:kalakeri:ramyavasavadan}

Darklord Perpetually Denied Control

[![(Artist: Camille Alquier)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/11-003.ramya-vasvadan.jpg)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/11-003.ramya-vasvadan.jpg)

> “Are you loyal to your maharani? Or are you yet more rebel skulls to add to the Tower of Traitors?”
>
> —Ramya Vasavadan

Ramya Vasavadan desperately fights to claim and hold power over Kalakeri in a brutal cycle of victory and loss with her equally uncompromising siblings. Unlike most Darklords, Ramya struggles to control her domain, leading her to seek allies in opposing her siblings and their treacherous allies.

In her true form, Ramya is an undead horror, but illusions mask her monstrous appearance. Her true nature is revealed by her chilled skin and her reflection, which always appears withered and terrifying.

Although Ramya’s siblings, Arijani and Reeva, aren’t Darklords of Kalakeri, they motivate the rebellions in Kalakeri and Ramya’s torments. This monstrous pair is detailed in “[Arijani and Reeva](#arijaniandreeva).”

#### Traits {#rthw:kalakeri:ramyavasavadan:6491382-ramya-vasavadan:Traits}

**_Darklord Restoration._** If Ramya dies, her body dissolves into mist, and she gains a new body in 1d10 weeks, reviving with all her Hit Points somewhere in Kalakeri.

**_Legendary Resistance (3/Day, or 4/Day in Domain)._** If Ramya fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.

**_Magic Resistance._** Ramya has [**Advantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#advantage) on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**_Marshal Undead._** Undead creatures of Ramya’s choice (excluding herself) in a 60-foot [**Emanation**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#emanationareaofeffect) originating from her have [**Advantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#advantage) on attack rolls and saving throws. She can’t use this trait if she has the [**Incapacitated**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#incapacitatedcondition) condition.

### Ramya’s Tale {#rthw:kalakeri:ramyavasavadan:ramyastale}

The Vasavadan dynasty has ruled Kalakeri since ancient times. Ramya Vasavadan was handpicked by her father to succeed him, but on the bleak day of his death, Ramya’s brother, Arijani, declared himself the new maharaja.

Ramya refused to yield to her brother, and with her allies, she captured and prepared to execute him. But Ramya’s sister, Reeva, counseled mercy. Instead, Ramya imprisoned Arijani and ascended as Kalakeri’s maharani, Queen of Kings.

In the years that followed, Ramya ruled justly. But in the shadows, Reeva plotted with rebels, who eventually freed Arijani and spurred nobles across the nation to revolt.

Regretting her mercy, Ramya sent her army to crush the rebels and mercilessly execute the treacherous. Reeva and Arijani combated Ramya’s superior forces with ambushes and deceptions. Once again exploiting Ramya’s sympathies, the pair tricked Ramya, invaded the Cerulean Citadel, and executed their sister by strangling. With her final gasps, Ramya cursed her brother and sister, calling them bloodthirsty beasts. Soon after, a terrible monsoon struck Kalakeri, bringing with it the Mists.

The storm lashed Kalakeri for weeks. At its height, Ramya returned from the dead and called on those who had fallen in her service. Loyal in death, a corpse legion joined the revitalized maharani, besieged the Cerulean Citadel, and slaughtered Arijani and Reeva. Ramya then stole their skulls and built them into the foundations of what would become the Tower of Traitors.

Arijani and Reeva’s plots didn’t end with their lives. They too were reborn, transformed into monstrous reflections of their ravenous desires.

Now in the Land of the Mists, the conflict gripping Kalakeri continues, trapping the nation in an endless war and forcing its people to swear loyalty to monsters.

### Ramya’s Domain {#rthw:kalakeri:ramyavasavadan:ramyasdomain}

While Ramya is in control of the Sapphire Throne, Kalakeri is altered by Ramya’s presence, creating the following effects:

**Closing the Borders.** While in her domain, Ramya can close or open the borders to Kalakeri at will. While the borders are closed, the Mists surrounding Kalakeri sap the life of those within them. A creature that enters the Mists for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there and isn’t a Construct or an Undead must succeed on a DC 20 Constitution saving throw or gain 1 [**Exhaustion**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#exhaustioncondition) level. A creature makes this save only once per turn.

**Everlasting Army.** A Medium or Small Humanoid slain in combat rises 24 hours later as a [**Wight**](#ref:monster:5195269-wight "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195269-wight") under Ramya’s control, unless the Humanoid is restored to life or its body is destroyed.

If Ramya dies or if her siblings control the Sapphire Throne, these effects end immediately.

### Ramya’s Lair {#rthw:kalakeri:ramyavasavadan:ramyaslair}

The Cerulean Citadel is Kalakeri’s royal palace. It derives its name from the blue sandstone used in its construction. Control of the citadel and the Sapphire Throne within regularly passes between Ramya and her siblings.

#### Cerulean Citadel {#rthw:kalakeri:ramyavasavadan:ramyaslair:ceruleancitadel}

Bas-reliefs adorn the palace, depicting the history, heroes, and legends of the kingdom. The site is depicted on [map 2.20](#map220ceruleancitadel) and has the following features:

**Domed Court.** The central domed court houses the Sapphire Throne. It hosts guardians and treasures gathered by the paranoid Vasavadans. Towers guard the surrounding walls, which are accessible via bridges from the court’s upper floors.

**War-Torn Courtyards.** Within the palace walls lie marble pavilions arranged among baths, ponds, and gardens. Some are intact, but sieges and repeated conflicts have ravaged others.

### Horror in Kalakeri {#rthw:kalakeri:adventuresinkalakeri:horrorinkalakeri}

Adventures in Kalakeri often include the following elements.

#### Arijani and Reeva {#rthw:kalakeri:adventuresinkalakeri:horrorinkalakeri:arijaniandreeva}

Ramya’s rule is jeopardized by her siblings’ pursuit of power. Arijani and Reeva Vasavadan promise their followers wealth and freedom, but in truth, their agendas serve only them. Ramya still loves her siblings, making her vulnerable to their promises and lies. So long as Ramya exists, the Dark Powers resurrect Arijani and Reeva as if they had [Ramya’s](#ramyavasavadanstatblock) Darklord Restoration trait.

**_Arijani._** Arijani is the charming face of resistance for all who oppose Ramya. Now a [**Rakshasa**](#ref:monster:5195178-rakshasa "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195178-rakshasa"), gregarious Arijani has decadent tastes. When in control of the Cerulean Citadel, he hosts parties for Kalakeri’s elite that conceal private feasts featuring obscene delicacies and mortal flesh.

**_Reeva._** Reeva’s adoration of Ramya gave way to jealousy when Ramya eclipsed her in their family’s esteem. Now an [**Arcanaloth**](#ref:monster:5194899-arcanaloth "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194899-arcanaloth"), Reeve uses insidious magic from beneath the Cerulean Citadel to summon fiends and manipulate foes from afar. Behind her back, many mockingly call Reeva “Inajira,” her charismatic Arijani’s opposite.

### Renown in Kalakeri {#rthw:kalakeri:adventuresinkalakeri:renowninkalakeri}

You can track the characters’ influence with Ramya’s loyalist faction and her siblings’ rebel faction, using the [renown rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/dms-toolbox#renown) from the [Dungeon Master’s Guide](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024) to track the characters’ standing with these groups. Characters might gain renown with either faction by performing missions such as these:

- Capturing or killing a rival agent
- Planting evidence that a rival committed a crime
- Conscripting a powerful ally for the faction
- Revealing the location of a rival’s hold
- Revealing traitors within the faction

Anytime the characters complete a mission for either group, their Renown Score with that group increases by 1, but their Renown Score with the other group decreases by 1 (to a minimum of 0). After the first time characters gain renown with either faction, any faction the characters have a Renown Score of 0 with treat the characters as follows:

**Loyalist Faction.** Members of this faction are [**Hostile**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#hostileattitude) toward the characters. The faction sends troops of [**Wights**](#ref:monster:5195269-wight "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195269-wight") or other Undead to capture the characters weekly.

**Rebel Faction.** Members of this faction are Hostile toward the characters. The faction sends [**Assassins**](#ref:monster:5194904-assassin "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194904-assassin") or [**Wraiths**](#ref:monster:5174960-wraith "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5174960-wraith") under Reeva’s command to kill the characters weekly.

Characters can gain renown with a faction they have a Renown Score of 0 with by presenting proof of an action that undermines the other group. Even if betrayed, the factions seek the party’s aid, as they view the characters as useful pawns.

If the characters have a Renown Score of 5+ with either group, their deeds might benefit the faction enough for it to take control of Jadurai and claim the Sapphire Throne. The longer either faction controls the capital, the more extreme its reign becomes, its atrocities escalating week after week. The characters must balance aiding and opposing both factions to maintain equilibrium.

### Kalakeri Campaign Arc {#rthw:kalakeri:adventuresinkalakeri:kalakericampaignarc}

Campaigns set in Kalakeri typically involve balancing the horrors committed by the Vasavadan factions. A campaign here might follow this outline:

**Levels 1–4.** Agents from the rebel faction contact the characters, presenting Arijani and Reeva Vasavadan as misunderstood heroes seeking to overthrow a tyrant. They entreat the characters to save the heir of an allied noble family from a compound controlled by Ramya’s [**Wight**](#ref:monster:5195269-wight "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195269-wight") soldiers and return him to a hidden safe house.

**Levels 5–8.** The characters meet Arijani and Reeva in their disguises as charming human royals. They thank the characters for their aid and ask them to reclaim the shield of an ancient hero petrified in the Forest of the Still, hoping the shield will be a symbol of their right to rule. As the characters travel to the forest, a messenger delivers a request from [**Ramya**](#ref:monster:6491382-ramya-vasavadan "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491382-ramya-vasavadan") to do the same. The characters must face [**Basilisks**](#ref:monster:5194919-basilisk "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194919-basilisk") and the [**Wraiths**](#ref:monster:5174960-wraith "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5174960-wraith") of the petrified dead to recover the shield and then decide who to return it to.

**Levels 9–12.** Arijani and Reeva plot to attack Jadurai to claim the Sapphire Throne and request the characters’ help. Ramya’s spies have informed her of this plot, and she also seeks the characters’ aid. In the ensuing conflict, the characters might strike at any of the Vasavadan siblings.

### Tower of Traitors {#rthw:kalakeri:adventuresinkalakeri:toweroftraitors}

Adventure for Level 11 Characters

**Situation.** Arijani and Reeva Vasavadan seek to recover the skulls of their mortal bodies from the Tower of Traitors. They believe the skulls will grant them greater power.

**Hook.** Arijani or Reeva order the characters to recover two skulls from the Tower of Traitors but don’t say who the skulls belong to. They offer to pay the party with 10,000 GP in gemstones and a [**Candle of Invocation**](#ref:magic_item:9228374-candle-of-invocation "https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/9228374-candle-of-invocation").

#### Encounters {#rthw:kalakeri:adventuresinkalakeri:toweroftraitors:encounters}

This adventure assumes you are tracking the characters’ renown with Kalakeri’s loyalist and rebel factions (see “[Renown in Kalakeri](#renowninkalakeri)” earlier in this section). It includes these encounters.

**_Stolen Skulls._** The skulls Arijani and Reeva seek are from their own corpses, which were defiled by Ramya. The skulls are among hundreds in the Tower of Traitors. The pair don’t reveal who the skulls belong to but insist they’re vital to the rebel faction’s allies. To aid in the search, the siblings give the characters two [**Spell Scrolls**](#ref:magic_item:9229076-spell-scroll-level-2 "https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/9229076-spell-scroll-level-2") of [**Locate Object**](#ref:spell:2619002-locate-object "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619002-locate-object"). However, they don’t provide the characters with enough details for the spell to pinpoint the skulls.

**_Learning About the Skulls._** The characters must learn three details about the skulls for them to be familiar enough to the characters that a spell like _Locate Object_ can locate them. Characters can learn details in three ways:

**Research.** Characters who spend a day searching for rumors or investigating records in one of Kalakeri’s settlements learn a detail by succeeding on a DC 16 Charisma ([**Persuasion**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills)) check or Intelligence ([**Investigation**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills)) check respectively.

**Renown.** A character can spend a day questioning non-[**Hostile**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#hostileattitude) members of a faction. After doing so, the characters learn a detail, but their Renown Score with that faction is reduced by 1. The group can do this only once.

**Magic.** The [**Legend Lore**](#ref:spell:2619010-legend-lore "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619010-legend-lore") spell reveals one detail; each character can use this spell once.

Each time the party learns a detail about the skulls, reveal a portion of the story from the “[Ramya’s Tale](#ramyastale)” section, obscuring the names of the maharani and the traitors until the end.

**_Tower of Traitors._** The Tower of Traitors is depicted on [map 2.20](#map220toweroftraitors) and is guarded by patrols of two [**Revenants**](#ref:monster:5195182-revenant "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195182-revenant") and four [**Wights**](#ref:monster:5195269-wight "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195269-wight"). If the characters have a Renown Score of 3+ with the loyalist faction, the undead are [**Indifferent**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#indifferentattitude) and tell the party to move along—they fight only if characters linger for more than 5 minutes or remove a skull from the tower. If the characters have less renown with the loyalists, the undead are Hostile. A new patrol appears 2d6 rounds after a patrol is defeated.

**_Searching the Tower._** The tower is composed of thousands of skulls, and the ones the rebels want can be found via magic such as the [**Locate Object**](#ref:spell:2619002-locate-object "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619002-locate-object") spell. Once discovered, a skull can be removed in 1 minute using a sharp implement. When both skulls are freed, the hatred lurking in the skulls manifests as two Hostile [**Gallows Speakers**](#ref:monster:6491355-gallows-speaker "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491355-gallows-speaker") (see [chapter 5](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/denizens-of-the-mists#gallowsspeaker)).

**_Loyalty or Treachery._** The next time the characters rest, Arijani ([**Rakshasa**](#ref:monster:5195178-rakshasa "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195178-rakshasa")) appears and demands the skulls. If the characters give him the skulls, he compensates them and the characters’ Renown Score with the rebel faction increases by 4. If the characters refuse to give him the skulls, Arijani fights them. The party’s Renown Score with the rebels decreases by 4 if Arijani is slain. Soon after, Ramya invites the characters to discuss future plans.

Regardless of how the adventure concludes, each character earns a bonus 4,100 XP.
