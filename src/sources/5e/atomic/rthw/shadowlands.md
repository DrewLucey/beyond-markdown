# Shadowlands {#rthw:shadowlands:shadowlands}

Domain of Faded Glory and False Virtue

> #### **Darklord:** [_Ebonbane_](#ebonbane) {#rthw:shadowlands:ebonbane}
>
> **Genre:** [Gothic horror](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/creating-domains-of-dread#gothichorror)
>
> **Hallmarks:** Cursed magic items, doomed quests, falls from grace, knightly lineages, obsessive zeal
>
> **Mist Talismans:** Broken holy symbol, religious relic, symbol of the Circle
>
> **Aligned Tarokka Card:** Torturer
>
> **Opposed Tarokka Card:** Broken One

For the knights of the Shadowlands, the struggle with evil isn’t a philosophical trial—it is an active war. East of the rugged Theospine Mountains spreads the peaceful realm of Nidala, where the virtuous knights of the Circle guard their lands against wickedness beyond and within. To the mountains’ west lies the ruined land of Avonleigh, the birthplace of the knightly Circle; this realm fell to evil long ago and has become overgrown by the spirit-stalked Phantasmal Forest. Many knights of the Circle view themselves as inheritors of a legacy of shame. They aspire to the heroism of their storied ancestors and battle to absolve themselves of imagined failures. They throw themselves into crusades against foes, real and imagined, sacrificing their lives—and those of their people—for redemption and glory.

For all the Circle’s good intentions, these knights are cursed. Monsters and villains they’ve defeated reappear in the Shadowlands seeking vengeance. Most infamously, deep in the Phantasmal Forest lingers the evil that caused Avonleigh’s downfall: [**Ebonbane**](#ref:monster:6491352-ebonbane "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491352-ebonbane"), Darklord of the Shadowlands and the sapient sword of the Circle’s founder, Kateri Shadowborn.

## Domain Features {#rthw:shadowlands:domainfeatures}

The Shadowlands is known for the following features:

**Bastions on the Borderlands.** The Circle seeks to reclaim a foothold in Avonleigh in a region called the Borderlands. They often reward allies with Bastions here (see [chapter 3](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/ravenloft-adventures#hauntedbastions) and the [Dungeon Master’s Guide](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024) for details on [Bastions](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/bastions)).

**The Circle.** The knights of the Circle (described in [chapter 3](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/ravenloft-adventures#thecircle)) make their home in this domain.

**Nidala’s Ruler.** Elena Faith-hold rules Nidala. But this knight is rarely present in the land, as she leads deadly quests beyond the domain’s borders (see “[Faith-hold’s Crusades](#faithholdscrusades)”).

## Locations in the Shadowlands {#rthw:shadowlands:locationsintheshadowlands}

The Shadowlands is a dualistic domain: Avonleigh remains an overtly haunted wilderness home to lurking evils, while Nidala touts itself as the virtuous opposite. But the land’s harmony is artificial, its people forced to smile while trapped in the service of Elena Faith-hold and her sycophants. Accordingly, the domain’s conflict isn’t one of good versus evil, but rather of overt and covert evils.

The Phantasmal Forest is detailed below, and other notable locations are summarized on the [Shadowlands Locations table](#shadowlandslocations).

### Phantasmal Forest {#rthw:shadowlands:locationsintheshadowlands:phantasmalforest}

The Phantasmal Forest dominates the haunted land of Avonleigh, a shifting, surreal woodland cloaked by perpetual night. A dreamlike place controlled by _Ebonbane_, the forest changes constantly, and locations within drift like flotsam on a dark sea. The River Avalon and the King’s Highway—an overgrown route of misaligned stones—enter the forest, but within the woods they circle endlessly and never leave. Only those descended from the Circle’s founding members can navigate the Phantasmal Forest without becoming lost. All others wander at _Ebonbane_’s whim, becoming prey for the wolves, corrupted fey, cursed knights, or wicked souls that haunt the forest.

## Ebonbane {#rthw:shadowlands:ebonbane}

Darklord of Endless Vengeance

[![(Artist: Matthew G. Lewis)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/15-003.ebonbane.jpg)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/15-003.ebonbane.jpg)

> “Questing knights? Champions of the realm? Look upon thy sterling souls. Tell me, heroes, by what fair deeds art thou known in this rotted world?”
>
> —Ebonbane

[**Ebonbane**](#ref:magic_item:11610583-ebonbane "https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/11610583-ebonbane") is an evil, sapient blade that seeks to annihilate the legacy of the Shadowlands’ greatest hero and her allies. This includes all with the Shadowborn name and every member of the Circle.

_Ebonbane_ is a magic weapon (see “[Ebonbane as a Magic Item](#ebonbaneasamagicitem)” later in this section), but within Shadowborn Manor, it becomes a creature that can move on its own (see the **Ebonbane** stat block). Beyond the manor, it doesn’t use its stat block—it requires a wielder to work its evil in the world.

Many who attempt to attune to the weapon are slain outright by its evil. Those who do manage to wield _Ebonbane_ must endure the Darklord’s constant demands and risk becoming its pawn (see “[Sentient Magic Items](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/treasure#sentientmagicitems)” in the [Dungeon Master’s Guide](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024)). If unattended outside Shadowborn Manor, _Ebonbane_ eventually finds its way back there. No one can carry _Ebonbane_ out of the Shadowlands. If someone tries, the sword vanishes and reappears in Shadowborn Manor.

_Ebonbane_ has a flamboyant personality and a penchant for the dramatic and sadistic. It has no regard for life and delights in animating the corpses of foes, then destroying them in front of their allies. When it speaks, it uses language that mocks the elevated speech of knights.

#### Traits {#rthw:shadowlands:ebonbane:6491352-ebonbane:Traits}

**_Darklord Restoration._** If Ebonbane dies, it revives with all its Hit Points in 1d10 weeks, teleporting somewhere in Shadowborn Manor. **_Magic Resistance._** Ebonbane has [**Advantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#advantage) on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

#### Actions {#rthw:shadowlands:ebonbane:6491352-ebonbane:Actions}

**_Multiattack._** Ebonbane makes three Darkflame Slash attacks. It can replace one of these attacks with a use of Possess Dead if available.

**_Darkflame Slash._** _Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:_ +9, reach 5 ft. or range 90 ft. _Hit:_ 9 (1d10 + 4) Slashing damage plus 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage.

**_Possess Dead (Recharge 5–6)._** Ebonbane raises a Medium or Small Humanoid corpse within 5 feet of itself and possesses it. The corpse wields Ebonbane and is under its control. Ebonbane gains 50 [**Temporary Hit Points**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#temporaryhitpoints), and its statistics are the same, except its Speed increases to 30 feet, and it can speak through the corpse using any language the corpse knew in life.

The possession lasts until Ebonbane drops to 0 Hit Points or Ebonbane ends the possession as a Bonus Action. Once the possession ends, Ebonbane can’t possess the corpse again. The corpse crumbles to dust 24 hours after the possession ends.

**_Worthy Wielder._** _Charisma Saving Throw:_ DC 17, one willing Humanoid within 5 feet. _Failure:_ 36 (8d8) Force damage. If this damage reduces the target to 0 Hit Points, Ebonbane devourers its soul. A creature whose soul has been devoured by Ebonbane can be restored to life only by a [**Wish**](#ref:spell:2619213-wish "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619213-wish") spell. _Success:_ The target attunes to Ebonbane and can wield the sword (see “[Ebonbane as a Magic Item](#ebonbaneasamagicitem)”).

The target succeeds automatically after 1 minute. _Failure or Success:_ Ebonbane can’t take this action again until the start of its next turn.

### Ebonbane’s Tale {#rthw:shadowlands:ebonbane:ebonbanestale}

Kateri Shadowborn was a great hero who, alongside her order of valorous knights called the Circle, spent her life in service of good. Among her greatest deeds was leading an army against a nation whose king had been possessed by a fiend. She defeated the fiend, though doing so caused her magic sword to shatter.

After a life of great deeds, Kateri Shadowborn retired to live in peace with her family at Shadowborn Manor. But the hero had made many enemies. Thinking her vulnerable, wicked cultists recovered the pieces of Kateri’s broken sword and conjured the lingering malice of the evils she had thwarted. These the cultists forged into a terrible weapon, _Ebonbane_—and they became its first victims.

Animated by hatred for Kateri and her allies, _Ebonbane_ took control of its creators and became a curse on the world. For years it worked to destroy what Kateri had created, ruin what she loved, and slaughter those who aided her. Many times the evil sword’s wielder was captured, but none ever suspected that the weapon itself was behind the grisly crimes.

Finally, in the hands of an unassuming monk, _Ebonbane_ found its way to Shadowborn Manor, where grim news had isolated Kateri and worn her down. Aged but still a mighty knight, Kateri recognized _Ebonbane_’s threat, and a great battle ensued. During the clash, Kateri kept _Ebonbane_’s attention and ensured her family and friends escaped the manor. When only the hero and the wicked sword remained, Kateri summoned her remaining might into a blow to destroy _Ebonbane._ But the wicked sword proved too powerful. Kateri’s blade shattered, and _Ebonbane_ struck down the legendary knight. Exalting in its victory, the evil weapon possessed Kateri’s body and pursued her fleeing family. But as it did so, it witnessed its quarry fading into the rising Mists.

When the Mists receded, _Ebonbane_ found itself in a realm where the residents regarded the ancestors of Kateri Shadowborn and her fellow knights as heroes. Thrilled to continue its spree, _Ebonbane_ sought to leave Shadowborn Manor, but it had no one to carry it beyond the grounds. Now, the evil blade seethes in frustration, waiting for a worthy bearer to carry it into the world so it might destroy all the good that came from Kateri’s work.

#### [Ebonbane](#ref:magic_item:11610583-ebonbane "https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/11610583-ebonbane") {#rthw:shadowlands:ebonbane:ebonbaneasamagicitem:11610583-ebonbane}

Weapon (Longsword), Artifact (Requires Attunement)

The Darklord of the Domain of Dread known as the Shadowlands is _Ebonbane_, a sapient sword. [**Ebonbane**](#ref:monster:6491352-ebonbane "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491352-ebonbane") uses its monster stat block while in Shadowborn Manor.

**_Bound to the Shadowlands._** When you enter a space outside of the Shadowlands, your Attunement immediately ends and _Ebonbane_ teleports to somewhere in Shadowborn Manor.

**_Insatiable Rage._** The sword demands destruction. If the sword hasn’t slain a Celestial or a Humanoid for 3 days, you make a DC 17 Charisma saving throw at the next dawn. On a successful save, you take 8d8 Force damage. On a failed save, you are dominated by the sword, as if by the [**Dominate Monster**](#ref:spell:2619147-dominate-monster "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619147-dominate-monster") spell, and the sword demands the blood of a Celestial or a Humanoid. The spell effect ends when the sword’s demand is met.

**_Magic Weapon._** You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. When you hit a Celestial or a Humanoid with it, that attack also deals 3d6 Necrotic damage.

While you hold this weapon, you have [**Advantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#advantage) on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**_Sentience._** _Ebonbane_ is a sentient Chaotic Evil weapon with an Intelligence of 17, a Wisdom of 14, and a Charisma of 19. It has hearing and [**Truesight**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#truesight) out to 120 feet. The weapon speaks Common and can communicate with its wielder telepathically. While you are attuned to it, _Ebonbane_ also understands every language you know.

**_Personality._** _Ebonbane_ was created to kill the paladin Kateri Shadowborn, and it still possesses a burning hatred for goodly knights. _Ebonbane_ revels in seeing good defeated and hope vanquished. The sword bonds with any wielder that assists in its goals but always views it wielder as a lackey.

**_Destroying Ebonbane._** The only way to destroy _Ebonbane_ is to forge a weapon from the [Four Keys](#questofthefourkeys) and reduce _Ebonbane_ to 0 Hit Points with it (see the **Ebonbane** stat block).

### Ebonbane’s Domain {#rthw:shadowlands:ebonbane:ebonbanesdomain}

The Shadowlands is altered by _Ebonbane_’s presence, creating the following effects:

**Closing the Borders.** While in its domain, _Ebonbane_ can close or open the borders of the Shadowlands at will. While the borders are closed, whirling blades of magic fly through the Mists surrounding the Shadowlands. A creature that enters the Mists for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or take 3d10 Force damage. A creature makes this save only once per turn.

**Haunted Woods.** The forest within 1 mile of _Ebonbane_’s lair is wreathed in a powerful illusion. Whenever a creature other than _Ebonbane_ or the sword’s allies finishes a Short or Long Rest in that area, it must succeed on a DC 15 Intelligence saving throw, or it is lost in the forest, always finding itself at the location it started in until it finishes a Short or Long Rest.

**Turning Weapons.** While in its lair, _Ebonbane_ can transform nonmagical [**Greatswords**](#ref:equipment:22-greatsword "https://www.dndbeyond.com/equipment/22-greatsword") and [**Longswords**](#ref:equipment:4-longsword "https://www.dndbeyond.com/equipment/4-longsword") within 1 mile of the lair into [**Animated Flying Swords**](#ref:monster:5194895-animated-flying-sword "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194895-animated-flying-sword") by completing a 24-hour ritual.

If _Ebonbane_ is destroyed, these effects end immediately.

### Ebonbane’s Lair {#rthw:shadowlands:ebonbane:ebonbaneslair}

_Ebonbane_ remains imprisoned in Shadowborn Manor, a fortified estate hidden in the Phantasmal Forest. The grounds are strewn with the bodies of those who sought the legendary blade and failed.

#### Shadowborn Manor {#rthw:shadowlands:ebonbane:ebonbaneslair:shadowbornmanor}

Though abandoned, Shadowborn Manor remains in decent repair. The estate is depicted on [map 2.29](#map229shadowbornmanor) and has the following features:

**Dungeon.** The Circle understood that some evils needed to be contained. A secret stairway in the east wing leads to a dungeon that has remained hidden from _Ebonbane._

**Great Hall.** This hall hosts the round table where members of the Circle once met. _Ebonbane_ poses the bodies of knights here to possess as it pleases.

**Lady’s Chambers.** Kateri Shadowborn’s armored corpse lies in the largest bedroom on the second floor. It is _Ebonbane_’s favorite body to reanimate, and if destroyed, it reappears in this chamber.

## Adventures in the Shadowlands {#rthw:shadowlands:adventuresintheshadowlands}

Adventures set in the Shadowlands feature individuals striving to prove their worth or superiority. Characters might be welcome to join the knights of the Circle, or perhaps they belong to families with heroic histories. Such ties could connect characters to the domain’s history and future quests. Use this section to develop horror adventures in this domain.

### Horror in the Shadowlands {#rthw:shadowlands:adventuresintheshadowlands:horrorintheshadowlands}

Adventures in the Shadowlands often include the following elements.

#### Faith-hold’s Crusades {#rthw:shadowlands:adventuresintheshadowlands:horrorintheshadowlands:faithholdscrusades}

Elena Faith-hold rules Nidala, crushing its people under the weight of her intolerance and need to maintain her virtuous reputation. While she doesn’t lead the Circle, she is its most respected member.

Elena leads forays of knights into the Mists on lengthy quests to defeat wicked foes in mysterious lands. An invitation to join one of Elena’s crusades is a great honor and a death sentence. Whenever Elena returns from the Mists, she always does so alone, claiming to be the sole survivor of a great victory.

Elena Faith-hold isn’t a Darklord, but should someone ever destroy _Ebonbane_, she might ascend to that position.

#### Quest of the Four Keys {#rthw:shadowlands:adventuresintheshadowlands:horrorintheshadowlands:questofthefourkeys}

One can destroy _Ebonbane_ permanently using the sword of Kateri Shadowborn. However, the Dark Powers shattered her sword and scattered its pieces. These fragments are known as the Four Keys—emblems of Kateri’s valor and faith. Each embodies a different virtue and one of the four elements.

**_The Keys’ Locations._** The Four Keys might appear anywhere in the Shadowlands or other domains. The ghost of Kateri Shadowborn or lore hidden amid tragic sites might point toward the keys.

**_Using the Keys._** Should the characters collect the Four Keys, they can reforge them into a weapon that—if used to reduce [_Ebonbane_](#ebonbanestatblock) to 0 Hit Points—negates its Darklord Restoration trait. It’s up to you whether this ends the Darklord’s threat or unleashes a greater evil bound within.

### Shadowlands Campaign Arc {#rthw:shadowlands:adventuresintheshadowlands:shadowlandscampaignarc}

Campaigns in the Shadowlands often involve quests that reveal evils steeped in tragedies and abuses of power. A campaign here might follow this outline:

**Levels 1–4.** The characters aid members of the Circle in defeating a dangerous [**Basilisk**](#ref:monster:5194919-basilisk "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194919-basilisk") in Nidala. Impressed, the knights charge the characters with reclaiming a fort in the Borderlands from a wicked knight. The knight—a Circle member who died trying to recover Kateri Shadowborn’s sword—is now a [**Revenant**](#ref:monster:5195182-revenant "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195182-revenant"). Once the knight is defeated, the Circle gives the characters its fort as a Bastion ([detailed](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/bastions) in the [Dungeon Master’s Guide](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024)).

**Level 5–8.** In their Bastion, the characters find a piece of a broken sword. The spirit of Kateri Shadowborn appears to the most virtuous character and asks for their help freeing her spirit and defeating _Ebonbane._ She shares the details in the “[Quest of the Four Keys](#questofthefourkeys)” section. The characters begin a journey across the Shadowlands and beyond to recover the other three sword fragments.

**Levels 9–11.** Once the characters have the Four Keys, Kateri’s spirit instructs them to travel to the Stone Circle in the Phantasmal Forest, where the sword can be magically reforged as a [**Holy Avenger**](#ref:magic_item:9228774-holy-avenger "https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/9228774-holy-avenger"). With this weapon, the characters journey to Shadowborn Manor and face [**Ebonbane**](#ref:monster:6491352-ebonbane "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491352-ebonbane").

### The Shadowborn Sword {#rthw:shadowlands:adventuresintheshadowlands:theshadowbornsword}

Adventure for Level 10 Characters

**Situation.** A knight seeks to prove herself by finding a hero’s lost sword.

**Hook.** The human knight Lucia Lambert (Medium, Lawful Good [**Questing Knight**](#ref:monster:5195177-questing-knight "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195177-questing-knight")) invites the characters to join her mission to recover the lost sword of the hero Kateri Shadowborn. In return, she’ll reward them with 4,000 GP—or double that amount in additions to the characters’ Bastions.

#### Encounters {#rthw:shadowlands:adventuresintheshadowlands:theshadowbornsword:encounters}

The adventure starts at the edge of the Phantasmal Forest and consists of these encounters.

**_Phantasmal Forest._** The journey to Shadowborn Manor is 18 miles, with the forest’s density and perpetual [**Darkness**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#darkness) limiting characters to a [Slow pace](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/playing-the-game#travelpace). As Lucia’s family members have belonged to the Circle since its founding, she can navigate the forest at a Normal pace, so the journey takes only 8 hours.

**_Fallen Knight._** Outside the manor, a [**Death Knight Aspirant**](#ref:monster:5194962-death-knight-aspirant "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194962-death-knight-aspirant") wearing the armor of a Circle knight bars the path and warns characters not to enter. A Good character who succeeds on a DC 16 Charisma ([**Persuasion**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills)) check can convince the corpse to let them pass; otherwise, it attacks those who try to enter.

**_Shadowborn Manor._** Armored corpses lie about Shadowborn Manor (use map 2.29). If the characters disturb them or enter the great hall, five rise as [**Strahd Skeletons**](#ref:monster:6491389-strahd-skeleton "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491389-strahd-skeleton") (see [chapter 5](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/denizens-of-the-mists#strahdskeleton)).

**_Shadowborn’s Ghost._** The Lawful Good [**Ghost**](#ref:monster:5195008-ghost "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195008-ghost") of Kateri Shadowborn haunts the manor. If the characters seem near defeat, she appears and can distract _Ebonbane_ or lead them to the hidden dungeon.

**_Ebonbane._** [**Ebonbane**](#ref:monster:6491352-ebonbane "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491352-ebonbane") (see earlier in [this chapter](#ebonbane)) lies in the great hall. If with the characters, Lucia claims the sword and is stricken dead by _Ebonbane_, who animates her body. Otherwise, _Ebonbane_ animates another corpse. The Darklord tries to convince the characters to take it from the manor so it can slay their foes. If they reject it, _Ebonbane_ fights.

**_Conclusion._** Should the characters escape Shadowborn Manor, each of them earns a bonus 3,100 XP. If they don’t take _Ebonbane_, Kateri’s ghost appears to them the following night and explains that _Ebonbane_’s evil isn’t ended. She goes explains the Darklord’s history and how to destroy it (see “[Quest of the Four Keys](#questofthefourkeys)”). The Circle rewards the characters in Lucia’s stead if she is slain.
