# Innsmouth {#rthw:innsmouth:innsmouth}

Domain of the Imprisoned Apocalyptic

> #### **Darklord:** [Cthulhu](#cthulhu) {#rthw:innsmouth:cthulhu}
>
> **Genre:** [Cosmic horror](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/creating-domains-of-dread#cosmichorror)
>
> **Hallmarks:** Cosmic horror creatures, invasive alien dreams, surreal locations, warring cults
>
> **Mist Talismans:** Effigy of Great Cthulhu, starmetal rod or amulet with eldritch runes, sahuagin-tooth fishing lure
>
> **Aligned Tarokka Card:** Bishop
>
> **Opposed Tarokka Card:** Donjon

[![(Artist: Pam Wishbow)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/10-001.bishop-tarokka.png)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/10-001.bishop-tarokka.png) [![](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/10-002.donjon-tarokka.png)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/10-002.donjon-tarokka.png)

Hidden amid the Domains of Dread lies a hideous realm defiant of reason and smothered by the Mists. From a violent, murky sea rises a crescent of shattered peaks, mangled spires broken like the jaws of some slain thalassic titan. From noxious fumaroles, miasmic pillars lurch from this accursed isle into a sky inconsistently lit by a distant, drowned light.

Strange ruins dot this island’s rocky coasts, some suggestive of forgotten peoples, others alien in form and purpose. Massive statues of crimson-flecked bloodstone loom over sheer cliffs, each a towering effigy of some profane monstrosity or alien god. One shore bears the wreckage of a few dozen collapsing wooden structures. Little evidence remains of this settlement’s residents, but at its edge lies a toppled sign bearing a word in Common that gives the island its name: Innsmouth.

Despite its forsaken appearance, the isle of Innsmouth isn’t abandoned. Cults vie for control of the island, among them the servants of Great Cthulhu, a harbinger of world-rending aberrance. At the island’s hidden heart, [**Cthulhu**](#ref:monster:6491348-cthulhu "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491348-cthulhu") lies dreaming, its alien mind reaching across the Domains of Dread and beyond, drawing to it those who might rouse its full might and unleash it from its prison in the Mists.

## Domain Features {#rthw:innsmouth:domainfeatures}

Innsmouth is known for the following features:

**Cosmic Horror Visions.** Cthulhu’s dreams draw aberrant beings and shape Innsmouth as an amalgamation of cosmic horrors, from disparate ruins on the surface to stranger realms in the depths.

**Cult Haven.** Evil cults devoted to the entities Cthulhu, Dagon, Olhydra, and Zhudun struggle to control the domain. These cults view strangers as useful allies (see “[Cults of Innsmouth](#cultsofinnsmouth)” for details).

**Dreams of Cthulhu.** [Cthulhu](#cthulhu) seeks freedom. It manipulates the dreams of those on the island, sharing visions of their escape from the domain resulting from the same rituals that would free it.

## Locations in Innsmouth {#rthw:innsmouth:locationsininnsmouth}

Innsmouth is an inhospitable expanse of alien mountains and ruin-littered coasts. The village of Innsmouth and the mazelike Vaults are described below, and other notable locations are summarized on the [Innsmouth Locations table](#innsmouthlocations).

The ruined coastal village of Innsmouth is the most obvious shelter from the storms, alien creatures, and strange events on the island. The Cult of the Chthonic Night knows this and realizes its imprisoned master, Great Cthulhu, calls to strangers to aid the cultists in releasing it. This makes the ruined village a shadowy war zone, where cultists and their servants hunt for fresh acolytes and sacrifices.

### The Vaults {#rthw:innsmouth:locationsininnsmouth:thevaults}

Beneath the mountains of Innsmouth winds a labyrinth of improbable passages. Dramatic caverns narrow into the corridors of buried palaces that transform into the wooden halls of forgotten farmhouses. Regularly, these underground paths open into vast cavities, many with their own strange environments, weather, and celestial bodies. What lies among the Vaults? Cultists speak of the realms of alien beings, ghoul empires, countrysides ravaged by sapient energies, the dwellings of reclusive witches, and a thin place in the walls of the prison where Great Cthulhu lies trapped.

## Cthulhu {#rthw:innsmouth:cthulhu}

Darklord What Eternal Lies

[![(Artist: Brian Valenzuela)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/10-003.cthulhu.jpg)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/10-003.cthulhu.jpg)

> #### “Ph’nglui mglw’nafh Cthulhu R’lyeh wgah’nagl fhtagn.” {#rthw:innsmouth:cthulhu:PhngluimglwnafhCthulhuRlyehwgahnaglfhtagn}
>
> —Cult of the Chthonic Night chant

Cthulhu was—and inevitably will be once more—a threat to the entire multiverse. For untold ages, the Dark Powers have bound this harbinger of doom. Unlike the other prisoners of the Domains of Dread, Cthulhu isn’t plagued by ironic torments or thwarted ambitions. Rather, this ageless terror remains physically bound and can exert only a fraction of its terrible might. Nevertheless, Cthulhu’s dreams slip beyond its prison, manipulating minds and orchestrating events to ensure its release and a new epoch of multiversal doom.

### Cthulhu’s Tale {#rthw:innsmouth:cthulhu:cthulhustale}

Spawned beyond the borders of the multiverse in realities where time and life have little worth, Cthulhu is a servant of elder evils and a harbinger of their inevitable ruin. For untold eons, Cthulhu’s will influenced receptive minds across the multiverse, reaching out in dreams and using them to open gateways to the Far Realm. Through such portals, Cthulhu visited the Material Plane, corrupted and laid waste to civilizations, and erected city-size monuments to its mighty predecessors.

Such distortion and defilement didn’t go unnoticed by the Dark Powers. Multiple times the Mists closed around dread Cthulhu, and multiple times the terror evaded them. It was drawn into the Shadowfell when an extinct cabal of mages summoned it onto a sepulchral world of the Material Plane during a once-in-an-era alignment of eclipses. With the magic of the Shadowfell enhanced, the Mists rose and drew in Cthulhu. Raging and grasping, Cthulhu dragged with it remnants of past conquests and violent minds linked to its own. These disparate nightmares became the foundation of its prison: a surreal island hidden amid the Domains of Dread.

### Cthulhu’s Domain {#rthw:innsmouth:cthulhu:cthulhusdomain}

Innsmouth is altered by Cthulhu’s presence, creating the following effects:

**Choking Depths.** A creature can [**hold its breath**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#suffocationhazard) for only 30 seconds while in the domain.

**Closing the Borders.** While in its domain, Cthulhu can close or open the borders of Innsmouth at will. While the borders are closed, those who enter the Mists surrounding Innsmouth become lost at sea amid inky fog for 3d4 days before arriving once more at Innsmouth.

**Something in the Air.** Creatures other than Cthulhu and its allies have [**Disadvantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#disadvantage) on saving throws to avoid or end the [**Frightened**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#frightenedcondition) condition.

If Cthulhu dies, these effects end immediately.

### Cthulhu’s Lair {#rthw:innsmouth:cthulhu:cthulhuslair}

Amid surreal expanses within Innmouth’s depths lies the entrance to Cthulhu’s Vault, a prison within the Mists where Cthulhu lies trapped. The entrance to Cthulhu’s Vault is a ritual site etched with symbols of power from across time and hundreds of worlds. Those who perform the proper rituals, guided by visions from Cthulhu itself, might free themselves from the domain of Innsmouth, and, in so doing, unleash Cthulhu on the multiverse.

#### Cthulhu’s Vault Features {#rthw:innsmouth:cthulhu:cthulhuslair:cthulhusvaultfeatures}

Cthulhu’s Vault is a hidden realm, but the entrance to it lies amid the Vaults beneath the island. The vault is depicted on [map 2.18](#map218cthulhusvault) and has the following features:

**Noisome Brine.** A dark, frothy waterway winds through the Vaults, ultimately emerging from either the Cleft of Dreams or the mouth of the Drowned Colossus.

**Ritual Site.** A partially toppled monument bearing a tentacled visage marks a weak point in Cthulhu’s prison. Before it stands an altar etched with bizarre runes. These symbols are of otherworldly design, but each cult of Innsmouth knows the meaning of a few. Only cultists imbued with the might of all four cults can perform the ritual these runes describe and force open Cthulhu’s prison.

**The Perceptive.** A small ship called the _Perceptive_ lies beached here, a remnant of a group of cultists’ past attempt to access Cthulhu’s Vault. The bowsprit is broken, ending in a jagged point.

**The Providence.** The shattered remains of a ship called the _Providence_ serves as the shelter of a mysterious seer. It’s said this former cultist knows more of Cthulhu’s Vault than any other creature.

## Cults of Innsmouth {#rthw:innsmouth:cultsofinnsmouth}

Four cults operate in Innsmouth, each with its own vile ends. While these groups are ruinously evil, characters might temporarily ally with one or more to oppose another foe or to escape the domain.

### Cults and Cultists {#rthw:innsmouth:cultsofinnsmouth:cultsandcultists}

The following groups vie for power on and under Innsmouth. Following each cult’s name are the ritual words cult members use in their blasphemous practices, along with other details.

#### Cult of the Chthonic Night {#rthw:innsmouth:cultsofinnsmouth:cultsandcultists:cultofthechthonicnight}

Hail, Hail, Cthulhu Awaits

**Worships:** Cthulhu  
**Leader:** King-Priest Eeyog-Yah  
**Goal:** Free Cthulhu from imprisonment  
**Lair:** The village of Innsmouth and the Vaults

Initiates of the Cult of the Chthonic Night open their minds to the whispers of Great Cthulhu and seek to free the imprisoned terror from the Domains of Dread. Predominantly composed of [**Aberrant Cultists**](#ref:monster:5194866-aberrant-cultist "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194866-aberrant-cultist") and [**Kuo-toa**](#ref:monster:5195101-kuo-toa "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195101-kuo-toa") that dwell amid waterlogged ruins deep below the Graven Peak, the cult interprets visions from Cthulhu as omens, calls for sacrifice, and signs of their god’s coming.

King-Priest Eeyog-Yah, an ageless and desiccated [**Kuo-toa Archpriest**](#ref:monster:5195098-kuo-toa-archpriest "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195098-kuo-toa-archpriest"), leads the cult. Eeyog-Yah occupies a state of perpetual somnambulism and narrates its visions. The priest’s attendants decipher their leader’s rambling as directives from their god.

Those who join the cult and participate in its induction rituals hear the voice of Cthulhu more clearly than others do (see “[Cult Dark Gifts](#cultdarkgifts)”).

#### Cult of the Drowning Wave {#rthw:innsmouth:cultsofinnsmouth:cultsandcultists:cultofthedrowningwave}

Make Clean for the Aeon Wave

**Worships:** Olhydra  
**Leader:** Draa Shoom  
**Goal:** Sink Innsmouth below the waves  
**Lair:** Eyes of Olhydra

Those who await the Drowning Wave seek to make Innsmouth and all its inhabitants part of Olhydra’s realm on the Elemental Plane of Water. Olhydra, the Prince of Evil Water, then could use the might of Cthulhu and the mass sacrifice of the island to dominate the Elemental Planes and flood the Material Plane.

Draa Shoom, the cult’s [**Aboleth**](#ref:monster:5194867-aboleth "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194867-aboleth") leader, believes Olhydra will return the cult to a place of dominance over countless worlds in return for its sacrifice. To effect this, Draa Shoom coaxes [**Elemental Cultists**](#ref:monster:5194983-elemental-cultist "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194983-elemental-cultist") and shipwrecked individuals to the Eyes of Olhydra. There, amid boggy ruins and the lairs of murky elemental creatures, rises the Fathomless Gate. This ancient stone arch, thought to connect to the Elemental Plane of Water, is covered by bars of starmetal, which prevent passage by solid creatures. The cult seeks the proper rituals to open the gate, allowing Olhydra’s scouring waters to flood the domain.

Those who participate in the cult’s induction rituals are infused with a draught of Olhydra’s sapient form (see “[Cult Dark Gifts](#cultdarkgifts)”).

#### Cult of the Heavenly Doom {#rthw:innsmouth:cultsofinnsmouth:cultsandcultists:cultoftheheavenlydoom}

Empty Yourself to Gorge on the Stars

**Worships:** Zhudun  
**Leader:** Celesta Grayl  
**Goal:** Open gateways to join Zhudun  
**Lair:** Blind Observatory and the Map of the Eclipse

Among the broken heights of the domain, the obsessed astronomers of the Cult of the Heavenly Doom seek signs in the heavens and omens from Zhudun, the Corpse Star, an Elder Evil that wanders space and knows the secrets of extinction. These cultists hope to climb into the heavens and join Zhudun.

The cult’s leader, Celesta Grayl, has sought Zhudun for centuries. While documenting her observations, Grayl discovered a path to immortality and became a [**Vampire Umbral Lord**](#ref:monster:5195250-vampire-umbral-lord "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195250-vampire-umbral-lord"). [**Death Cultists**](#ref:monster:5194960-death-cultist "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194960-death-cultist"), along with desperate and deluded souls, have flocked to the ancient stargazer, forming the cult around Grayl and her discoveries despite their leader’s protestations and predations. The vampire suffers the cultists only so long as they help her decipher secrets from ruins around the island and retrieve knowledge from the grip of other cults. She believes their discoveries will help her reveal when the stars are right to join Zhudun in the dark places between the stars.

Those who join the cult and participate in its induction rituals release a portion of their life to the stars (see “[Cult Dark Gifts](#cultdarkgifts)”).

#### Cult of the Scaly Abyss {#rthw:innsmouth:cultsofinnsmouth:cultsandcultists:cultofthescalyabyss}

The Prince Awaits—Become the Way

**Worships:** Dagon  
**Leader:** Xr’gh  
**Goal:** Summon Dagon  
**Lair:** Dagon’s Claws

The followers of Dagon, Demon Prince of the Darkened Depths, lurk among and beneath the waves of Innsmouth. Ancient even among inhabitants of the Abyss, Dagon seeks to drag demon lords and their Abyssal realms into its demesne of Shadowsea. Once sated on the flesh of its foes, Dagon aspires to unleash its might across the multiverse in a sanity-shattering wave.

Xr’gh, a [**Sahuagin Baron**](#ref:monster:5195188-sahuagin-baron "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195188-sahuagin-baron") fanatically devoted to Dagon, seeks to turn all who dwell beneath the waves to the demon lord’s worship. Xr’gh seeks enough sacrifices to turn the sea red with blood, an offering great enough to attract Dagon’s notice and draw the demon lord to Innsmouth. The cult leader expects Dagon then to feed on Cthulhu and begin its conquest of the Abyss. Ravenous [**Sahuagin**](#ref:monster:5195190-sahuagin-warrior "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195190-sahuagin-warrior") follow Xr’gh, as do [**Fiend Cultists**](#ref:monster:5194993-fiend-cultist "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194993-fiend-cultist") and murderers desirous of the eerie gold treasures the sahuagin offer from their submerged vaults. The Cult of the Scaly Abyss hunts the domain’s ruined villages, abducting anyone who seeks shelter amid the wreckage. Those they find, they drag through secret paths beneath the waves to their ritual site under the island called Dagon’s Claw.

Those who join the cult and participate in its induction rituals develop shark-like organs (see “[Cult Dark Gifts](#cultdarkgifts)”).

### Cult Rites {#rthw:innsmouth:cultsofinnsmouth:cultrites}

The cults of Innsmouth seek initiates to aid in their unfathomable works. Those who prove themselves reliable devotees gain access to the transformative power of strange gods.

#### Cult Dark Gifts {#rthw:innsmouth:cultsofinnsmouth:cultrites:cultdarkgifts}

Characters who have a Renown Score of 10+ with an Innsmouth cult are honored as the chosen of the cult’s patron. They are urged to take part in a ritual that inducts them into the cult and bestows on them a supernatural boon. A character who participates in such a ritual gains the Dark Gift (see [chapter 1](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/character-options#darkgifts)) noted on the Innsmouth Cults Dark Gifts table.

**Innsmouth Cults Dark Gifts**

| Cult                                                | Dark Gift                                                                                          |
| --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Cult of Chthonic Night](#cultofthechthonicnight)   | [Gathered Whispers](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/character-options#gatheredwhispers) |
| [Cult of the Drowning Wave](#cultofthedrowningwave) | [Symbiotic Being](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/character-options#symbioticbeing)     |
| [Cult of the Heavenly Doom](#cultoftheheavenlydoom) | [Touch of Death](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/character-options#touchofdeath)        |
| [Cult of the Scaly Abyss](#cultofthescalyabyss)     | [Aberrant Anatomy](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/character-options#aberrantanatomy)   |

These Dark Gifts include magical transformations associated with the cult’s patron and its members. This magic can make it easier to navigate parts of the domain and bypass seals made by cult magic. This includes the binding sigils that mark Cthulhu’s Vault. A character with one of these Dark Gifts can’t gain another. A character with one of these gifts who leaves Innsmouth can choose to lose the Dark Gift or keep it.

## Adventures in Innsmouth {#rthw:innsmouth:adventuresininnsmouth}

Adventures in Innsmouth involve cults, degenerate rituals, nightmarish visions, and aberrant transformations. Use this section to develop horror adventures in this domain.

[![Nightmarish monuments and reality-defying ruins cover the island called Innsmouth (Artist: David Frasheski)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/10-005.nightmarsh-monuments.png)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/10-005.nightmarsh-monuments.png)

### Corruptive Horror {#rthw:innsmouth:adventuresininnsmouth:corruptivehorror}

The cults of Innsmouth are monstrous and dangerous, but not unwelcoming. Each seeks to bring a different terrifying cataclysm to Innsmouth. As characters interact with these cults and learn more of the island’s secrets, bizarre dreams encourage the characters to accept the Dark Gifts the cults offer and balance rivalries to gain the might of each. Groups that engage with each cult might have strange adventures across Innsmouth and undergo bizarre transformations. Those that do will find themselves with the power to free Cthulhu from the island—and maybe themselves as well.

### Dreams of Innsmouth {#rthw:innsmouth:adventuresininnsmouth:dreamsofinnsmouth}

Cthulhu sends dreams to those in Innsmouth—and in realms beyond—that might help it escape its prison. These dreams include strange images spurring the dreamers to seek the cults of Innsmouth, gain their powers, and then dispel the sigils in Cthulhu’s Vault. Dreams sent by Cthulhu might focus on the following topics and imagery:

**Cult of the Chthonic Night:** The village of Innsmouth, the Graven Peak, desiccated fish, tentacles

**Cult of the Drowning Wave:** The Eyes of Olhydra, shipwrecks, tidal waves, three eyes underwater

**Cult of the Heavenly Doom:** The peaks of Innsmouth, corpses in space, monoliths, whirling stars

**Cult of the Scaly Abyss:** Dagon’s Claws, deep-sea predators, sharks eating sharks, undersea ruins

**Cthulhu’s Vault:** The Cleft of Dreams, the Drowned Colossus, dark passages, strange sigils

Any of these dreams might end with a vision of the Mists parting and the characters’ home or a similarly tantalizing location appearing beyond.

### Old One’s Bargain {#rthw:innsmouth:adventuresininnsmouth:oldonesbargain}

Cthulhu’s dream-messages tempt characters to release the entity to win their own escape from its domain. Whether Cthulhu can or will fulfill this offer is up to you, but the characters might not wish to jeopardize reality to escape. In the course of exploring Innsmouth, they can find the writings of obsessed wizards and cult heresies that suggest the following secrets:

**Cthulhu’s Disruption.** Cthulhu’s power is such that, should someone release it, the Mists around Innsmouth would open and those in the domain could escape.

**Cthulhu’s Might.** Cthulhu wouldn’t emerge from its prison at full strength. For approximately one hour after its release, heroes might force Cthulhu back into its prison, should they subject the terror to potent magic such as [**Imprisonment**](#ref:spell:2619175-imprisonment "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619175-imprisonment") or [**Wish**](#ref:spell:2619213-wish "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619213-wish"), or a blow powerful enough to disrupt its physical form, like a powerful lightning strike, a massive explosion, or a strike from a large sailing ship.

Elaborate on these possibilities as characters explore Innsmouth, and encourage them to believe they might be able to outwit a Great Old One.

### Otherworldly Relics {#rthw:innsmouth:adventuresininnsmouth:otherworldlyrelics}

### Innsmouth Campaign Arc {#rthw:innsmouth:adventuresininnsmouth:innsmouthcampaignarc}

Campaigns set in Innsmouth involve warring cults, unfathomable beings, and bizarre transformations. A campaign here might follow this outline:

**Levels 1–2.** The characters wash up on Innsmouth island and shelter in the village. They experience strange dreams. Soon after, they encounter cultists who seek to initiate them and others who seek to sacrifice them. The characters gain allies among one of the island’s cults.

**Levels 3–5.** The allied cult sends the characters to disrupt other cults and retrieve relics from the ruins. In return, the cult offers insights into the characters’ dreams and suggests ways to escape the island. After proving their worth, a character receives the cult’s Dark Gift (see “[Cult Rites](#cultrites)”).

**Levels 6–16.** Led by their dreams and duplicitous cult members, the characters move from cult to cult, gaining influence by delving into ruins and strange vaults. In doing so, they collect Dark Gifts, as well as hints about Cthulhu’s Vault and ways to undermine the Great Old One (see “[Old One’s Bargain](#oldonesbargain)”).

**Levels 17–20.** After collecting a Dark Gift from each cult, the characters follow a treacherous path through the Vaults to Cthulhu’s Vault. There, they can risk releasing [**Cthulhu**](#ref:monster:6491348-cthulhu "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491348-cthulhu") to escape the island.

### Vault of the Elder {#rthw:innsmouth:adventuresininnsmouth:vaultoftheelder}

Adventure for Level 11 Characters

**Situation.** Cult lore tells of a vault that is home to a wise elder who knows the secrets of escaping the island.

**Hook.** A cult leader shares directions to the vault but wants one of the strange relics the elder is said to hoard.

#### Encounters {#rthw:innsmouth:adventuresininnsmouth:vaultoftheelder:encounters}

The adventure uses map 2.18 and consists of these encounters:

**_Vault Ambush._** On the way to the vault, two [**Cult Fanatics**](#ref:monster:16836-cult-fanatic "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16836-cult-fanatic") and a [**Fiend Cultist**](#ref:monster:5194993-fiend-cultist "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194993-fiend-cultist") (or another enemy cultist selected from those mentioned in “[Cults of Innsmouth](#cultsofinnsmouth)”) ambush the characters.

**_Frigid Spires._** The Vault of the Elder lies in a realm of icy mountains gripped by extreme cold (see “[Environmental Effects](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/dms-toolbox#environmentaleffects)” in the [Dungeon Master’s Guide](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024)). The towers of a gravity-defying stone city protrude from one mountain.

**_Hollow City._** The characters find the city filled with narrow ramps and abandoned, tubelike structures etched with eerie hieroglyphs. Of particular note is an out-of-place collection of tents and a templelike building.

**_Encampment._** Brown mold covers the tents (see [chapter 3](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/dms-toolbox#brownmold) of the _Dungeon Master’s Guide_). If the characters remove the mold, they find five cultists frozen inside the tents, wrapped in blankets. One wears a [**Ring of Mind Shielding**](#ref:magic_item:9228967-ring-of-mind-shielding "https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/9228967-ring-of-mind-shielding").

**_Elder Temple._** The templelike building bears carvings depicting five-winged beings fighting giant, fungus-like insects. Through the temple lies a path leading deeper into the mountain—and defended by a [**Shoggoth**](#ref:monster:6491388-shoggoth "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491388-shoggoth") (see [chapter 5](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/denizens-of-the-mists#shoggoth)).

**_Frozen Warning._** Ice encrusts the walls of the deeper temple, covering more strange carvings. Etched into the ice over a depiction of another five-winged creature is a warning in Common: “Beware the Elder.”

**_Elder’s Reliquary._** The passage ends in a chamber with four pillars of ice. Perched on one is a [**Hostile**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#hostileattitude) [**Elder Thing**](#ref:monster:6491353-elder-thing "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491353-elder-thing") (see [chapter 5](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/denizens-of-the-mists#elderthing)).

**_Ice Tombs._** The pillars of ice each hold a shadowy shape. These pillars have AC 14, HP 20, Immunity to Poison and Psychic damage, and Vulnerability to Fire damage. The pillars contain the following:

**Alien Relic.** The first pillar contains a strange relic. Roll on the “[Alien Relics](#alienrelics)” table earlier in this chapter to determine what it is.

**Mi-Go.** Two pillars contain [**Mi-Go**](#ref:monster:6491373-mi-go "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491373-mi-go") (see [chapter 5](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/denizens-of-the-mists#migo)), which awake 1 round after being freed.

**Vault Map.** A hieroglyph-etched slab in the final pillar charts a route from Innsmouth’s surface to Cthulhu’s Vault. It also provides information from the “[Old One’s Bargain](#oldonesbargain)” section. However, only someone with the [Dark Gift](#innsmouthcultsdarkgiftstable) of the [Cult of Heavenly Doom](#cultoftheheavenlydoom) can decipher its alien language.
