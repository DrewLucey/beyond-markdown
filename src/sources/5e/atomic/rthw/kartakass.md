# Kartakass {#rthw:kartakass:kartakass}

Domain of Tarnished Dreams

> #### **Darklord:** [Harkon Lukas](#harkonlukas) {#rthw:kartakass:harkonlukas}
>
> **Adventure Genres:** [Dark fantasy](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/creating-domains-of-dread#darkfantasy) and [gothic horror](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/creating-domains-of-dread#gothichorror)
>
> **Hallmarks:** Hidden identities, dangerous performances, exploitative ambitions, werewolves
>
> **Mist Talismans:** Handbill advertising remarkable performances, rustic woodcut of hunting wolves, wolf’s tooth necklace
>
> **Aligned Tarokka Card:** Swashbuckler
>
> **Opposed Tarokka Card:** Beast

Life in Kartakass feels staged and surreal, as every plant, beast, and person strives to prove their greatness. Trees and flowers erupt into bloom; songbirds sing themselves hoarse; and every local knows that dreams, fame, and immortal adulation are theirs should they prove worthy. And where talent and expertise fail, obsession and duplicity reign, leading to cycles of triumph, betrayal, and despair. Predators flourish in this land of unrealistic hopes, consuming passions, and vicious secrets.

With each full moon, the truth of Kartakass is exposed, as the domain’s many werewolves and other lycanthropes indulge their hunger for dominance and blood. After the full moon passes, the idyllic facade returns. Locals don’t speak of why most bar their doors and shutter their windows on the nights of the full moon, but they acknowledge that the path to greatness is messy.

## Domain Features {#rthw:kartakass:domainfeatures}

Kartakass is known for the following features:

**False Idylls.** Kartakass is supernaturally picturesque and outwardly serene. Every season is strikingly beautiful, communities appear idyllic, and many animals are affected as if by the [**Awaken**](#ref:spell:2618897-awaken "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618897-awaken") spell. This makes deceptions and violence all the more stark.

**Meistersingers.** Kartakass has no unified government. Each settlement is governed by a meistersinger, the acknowledged mayor and embodiment of local art. Meistersingers maintain creative rivalries with one another largely for show.

**Theatricality.** Settlements pride themselves on performative traditions, such as distinctive dress, traditional dances, or song stylings.

**Wolves and Werewolves.** Wolves roam the land freely, and [**Werewolves**](#ref:monster:5195267-werewolf "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195267-werewolf") lurk in the domain’s settlements. Residents seldom venture outside during the full moon, fearing the lycanthropes’ hunger.

## Locations in Kartakass {#rthw:kartakass:locationsinkartakass}

Kartakass is a land of rolling hills, light forests, clear lakes, and pastoral farmlands. Communities are welcoming and host frequent festivals. The settlements of Harmonia and Skald are detailed below, and other notable locations are summarized on the [Kartakass Locations table](#kartakasslocations).

### Harmonia {#rthw:kartakass:locationsinkartakass:harmonia}

Artists vie to perform at the town of Harmonia’s popular Crystal Club, a members-only venue inside a cave that features a stage constructed from a massive geode. The club features dramatic decor and discerning clientele. Rumors say that performers who fail to impress are exiled. The bloodier truth is that the club restricts membership to lycanthropes. Talented artists gain the curse and join their ranks, while the mediocre get devoured.

### Skald {#rthw:kartakass:locationsinkartakass:skald}

The largest community in Kartakass, Skald is a bustling hub of commerce and creativity. The lure of overnight celebrity and fawning crowds attracts the ambitious. Yet behind the facade of creativity and freethinking, business owners and celebrities prey on creativity, while performers struggle viciously over paltry opportunities. Vice, criminality, dark bargains, and supernatural predators flourish amid Skald’s many stages and frequent festivals. Among Skald’s most popular venues is the Old Kartakan Inn and Taverna, owned by the performer and Darklord Harkon Lukas.

## Harkon Lukas {#rthw:kartakass:harkonlukas}

Darklord Who Preys on Talent

[![(Artist: Igor Krstic)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/12-003.harkon-lukas.jpg)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/12-003.harkon-lukas.jpg)

> “I must say, your skill is unlike any I’ve ever seen. The name’s Harkon. I’m sure you’ve heard of me.”
>
> —Harkon Lukas

The charming werewolf Harkon Lukas manipulates those around him to satisfy his narcissistic needs for adulation and control. Endlessly seeking fame, Harkon travels widely, earning applause and accolades. Yet, whenever he returns to a town he’s performed in, he finds he’s been forgotten. Lukas believes he needs just the right song or inspiration to make himself unforgettable. To that end, he searches for talented protégés, cultivating their skills, then stealing their work. He ensures their loyalty by gifting them copies of his cursed wolf-tooth necklace, [**Harkon’s Bite**](#ref:magic_item:11610584-harkons-bite "https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/11610584-harkons-bite"), or by transforming them into werewolves. He then manipulates these victims into believing only he will accept them.

Harkon wears a signature wide-brimmed hat and wolf-tooth necklace, and he carries a violin he calls Bleeding Heart. He uses flattery to charm those he takes an interest in. A skilled artist who declines the offer to become his protégé often earns his obsession. The Darklord might go to any extreme to claim such performers’ talents as his own.

#### Traits {#rthw:kartakass:harkonlukas:6491360-harkon-lukas:Traits}

**_Blood Frenzy._** Harkon has [**Advantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#advantage) on attack rolls against any creature that doesn’t have all its Hit Points.

**_Darklord Restoration._** If Harkon dies, he revives with all his Hit Points in 1d10 weeks somewhere in Kartakass.

**_Legendary Resistance (3/Day, or 4/Day in Domain)._** If Harkon fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

**_Regeneration._** Harkon gains 10 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns if he has at least 1 Hit Point. If Harkon takes Radiant damage, this trait doesn’t function at the start of its next turn.

#### Actions {#rthw:kartakass:harkonlukas:6491360-harkon-lukas:Actions}

**_Multiattack._** Harkon makes two Swipe attacks. He can replace one attack with (A) Bite or (B) Enthralling Performance.

**_Bite (Dire Wolf or Hybrid Form Only)._** _Melee Attack Roll:_ +10, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 14 (2d8 + 5) Piercing damage plus 14 (4d6) Necrotic damage. If the target is a Humanoid, it is subjected to the following effect: _Constitution Saving Throw:_ DC 17. _Failure:_ The target is cursed. If the cursed target drops to 0 Hit Points, it instead becomes a [**Werewolf**](#ref:monster:5195267-werewolf "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195267-werewolf") under the DM’s control and has 50 Hit Points. The curse can’t be removed until Harkon dies. _Success:_ The target is immune to Harkon’s curse for 24 hours.

**_Swipe (Dire Wolf or Hybrid Form Only)._** _Melee Attack Roll:_ +10, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 16 (2d10 + 5) Slashing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the [**Prone**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#pronecondition) condition.

**_Enthralling Performance (Humanoid Form Only)._** _Wisdom Saving Throw:_ DC 17, each creature in a 30-foot [**Emanation**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#emanationareaofeffect) originating from Harkon. _Failure:_ The target has the [**Charmed**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#charmedcondition) condition for 1 hour or until it takes damage.

#### Bonus Actions {#rthw:kartakass:harkonlukas:6491360-harkon-lukas:BonusActions}

**_Shape-Shift._** Harkon [**shape-shifts**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#shapeshifting) into a Large dire wolf–humanoid hybrid or a Large dire wolf, or he returns to his true humanoid form. His game statistics, other than his size, are the same in each form. Any equipment he is wearing or carrying isn’t transformed.

#### Legendary Actions {#rthw:kartakass:harkonlukas:6491360-harkon-lukas:LegendaryActions}

Legendary Action Uses: 3 (4 in Domain). Immediately after another creature’s turn, Harkon can expend a use to take one of the following actions. Harkon regains all expended uses at the start of each of his turns.

**_Crushing Insult._** Harkon uses Shape-Shift to return to his humanoid form, then uses the following effect. _Wisdom Saving Throw:_ DC 17, each creature of Harkon’s choice that he can see within 30 feet of himself (creatures [**Charmed**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#charmedcondition) by Harkon automatically fail this save). _Failure:_ 16 (3d10) Psychic damage. _Failure or Success:_ Harkon can’t take this action again until the start of his next turn.

**_Pounce._** Harkon moves up to half his Speed and makes one Swipe attack.

**_Surprise Bite._** Harkon uses Shape-Shift to turn into his hybrid or dire wolf form and then makes one Bite attack. Harkon can’t take this action again until the start of his next turn.

### Harkon’s Tale {#rthw:kartakass:harkonlukas:harkonstale}

Harkon Lukas weaves numerous self-aggrandizing tales of his origins. Among them, Harkon tells of being spurned for his ambitions by the werewolves that raised him. Through talent and sacrifice, he became a legendary performer, and people flocked to bask in his remarkable presence.

Eventually, the rulers of Harkon’s homeland realized the threat posed by Harkon’s growing fame. Officials tried to imprison him for invented crimes, but Harkon’s fans defended him. When royal agents came for him, Harkon feigned his death, and his followers revolted. When outraged masses bearing Harkon’s coffin besieged the royal palace, Harkon ended his ploy. He burst from his crimson coffin in wolf form and devoured the land’s monarch. Drenched in blood, the “resurrected” Harkon returned to his human form and donned the monarch’s crown amid the cheers of his deluded followers. Yet before Harkon’s reign could begin, the Mists rose around him. When they cleared, Harkon found himself in an unfamiliar land called Kartakass.

### Harkon’s Domain {#rthw:kartakass:harkonlukas:harkonsdomain}

Kartakass is altered by Harkon’s presence, creating the following effects:

**Beast Spies.** From dawn until dusk, Tiny Beasts in the domain magically gain the ability to understand Common and can communicate telepathically with other Beasts and Monstrosities.

**Children of the Night.** Harkon exerts influence over the animals of his domain. From dusk until dawn, Medium or smaller Beasts within 1 miles of Harkon have the [**Charmed**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#charmedcondition) condition. If Harkon pleases, these animals become [**Hostile**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#hostileattitude) to creatures other than Harkon and his allies.

**Closing the Borders.** While in his domain, Harkon can close or open the borders to Kartakass at will. While the borders are closed, a strange melody fills the Mists surrounding Kartakass. A creature that enters the Mists for the first time on a turn or starts its turn in them must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw, or it has the [**Unconscious**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#unconsciouscondition) condition for 1d6 hours, then awakes within sight of the town of Harmonia in Kartakass. A creature makes this save only once per turn.

If Harkon dies, these effects end immediately.

### Harkon’s Lair {#rthw:kartakass:harkonlukas:harkonslair}

The Darklord owns a tavern in Skald called the Old Kartakan Inn and Taverna. The inn occupies a small island on the Sing Song River. Nightly, Harkon Lukas or artists under his tutelage perform there.

#### Old Kartakan Inn and Taverna {#rthw:kartakass:harkonlukas:harkonslair:oldkartakaninnandtaverna}

Welcoming and rustic, the Old Kartakan features subtle wolf-themed decor. The site is depicted on [map 2.22](#map222oldkartakaninnandtaverna) and has the following features:

**Bedrooms.** The inn’s rooms are insulated to block the sounds of performances and maulings alike.

**Harkon Lukas’s Room.** Several secret doors and a trapdoor lead to Harkon’s tower bedroom, where he hides evidence of his own uninspired music and trophies from past victims.

**Secret Doors and Chambers.** The inn is riddled with secret spaces to spy into other chambers, hide bodies, or indulge in bestial meals.

**Taproom.** Among the drinks the taverna offers is Meekulbrau, a blood-red berry wine that soothes the throat and improves vocal performances.

#### Harkon’s Manipulations {#rthw:kartakass:adventuresinkartakass:horrorinkartakass:harkonsmanipulations}

Harkon Lukas seeks to win the attention and trust of artistic and charismatic individuals. This might include characters—especially bards—or their allies. Harkon might do this to further a grand plot, but he also does so to assuage his perpetually bruised ego and satisfy his need for control. Harkon also seeks supernatural forces he can exploit, including magic items or beings with performative powers—such as an [**Instrument of the Bards**](#ref:magic_item:9228788-instrument-of-the-bards "https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/9228788-instrument-of-the-bards"), [**harpies**](#ref:monster:5195069-harpy "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195069-harpy"), or [**satyrs**](#ref:monster:5195195-satyr "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195195-satyr").

Harkon presents himself as a charming admirer or mentor seeking to aid others. He tries to manipulate those he pursues and earn their trust by encouraging them to do the following:

- Collaborate in creating a new song or show
- Compete in performances against the domain’s meistersingers
- Join him for tutoring or training exercises
- Protect him from a jealous former student
- Find a performer who stole one of his songs
- Travel with him on a tour of other towns

Harkon’s requests eventually turn criminal or violent. He might then blackmail those who aided him or threaten their reputations, or he might isolate them by gifting them [**Harkon’s Bite**](#ref:magic_item:11610584-harkons-bite "https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/11610584-harkons-bite"). Harkon continues such manipulations until it bores him, at which point he consumes his prey.

#### The Lukas Pack {#rthw:kartakass:adventuresinkartakass:horrorinkartakass:thelukaspack}

An ever-changing entourage of followers and victims surrounds Harkon Lukas, but three individuals are constants in the Darklord’s life:

**Akriel Lukas.** Harkon’s eldest daughter, Akriel (Medium, Chaotic Evil [**Performer Legend**](#ref:monster:5195154-performer-legend "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195154-performer-legend")), idolizes Harkon and pursues her own performing career. The Dark Powers don’t torment Akriel as they do her father, and her reputation steadily grows—much to her father’s resentment. Akriel is a human and isn’t a lycanthrope.

**Casimir Lukas.** Harkon’s eldest son, Casimir (Medium, Chaotic Neutral [**Spy Master**](#ref:monster:5195216-spy-master "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195216-spy-master")), is seeking his place in the world. He wants nothing to do with Harkon, preferring to travel, even to other domains. Whether at the whims of his father or the Mists, Casimir always finds his way back to Skald. Casimir is a human and isn’t a lycanthrope.

**Haldrake Moonbaun.** Harkon’s oldest ally, Haldrake (Medium [**Werewolf**](#ref:monster:5195267-werewolf "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195267-werewolf")) appears as a heavily tattooed human. He serves as the Old Kartakan Inn and Taverna mixologist and head bouncer. When Harkon isn’t in Skald, he ensures things at the taverna run smoothly. Haldrake aids Harkon in his plots and knows many of the Darklord’s secrets, so Harkon compensates him well.

### Kartakass Campaign Arc {#rthw:kartakass:adventuresinkartakass:kartakasscampaignarc}

A campaign in Kartakass might follow this outline:

**Levels 1–2.** The characters meet Maria, a human woman whose idealistic son, Frantosh, ran away to Harmonia to become a performer. She gives the characters an Uncommon [**Instrument of the Bards**](#ref:magic_item:9228788-instrument-of-the-bards "https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/9228788-instrument-of-the-bards") and will allow them to keep it if they return Frantosh home safely. Along the way to Harmonia, the characters are plagued by [**Wolves**](#ref:monster:4775850-wolf "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4775850-wolf") and people-eating [**Goblins**](#ref:monster:16907-goblin "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16907-goblin").

**Levels 3–6.** In Harmonia, the characters find Frantosh has begun performing with a skilled bard named [**Harkon Lukas**](#ref:monster:6491360-harkon-lukas "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491360-harkon-lukas"). Harkon encourages the characters to help Frantosh follow his dreams. To help Frantosh, Harkon asks the characters to fetch voice-enchanting waters from a mystical silver spring near Argent Falls Conservatory. The spring is guarded by dead performers risen as [**Banshees**](#ref:monster:5194916-banshee "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194916-banshee") and a covetous [**Young Copper Dragon**](#ref:monster:5195281-young-copper-dragon "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195281-young-copper-dragon").

**Levels 7–8.** On returning to Harmonia, the characters find Frantosh went to Skald with Harkon. There, Frantosh is performing nightly with Harkon at the Old Kartakan Inn and Taverna. Frantosh—now wearing a wolf-tooth necklace Harkon gave him—refuses to leave with the characters until after his first solo performance. The “Hungry for More” adventure below details this performance. After this Harkon might resent the characters or start obsessing over one of them.

### Hungry for More {#rthw:kartakass:adventuresinkartakass:hungryformore}

Adventure for Level 7 Characters

**Situation.** The singer Frantosh has fallen under the sway of Harkon Lukas. He refuses to return to his family until after he performs at the Old Kartakan Inn and Taverna on the night of the full moon.

**Hook.** Frantosh’s mother, Maria, offers the party an Uncommon [**Instrument of the Bards**](#ref:magic_item:9228788-instrument-of-the-bards "https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/9228788-instrument-of-the-bards") if they return her son.

#### Encounters {#rthw:kartakass:adventuresinkartakass:hungryformore:encounters}

This adventure uses map 2.22 and includes these encounters.

**_Frantosh’s Debut._** Frantosh (Medium [**Performer**](#ref:monster:5195156-performer "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195156-performer")) is performing on his own for the first time tonight. If the characters seek him out and mention his mother, he agrees to leave with them after his performance—it ends around midnight. If the characters try to abduct Frantosh, three [**Tough Bosses**](#ref:monster:4904883-tough-boss "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4904883-tough-boss") (or more) in Harkon Lukas’s service intervene.

**_Full-Moon._** Mention that the performance is on first night of the full moon only if a character asks.

**_The Old Kartakan._** The taverna is a popular spot in Skald. Crowds of [**Commoners**](#ref:monster:5194951-commoner "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194951-commoner") fill the first floor of the Old Kartakan. [**Harkon Lukas**](#ref:monster:6491360-harkon-lukas "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491360-harkon-lukas") (detailed earlier in [this section](#harkonlukas)) is here, but he avoids combat unless otherwise mentioned.

**_The Show._** During his 1-hour performance, Frantosh makes a DC 15 Charisma ([**Performance**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills)) check every 15 minutes. Characters can aid him with the [**Help**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#helpaction) action or in other ways. Track how many of these checks Frantosh succeeds on. During the performance, these events occur:

**Heckler.** A drunk **Commoner** heckles Frantosh. If he’s not silenced within 3 rounds, Frantosh has [**Disadvantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#disadvantage) on his next ability check.

**Brawl.** A fight breaks out between three [**Bandits**](#ref:monster:5194915-bandit "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194915-bandit"). After the first round of combat, a **Bandit** with bad aim targets Frantosh with its Light Crossbow action each round. If Frantosh is struck, he automatically fails his next ability check.

**Howl.** A wolf howls outside, distracting the audience. The characters have 1 round to direct attention back to Frantosh, perhaps with flashy magic or by making a DC 18 Charisma ([**Performance**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills)) check. If they don’t, Frantosh has [**Disadvantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#disadvantage) on his next ability check.

**_After the Show._** After the show, the characters see Frantosh vanish through a secret door. The next events take place in the inn’s hidden areas.

If Frantosh succeeded on three or more checks during the show, Harkon Lukas seeks to keep Frantosh. He won’t fight the characters, but he uses the necklace Frantosh wears to transform the singer into a [**Werewolf**](#ref:monster:5195267-werewolf "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195267-werewolf"). Frantosh and four more **Werewolf** servants of Harkon assault the characters.

If Frantosh didn’t succeed on three or more checks during the show, Harkon has grown bored with Frantosh. The characters find Frantosh [**Bloodied**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#bloodied) and being attacked by four **Werewolves**.

**_Conclusion._** If the characters escape the Old Kartakan with Frantosh (potentially now a **Werewolf**), each earns a bonus 1,700 XP. Even if forced into battle, Harkon Lukas won’t kill the characters—they’ve intrigued the Darklord, and he might appear to toy with them later.
