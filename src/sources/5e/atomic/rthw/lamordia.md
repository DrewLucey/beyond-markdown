A domain of amoral science, Lamordia shudders amid the icy environment and terror of its residents’ creations. Revolutions of steam and steel spur Lamordia’s people into frenzies of amoral invention. From industrialized communities and the iron walls of Ludendorf, the domain’s largest city, flow streams of scrap and chemical poisons that taint the land and transform creatures into horrors. Yet, the domain’s reckless scientists don’t pursue just mechanized inventions—the greatest goal is reinvention of life itself, transmuting people into ideals and unlocking generations of potential. In the name of such ambitions, the depraved reshape life and unleash abominations. And no aspirations have unleashed greater evils than those of the domain’s Darklord, [**Dr. Viktra Mordenheim**](#ref:monster:6491399-viktra-mordenheim "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491399-viktra-mordenheim").

## Domain Features {#rthw:lamordia:domainfeatures}

Lamordia is known for the following features:

**Frigid Environs.** Lamordia is a frigid land of bleak mountains, frozen swaps, and icy seas. Few voluntarily trek into its wilderness, fearing freak blizzards and starving beasts.

**Mutated Creatures.** Creatures warped by alchemy roam Lamordia. The most infamous are the monstrous “whales” of the Sea of Secrets—violent, unnatural leviathans, no two of which are alike.

**Rational Culture.** The people of Lamordia are direct and skeptical of superstition. They revere scientific innovation—as it’s all that stands between them and a life of frigid starvation. Magic is acknowledged but widely considered backward.

**Steam Technology.** Steam power driven by boiling sewers fuels clockwork inventions and massive cranes in the city of Ludendorf.

## Locations in Lamordia {#rthw:lamordia:locationsinlamordia}

Lamordia is a frigid land. In the north, the Sea of Secrets is choked with ice, while the mountains known as the Sleeping Beast are never without snow. The cities of Ludendorf and Neufurchtenburg are detailed below, and other notable locations are summarized on the [Lamordia Locations table](#lamordialocations).

### Ludendorf {#rthw:lamordia:locationsinlamordia:ludendorf}

The city of Ludendorf is a morass of steam-powered factories operating at the behest of morally bankrupt barons. Boiling sewers and steam tunnels thread the city’s foundations, heating cramped tenements and powering cranes serving the city’s fleet of ironclad ships. These ice-breaking, whaling vessels dare the Sea of Secrets to bring back massive beasts that provide vital food and fuel for the city.

At the city’s heart, the unscrupulous Ludendorf University conducts risky investigations into such fields as alchemical combustion, automated ingenuity, chemical sentience, and speculative anatomy. The university continually needs new subjects for experiments and hires volunteers or pays individuals for “flesh rights”—ownership of their cadavers once they die. A secret society within Ludendorf University idolizes Viktra and follows the reckless paths laid by her work.

### Neufurchtenburg {#rthw:lamordia:locationsinlamordia:neufurchtenburg}

The smoking refineries of Neufurchtenburg process minerals harvested from the Sleeping Beast. These rare, little-understood elements are then shipped to Ludendorf’s factories. Radiation-warped monsters from the nearby bogs frequently attack, disrupting operations, but the town’s people use hulking clockwork machines, cutting-edge firearms, and destructive alchemy to fend off incursions.

Darklord Who Pursues Perfection

From her laboratory at Schloss Mordenheim, Dr. Viktra Mordenheim uses her scientific genius to create horrors and wonders. All manner of undead and constructs march from her laboratory, but such flawed creations don’t satisfy Mordenheim. Rather, she seeks to re-create or reclaim her greatest creation, the Unbreakable Heart, a miraculous device that grants life to Mordenheim’s elusive reanimated victim, Elise.

**Corrosive Serum.** The target takes 10 (4d4) Acid damage at the start of each of its turns, and its Speed decreases by 10 feet.

**Deadly Serum.** The target takes 11 (2d10) Necrotic damage at the start of each of its turns, and it can’t regain Hit Points.

**Mind-Altering Serum.** The target takes 10 (3d6) Psychic damage at the start of each of its turns, and it has [**Disadvantage**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#disadvantage) on saving throws to maintain [**Concentration**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#concentration).

### Viktra’s Tale {#rthw:lamordia:viktramordenheim:viktrastale}

From an early age, Viktra Mordenheim sought to understand the complexities of anatomy. She pursued medicine not to aid patients but to reveal the secrets of life. Eventually, her research bent toward reanimating dead flesh and improving humanoid bodies. When Viktra began employing thieves to procure specimens for her tests, she met Elise, a beautiful resurrectionist whose spontaneity entranced the methodical surgeon. Viktra was smitten, and with Elise she discovered there might be more to life than chemical reactions.

When Elise started exhibiting signs of an incurable disease, Viktra hastened her experiments. On the doctor’s operating table, victims were killed, returned to life, and died again as Viktra sought a breakthrough. When finally Elise passed away, Viktra refused to accept failure. The doctor worked feverishly crafting her masterpiece, an artificial organ she called the Unbreakable Heart. But as she fitted the device into Elise’s corpse, constables burst into the lab and accused the doctor of numerous murders. As Viktra struggled against arrest, smoking chemicals, arcing electricity, and a strange mist filled the laboratory.

When the chaos cleared, Viktra awoke in Lamordia, an unfamiliar land where her genius was celebrated. Elise and the Unbreakable Heart were nowhere to be found, but the doctor soon heard rumors of a wondrous and terrifying figure who wandered the icy realm and whose chest glowed as though her heart were the sun. Ever since, Viktra has continued her experiments, striving to re-create her successes but failing every time. Between disappointments she searches for whatever Elise has become—and with her, the miracle of the Unbreakable Heart.

### Viktra’s Domain {#rthw:lamordia:viktramordenheim:viktrasdomain}

Lamordia is altered by Viktra’s presence, creating the following effects:

**Air of Obsession.** When a creature fails a Constitution saving throw to maintain [**Concentration**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#concentration), that creature takes 3 (1d6) Psychic damage.

**Closing the Borders.** While in her domain, Viktra can close or open the borders to Lamordia. While the borders are closed, lightning storms roll through the Mists surrounding Lamordia. A creature that enters the Mists for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there makes a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 4d6 Lightning damage on a failed save and half as much on a successful one. A creature makes this save only once per turn.

If Viktra dies, these effects end immediately.

### Viktra’s Lair {#rthw:lamordia:viktramordenheim:viktraslair}

Schloss Mordenheim is a castle that stands amid dramatic cliffs overlooking the sea. Travelers from Ludendorf reach Schloss Mordenheim by crossing the ice and following a treacherous road.

#### Schloss Mordenheim {#rthw:lamordia:viktramordenheim:viktraslair:schlossmordenheim}

Schloss Mordenheim holds dozens of rooms, but most are empty or have furnishings pushed aside to make room for scientific supplies. Prominent locations from this site are depicted on map 2.24 and have the following features:

**Auxiliary Workshops.** Viktra’s assistants toil in workshops where they craft tools and devices to the doctor’s exacting specifications.

**Disused Chambers.** Rooms originally constructed for relaxation serve as storage rooms, act as extra laboratories, or sit eerily empty. A dining hall half filled with crates is set for a single diner, but it’s rarely used.

**Gate and Entry.** A stone bridge crosses a chasm leading to the castle’s keep. Stairs flanked by grim statuary lead to the keep’s entrance.

**Laboratory Tower.** A heavily renovated turret holds Viktra’s laboratory. Mechanisms allow the tower’s roof to part and its operating table to rise 30 feet, exposing subjects to lightning during the island’s frequent storms.

## Adventures in Lamordia {#rthw:lamordia:adventuresinlamordia}

Lamordia provides a stage for adventures featuring amoral science and altered or resurrected beings. Use this section to develop horror adventures in this domain.

### Horror in Lamordia {#rthw:lamordia:adventuresinlamordia:horrorinlamordia}

Adventures in Lamordia often include the following elements.

#### Elise von Brandthofen {#rthw:lamordia:adventuresinlamordia:horrorinlamordia:elisevonbrandthofen}

Elise knows she died yet finds herself alive again. She is now a [**Mordenheim’s Monster**](#ref:monster:6491376-mordenheims-monster "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491376-mordenheims-monster") (see [chapter 5](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/denizens-of-the-mists#mordenheimsmonster)), and her life is sustained by the Unbreakable Heart (see the following section). Elise is learning to accept her new life but avoids Lamordia’s people, who label her as an outcast and a monster. She also knows that Viktra Mordenheim seeks to recover the Unbreakable Heart. The doctor’s actions—as well as the mercenaries she sends to abduct Elise—have shattered the pair’s bond. Now Elise hides at a secret sanctuary in the [Isles of Agony](#islesofagony) called Hope’s Heart. Here, she shelters other victims and creations of Viktra, showing them the esteem their creator denies them. Those in Elise’s care have taken to calling themselves the Children (detailed below).

#### Mordenheim’s Masterpiece {#rthw:lamordia:adventuresinlamordia:horrorinlamordia:mordenheimsmasterpiece}

The Unbreakable Heart is a nonmagical scientific wonder. Despite being the creator of the Unbreakable Heart, Viktra can’t re-create it and is obsessed with reclaiming it and rediscovering its secrets.

---

##### Unbreakable Heart {#rthw:lamordia:adventuresinlamordia:horrorinlamordia:mordenheimsmasterpiece:unbreakableheart}

This heart-shaped, clockwork device installs itself, fastening into place if positioned where a creature’s heart used to be. A creature that contains the device is affected as if by the [**Raise Dead**](#ref:spell:2618922-raise-dead "https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618922-raise-dead") spell. After this, the creature is immune to disease, ceases to age, and doesn’t die of old age. The Unbreakable Heart glows, shedding [**Bright Light**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#brightlight) in a 10-foot radius and [**Dim Light**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#dimlight) for an additional 10 feet. While inside a creature, the device causes the creature to shed Dim Light in a 10-foot radius.

---

#### The Children {#rthw:lamordia:adventuresinlamordia:horrorinlamordia:thechildren}

The Children are discarded creations of Dr. Viktra Mordenheim who have found shelter with Elise. Composed a few dozen Constructs and chemically animated Undead, the Children follow Elise’s lead and seek to live peacefully away from those who would misunderstand and harm them.

The exception to this is Adam, a [**Mordenheim’s Monster**](#ref:monster:6491376-mordenheims-monster "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491376-mordenheims-monster") who recently joined the Children. Adam was created by Viktra to capture Elise, but Elise convinced him to join her instead. Unlike Elise, Adam was created from disparate parts and knows no previous life. Although Elise seeks to teach Adam humanity, he shows little respect for the lives of more fragile beings. Moreover, Adam is fascinated by Mordenheim’s work and quietly seeks ways to create life that will serve him.

### Lamordia Campaign Arc {#rthw:lamordia:adventuresinlamordia:lamordiacampaignarc}

Campaigns set in Lamordia often make the characters subjects or accomplices to Viktra’s schemes. A campaign here might follow this outline:

**Levels 1–2.** A dean of Ludendorf University hires the characters to escort the coach of students traveling to an assistantship at Schloss Mordenheim. Along the way, they are plagued by mismatched [**Homunculi**](#ref:monster:5195080-homunculus "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195080-homunculus") that sabotage their travels. These are members of the Children, seeking to stymie folk they take for Mordenheim’s allies.

**Levels 3–6.** At Schloss Mordenheim, Viktra Mordenheim hires the character to recover her invention, the Unbreakable Heart. She explains it was stolen by a monster that dwells on the Isles of Agony. Viktra wishes the characters to bring back the heart and the monster. The characters face dangers in the wilds, but the quest culminates in the “Isles of Agony” adventure (see below).

The conclusion of the campaign depends on whether the characters aid Viktra or Elise. When they return to Schloss Mordenheim, events might play out in the following ways:

**Levels 7–9 (Aiding Viktra).** If the characters aid Dr. Mordenheim, the doctor seeks to remove the Unbreakable Heart from Elise or test it by reanimating a new being. This results in a dramatic operation where an enraged [**Mordenheim’s Monster**](#ref:monster:6491376-mordenheims-monster "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491376-mordenheims-monster") (see [chapter 5](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/denizens-of-the-mists#mordenheimsmonster)) breaks free and rampages through the castle.

**Levels 7–9 (Aiding Elise).** If the characters aid Elise, the characters must free innocent victims of the doctor from the castle and prevent Dr. Mordenheim from animating new terrors by destroying her laboratory. Ultimately the characters must face [**Viktra Mordenheim**](#ref:monster:6491399-viktra-mordenheim "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491399-viktra-mordenheim") (detailed earlier in [this section](#viktramordenheim)) and two [**Flesh Golems**](#ref:monster:5194997-flesh-golem "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194997-flesh-golem") made from the assistants the characters brought to the castle.

The campaign might continue with the characters continuing to aid or being targeted by the Children.

### Isles of Agony {#rthw:lamordia:adventuresinlamordia:islesofagony}

Adventure for Level 5 Characters

**Situation.** The characters must recover the Unbreakable Heart (detailed earlier in [this section](#unbreakableheart)). They don’t know it’s inside the outcast Elise.

**Hook.** Viktra hires the characters to recover the Unbreakable Heart, which was stolen by a monster. They are offered 2,500 GP, plus a 1,000 GP bonus if they capture the monster.

#### Encounters {#rthw:lamordia:adventuresinlamordia:islesofagony:encounters}

The adventure begins when the characters reach the Isles of Agony and consists of these encounters.

**_Shipwreck._** Soon after reaching the icy isles, the characters find six human survivors of a wrecked whaling ship. These sailors ([**Pirates**](#ref:monster:4904830-pirate "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4904830-pirate")) are led by Mate Herzogin, and their captain was carried off by a half-human, half-machine monster. The survivors seek help returning home but want to recover their captain’s corpse (and their pay, which he carried).

**_The Monster._** The monster the survivors fear is one of the Children, a [**Flesh Golem**](#ref:monster:5194997-flesh-golem "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194997-flesh-golem") named Kartad. He’s peaceful, but the survivors’ captain, Hans Giesbrecht (Medium [**Pirate Captain**](#ref:monster:4904838-pirate-captain "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4904838-pirate-captain")), attacked him on sight. Kartad knocked out Hans, panicked, picked him up, and fled to Hope’s Heart.

**_Track of the Monster._** Characters who succeed on a DC 12 Wisdom ([**Survival**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills)) check find and can follow Kartad’s tracks to Hope’s Heart. If the characters fail, a survivor points out the path. Roll on or choose results from the following table for three encounters that happen on the way (rerolling duplicates).

| 1d4 | Encounter                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

**_Hope’s Heart._** Hope’s Heart is a hidden canyon home to numerous harmless Constructs that is accessed via a passage in an abandoned cabin. When the characters approach, Elise appears on the cliffs 20 feet above and demands to know their business. Elise is a [**Mordenheim’s Monster**](#ref:monster:6491376-mordenheims-monster "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491376-mordenheims-monster") (see [chapter 5](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/denizens-of-the-mists#mordenheimsmonster)). The Unbreakable Heart glows from her chest.

**_Fighting Elise._** Elise attacks to prevent entry to the cabin and to defend herself. She avoids killing and won’t pursue those who flee. If the characters attack, they’ll likely have to make multiple attempts to wear down Elise or use trickery to defeat her.

**_Negotiating with Elise._** Elise is [**Indifferent**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#indifferentattitude) to the characters or [**Hostile**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#hostileattitude) if they harmed any Constructs on the isle. She’s wary of those who working for Viktra and can share the details described in “[Viktra’s Tale](#viktrastale).” If a character explains they’re seeking the missing captain, she brings forth Hans and Kartad. Kartad has nursed Hans back to health; he apologizes and releases the bewildered captain.

**_The Crew._** The six survivors arrive to see the characters speaking with Kartad. They overreact on seeing the “monster” and initiate combat. Kartad panics and defends himself. Hans has 10 Hit Points but is too weak to command his crew or fight.

**_Aftermath._** If the characters fight alongside the crew, Kartad and Elise defend themselves but flee after 3 rounds or if anyone is killed. Elise leads her followers to hide elsewhere on the island. The sailors thank the characters and offer to help them rally aid in Ludendorf to return and hunt the monsters.

If the characters deescalate the conflict or defend Kartad, Elise’s attitude toward them improves. She won’t give them the Unbreakable Heart or return to Schloss Mordenheim. Rather, she tries to convince the characters to see Viktra for the villain she is. She entreats them to return to the castle, save captive members of the Children, and thwart the doctor, as detailed in “[Lamordia Campaign Arc](#lamordiacampaignarc).” In return, Elise offers a trove of 4,000 GP and an [**Immovable Rod**](#ref:magic_item:9228785-immovable-rod "https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/9228785-immovable-rod").

Characters who defeat or choose to aid Elise earn a bonus 1,100 XP.
