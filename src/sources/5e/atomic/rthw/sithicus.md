# Sithicus {#rthw:sithicus:sithicus}

Domain of the Soul’s Shadow

>
> **Genre:** [Dark fantasy](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/creating-domains-of-dread#darkfantasy)

>
> **Mist Talismans:** Black rose, burnt armor, scrap of black fabric, three thorns
>
> **Aligned Tarokka Card:** Paladin
>
> **Opposed Tarokka Card:** Innocent

[![(Artist: Pam Wishbow)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/16-001.paladin-tarokka.png)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/16-001.paladin-tarokka.png) [![](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/16-002.innocent-tarokka.png)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/16-002.innocent-tarokka.png)

Whatever Sithicus was or could have been, it will never be. The dramatic ruins of a fabulous elven realm cover a land realm forested by gigantic trees and burning with the flames of a forgotten magical disaster. Scattered communities appear among the dilapidation, the domain’s predominantly elf population living like strangers amid structures to which they have no connection. At dusk, people withdraw, hiding from the domain’s true residents: the shadows.

By night, the shadows of Sithicus reenact forgotten lives or mimic the deeds of questing knights, devoted lovers, and lurking monsters. Most of these shadows are harmless, if unnerving, and seem not to notice other beings. Some, however, resent the domain’s other residents and torment the living.

Though few in the domain realize it, Sithicus has ties to the world of Krynn on the Material Plane. Ruins suggest the work of elves from that world, while the night sky blazes with the five-headed dragon constellation of the deity Takhisis. As on Krynn, the black moon Nuitari is visible only to evil beings. But the domain’s clearest tie to Krynn is its Darklord, the accursed knight [**Lord Soth**](#ref:monster:6491370-lord-soth-of-sithicus "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491370-lord-soth-of-sithicus").

## Domain Features {#rthw:sithicus:domainfeatures}

Sithicus is known for the following features:

**Bound to Krynn.** Sithicus is a warped reflection of the lands of Krynn. This world and its people, such as kender, are described in [Dragonlance: Shadow of the Dragon Queen](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sotdq).

**Cataclysmic Fire.** Accursed flames blaze amid prominent ruins and turn shadows into monsters drawn from Lord Soth’s past.

**Living Shadows.** Shadows in Sithicus move on their own. Most are harmless, but some are deadly creatures such a [**Shadows**](#ref:monster:5174958-shadow "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5174958-shadow") or [**Shadow Demons**](#ref:monster:5195200-shadow-demon "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195200-shadow-demon").

**Soth’s Traitor.** Lord Soth’s undead soldiers hunt for the ghostly knight Caradoc, whom Soth blames for his imprisonment in Sithicus.

## Locations in Sithicus {#rthw:sithicus:locationsinsithicus}

Two significant features of Sithicus are detailed below, and other notable locations are summarized on the [Sithicus Locations table](#sithicuslocations).

### Endless Ruins {#rthw:sithicus:locationsinsithicus:endlessruins}

Sithicus lies blanketed in endless ruins. Ancient forests rise from them, rivers flow in their broken avenues, and whole mountain ranges are sculpted with crumbled towers. These structures suggest the work of a vast, magical elven nation, but all evidence of these people has been reduced to enigmatic scraps of art and lore. At the heights of crumbling towers or in empty crypts blaze violet, cataclysmic fire (see “[Cataclysmic Flames](#cataclysmicflames)”). In rare cases, these phantasmal flames reveal glimpses of Lord Soth’s past and details that the clever might use to win the Darklord’s favor.

### Great Chasm {#rthw:sithicus:locationsinsithicus:greatchasm}

The Great Chasm gapes in Sithicus like a wound. None can say how deep it goes, as light fails to pierce more than a few dozen feet into the ravine. Those who descend deeper vanish into the shadows and rarely return. Those who do tell of a malicious darkness beneath the domain, a cacophony of hateful voices, and shadows that hunger.

Hidden amid ruins spilling into the chasm is the home of Inza Kulchevich, a hateful witch who can speak to shadows and believes all virtue is a lie. Despite her cynicism, she might perform prophetic fortune-telling or share strange lore in return for her visitors’ shadows.

## Lord Soth {#rthw:sithicus:lordsoth}

Darklord Cursed by Memories

[![(Artist: Cynthia Sheppard)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/16-003.lord-soth-of-sithicus.jpg)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/16-003.lord-soth-of-sithicus.jpg)

> “Explain why you have interrupted my reverie, or I will turn your waking hours into nightmares.”
>
> —Lord Soth

Lord Loren Soth seethes within a realm that mocks and frustrates him. Having known the rigid standards of knighthood and warfare, Soth finds Sithicus empty and chaotic, his people apathetic and undisciplined. The elven beauty that once captivated him lies ruined, the land burns with the flames of Krynn’s Cataclysm, and the realm’s shadows remind Soth of his past failures. Worse, no worthy foes exist to challenge him. So Soth broods on his throne in Nedragaard Keep, haunted by banshees that sing the tale of his disgrace and by rotted, undead copies of the knights who once served him.

Lord Soth cares nothing for his domain. But should noteworthy threats or remarkable individuals appear, he seeks to crush or enlist them. The only exception is the spirit of his treacherous former ally, Caradoc. Soth’s undead servants and his werebadger seneschal, Azrael Dax, scour the land for the elusive ghost.

Soth shares his imprisonment in Sithicus with all in the domain by keeping its borders perpetually closed. Only those who rouse him to activity or invade Nedragaard Keep might convince him to let them escape. This risks provoking the mighty death knight’s wrath, but Soth still thinks of himself as a true knight—he might assist those who present themselves as honorable warriors or show knowledge of his home (see “[Influencing Lord Soth](#influencinglordsoth)”).

>
> Lord Soth features in adventures in both the Dragonlance and Ravenloft campaign settings. If you want to give Soth a unique relationship to the Domains of Dread, consider these possibilities:
>
> - The nature of Soth’s curse creates aspects of the death knight in both Krynn and Sithicus. When Soth broods in his fortress in Krynn, he remains active in Sithicus. When the evil god, Takhisis, calls for his service on Krynn, Soth enters a magical torpor in his fortress in Sithicus.
> - Soth traveled from Krynn to Sithicus and is destined to return. If he leaves Sithicus, the domain might be destroyed, or a villain like Inza Kulchevich might take over.
> - Soth was trapped in Sithicus, escaped, and was trapped again, perpetuating a cycle of despair.
>
> Soth’s story and where characters encounter him are for you to detail in your adventures.

### Lord Soth’s Tale {#rthw:sithicus:lordsoth:lordsothstale}

Lord Soth was given a quest to save the people of his world, Krynn, from the wrath of the gods. Possessed by baseless paranoia and possessiveness of his elf wife, Soth forsook that quest, and the world was devastated in a great Cataclysm. In punishment, Soth was resurrected as a death knight and, for centuries, brooded on his disgrace in the charred ruins of his fortress, Dargaard Keep.

When the evil god Takhisis whispered to Soth, promising to restore his glory and avenge him against the heroic knights who banished him long ago, Soth answered and served Takhisis. During this time, he called on his undead servants, including the ghost of the knight Caradoc. Seeking to forge a new knighthood of loyal dead, Soth became obsessed with Dragon Highlord Kitiara Uth Matar and charged Caradoc with claiming her soul. When Kitiara died in battle, Caradoc refused to capture her spirit unless Soth bargained with Takhisis to restore Caradoc to life. Enraged, Soth tried to destroy Caradoc as the Mists rose around them.

When the Mists faded, Soth found himself in the domain of [Barovia](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/barovia). For a time, he found an ally in [**Count Strahd von Zarovich**](#ref:monster:6491390-strahd-von-zarovich "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491390-strahd-von-zarovich"), but the two villains couldn’t abide one another for long. Soth eventually pursued rumors of his treacherous servant, Caradoc, into the Mists. When the Mists parted again, the domain of Sithicus spread before him.

### Lord Soth’s Domain {#rthw:sithicus:lordsoth:lordsothsdomain}

Sithicus is altered by Lord Soth’s presence, creating the following effects:

**Closing the Borders.** While in his domain, Lord Soth can close or open the borders to Sithicus. While the borders are closed, purple flames fill the Mists surrounding Sithicus. A creature that enters the Mists for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there takes 5 (1d10) Fire damage plus 5 (1d10) Necrotic damage.

**Fearless Soldiers.** Undead allies of Lord Soth have Immunity to the [**Frightened**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#frightenedcondition) condition.

**Guilt of Sithicus.** Few sleep soundly in Sithicus. Whenever a creature other than Lord Soth and his allies finishes a Long Rest in the domain, the creature must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or gain 1 [**Exhaustion**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#exhaustioncondition) level.

If Lord Soth dies, these effects end immediately.

### Lord Soth’s Lair {#rthw:sithicus:lordsoth:lordsothslair}

[![Nedragaard Keep (Artist: Titus Lunter)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/16-004.nedagaard-keep.png)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/16-004.nedagaard-keep.png)

Lord Soth rules from Nedragaard Keep, a twisted, copy of his accursed fortress on Krynn, Dargaard Keep. Nedragaard Keep grows and withers over time like a living thing. Undead soldiers patrolling the keep regularly forget their duties or collapse into lifelessness. The disorder within the rigid death knight’s fortress endlessly frustrates Lord Soth.

#### Nedragaard Keep {#rthw:sithicus:lordsoth:lordsothslair:nedragaardkeep}

Nedragaard Keep is a towerlike fortress of crumbling, flame-scarred stone. Prominent locations in the keep are depicted on [map 2.31](#map231nedragaardkeep) and have the following features:

**Gatehouse.** Undead soldiers guard the gates to Nedragaard Keep, demanding to know trespassers’ identities. Those who identify themselves with the proper passphrase, such as “a seeker of the rose” or “a servant of Nightlund’s lord,” are admitted. Stairs beyond the gate climb several floors to the top of the keep’s walls.

**Garden.** Gardens on the keep’s second floor are overgrown with thorny plants—use the rules for [razorvine](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/dms-toolbox#razorvine) from the [Dungeon Master’s Guide](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024). From here spread massive vines that bloom with black roses and strangle the entire castle.

**Throne Room.** High in the keep is Soth’s throne room. Here Lord Soth spends much of his time dwelling on failures and twisted fantasies. Thirteen [**Banshees**](#ref:monster:5194916-banshee "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194916-banshee") haunt the keep, and at any time, at least three linger here, singing of Soth’s disgrace.

## Adventures in Sithicus {#rthw:sithicus:adventuresinsithicus}

Adventures set in Sithicus feature mysterious shadows, grim ruins, and strange foes from the warped memories of Lord Soth. Use this section to develop horror adventures in this domain.

### Horror in Sithicus {#rthw:sithicus:adventuresinsithicus:horrorinsithicus}

Adventures in Sithicus often include the following elements.

#### Cataclysmic Flames {#rthw:sithicus:adventuresinsithicus:horrorinsithicus:cataclysmicflames}

Illusory violet flames burn across Sithicus—fires from the Cataclysm that devastated Krynn. Powerful shadows guard prominent, bonfire-like pillars of these flames. These shadows might use the stat blocks of any monster, but they resemble dragons, knights, and figures from Soth’s past.

Whenever someone defeats one of these guardians, the flame it protected reveals an illusory scene from the Darklord’s past before vanishing. Any character who witnesses such a scene learns something of Soth’s history, the world he comes from, or the knighthood he once belonged to. One of the viewers then gains the Charm of the Black Rose (see “[Supernatural Gifts](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/dms-toolbox#supernaturalgifts)” in the [Dungeon Master’s Guide](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024) for additional details).

The Charm vanishes once you use either effect.

#### Influencing Lord Soth {#rthw:sithicus:adventuresinsithicus:horrorinsithicus:influencinglordsoth}

Lord Soth is typically [**Hostile**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#hostileattitude) to strangers, but characters can adjust his attitude to [**Indifferent**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#indifferentattitude) by succeeding on a DC 20 Charisma ([**Deception**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills) or [**Persuasion**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills)) check. Soth might employ characters he deems worthy on quests, such as the hunt for the ghost Caradoc. Making a second successful DC 20 Charisma ([**Deception**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills) or [**Persuasion**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#skills)) check while offering Soth something he covets can improve Soth’s attitude to [**Friendly**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#friendlyattitude) for a short time, during which he might let others leave his domain.

#### Sithicus Campaign Arc {#rthw:sithicus:adventuresinsithicus:horrorinsithicus:sithicuscampaignarc}

Campaigns set in Sithicus often involve trying to gain Lord Soth’s favor and win escape from the domain. A campaign here might follow this outline:

**Levels 1–4.** Azrael Dax, Lord Soth’s werebadger seneschal, brusquely recruits the characters to hunt down deadly shadows. These shadows take the form of and behave as noble knights.

**Level 5–9.** Impressed by the characters, Azrael sends them to Inza Kulchevich to ask the witch to use her powers to find Lord Soth’s elusive former servant, Caradoc. Inza can do this, but first she requires the characters to collect the cataclysmic flame from three ruins across the domain in a magical iron pendant. Each flame is guarded by a shadow creature that resembles some foe from Soth’s twisted memories—like a lance-wielding [**Warrior Commander**](#ref:monster:5195258-warrior-commander "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195258-warrior-commander") or a pompous [**Archpriest**](#ref:monster:5194903-archpriest "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194903-archpriest").

**Levels 10–14.** Inza uses the pendant to reveal that Caradoc’s soul is trapped in a black sapphire that Azrael already possesses. The characters must recover the gem. They might then infiltrate Nedragaard Keep to offer it to [**Lord Soth**](#ref:monster:6491370-lord-soth-of-sithicus "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491370-lord-soth-of-sithicus"). Soth is fickle though, and this approach might end in a confrontation with the death knight.

### Shadow of the Black Rose {#rthw:sithicus:adventuresinsithicus:shadowoftheblackrose}

Adventure for Level 14 Characters

**Situation.** The characters seek to escape Sithicus or the Domains of Dread.

**Hook.** Inza Kulchevich (Medium, Neutral Evil [**Death Cultist**](#ref:monster:5194960-death-cultist "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194960-death-cultist")) offers to tell the characters how to escape Sithicus if they recover a magical gem from Lord Soth’s seneschal, Azrael Dax.

[![Map 2.31: Sithicus Ruins View Player Version (Artist: NerdyMaps)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/map-16-03.sithicus-ruins.jpg)](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/map-16-03.sithicus-ruins.jpg)

#### Encounters {#rthw:sithicus:adventuresinsithicus:shadowoftheblackrose:encounters}

The adventure consists of these encounters.

**_Inza’s Quest._** The witch Inza loathes Lord Soth. She knows he has tasked Azrael with recovering a magical gem that holds the spirit of the Darklord’s treacherous former ally, the knight Caradoc. Inza gives the characters an iron pendant bearing an image of a rose that’s missing its central gem. She asks them to follow Azrael to a ruin in the Misttop Mountains, where he hunts the missing gem, and recover it before him.

**_Inza’s Secret._** Inza doesn’t share that she seeks to release Caradoc to provoke Soth into a conflict with the characters.

**_Searching the Ruins._** The ruins where Azrael hunts for the gem appear on map 2.31. For every 10 minutes the characters are exploring, roll 1d6 on the table below for an encounter. For each previous encounter rolled on this table, add 1 to the result.

**Encounters in the Ruins**

**_Kender Nosferatu._** A morbidly whimsical pair of [**Vampire Nosferatu**](#ref:monster:6491398-vampire-nosferatu "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491398-vampire-nosferatu") (see [chapter 5](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/denizens-of-the-mists#vampirenosferatu)) pesters the characters, hoping to be entertained. If the characters don’t keep them amused, the nosferatu attack. Because these monsters were kender in life—mischievous folk from the world of Krynn—they are Small.

**_Shadow Highlord._** A dragon rider made of shadows guards a pillar of illusory violet flame. It reflects one of Lord Soth’s warped memories and uses the stat blocks of a [**Dullahan**](#ref:monster:6491351-dullahan "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491351-dullahan") (see [chapter 5](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/denizens-of-the-mists#dullahan)) riding a [**Young Blue Dragon**](#ref:monster:5195278-young-blue-dragon "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195278-young-blue-dragon"), followed by a withered [**Death’s Head**](#ref:monster:6491350-deaths-head "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491350-deaths-head") (see [chapter 5](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/denizens-of-the-mists#deathshead)). The monsters are [**Hostile**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#hostileattitude). If they’re defeated, the nearby pillar of flames shows a scene of Lord Soth being knighted and grants a character the benefits noted in “[Cataclysmic Flames](#cataclysmicflames).” If you roll this encounter a second time, treat it as a meeting with Azrael Dax.

**_Azrael Dax._** Azrael Dax is Lord Soth’s dwarf seneschal and a murderous werebadger (use the [**Werebear**](#ref:monster:5195263-werebear "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195263-werebear") stat block). Azrael and the six [**Strahd Skeletons**](#ref:monster:6491389-strahd-skeleton "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491389-strahd-skeleton") (see [chapter 5](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/rthw/denizens-of-the-mists#strahdskeleton)) attending him are Hostile. If reduced to 50 Hit Points or fewer, Azrael bargains for his life and offers the characters the black sapphire they’re looking for, which he’s been hiding from Soth for moons. After this, don’t roll for more encounters in the ruins.

**_Repairing the Pendant._** Placing the black gem in Inza’s iron amulet releases Caradoc (Neutral Evil [**Ghost**](#ref:monster:5195008-ghost "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195008-ghost")). The arrogant creature is [**Indifferent**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#indifferentattitude) to the characters. If they don’t free Caradoc, Inza does when the group returns the gem and pendant to her.

**_Soth Appears._** Three rounds after Caradoc is freed, [**Lord Soth of Sithicus**](#ref:monster:6491370-lord-soth-of-sithicus "https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6491370-lord-soth-of-sithicus") (see earlier in [this chapter](#lordsoth)) appears from the shadows. Soth fights Caradoc and mistakes the characters for the ghost’s allies. A character might convince him otherwise—see “[Influencing Lord Soth](#influencinglordsoth).”

**_Escape._** Characters who flee or defeat Lord Soth gain an additional 6,200 XP. Those who change the Darklord’s attitude to [**Friendly**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#friendlyattitude) or help him defeat Caradoc can convince him to let them leave Sithicus. Defeating Soth also causes the domain’s borders to open. If the characters confront Inza about her method for leaving Sithicus, she cryptically explains that the only route to do so leads through Soth, encouraging them to seek the Darklord’s aid or confront the death knight again.
