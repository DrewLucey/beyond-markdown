You’ve pledged your loyalty to a member-city of the [Lords’ Alliance](https://www.dndbeyond.com/feats/2158469-lords-alliance-agent). As an Alliance agent, you must uphold the tenets of the Alliance and seek to increase safety and prosperity along the [Sword Coast](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/frhof/a-guide-to-the-realms#SwordCoast). You’re sworn to bring honor and glory to your lord’s house, whether that means securing trade roads for a merchant-lord of [Waterdeep](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/frhof/a-guide-to-the-realms#Waterdeep) or vanquishing monsters upriver of Daggerford. You’ve trained in the arts of swordplay and statecraft and are as deft with a blade as you are with a quill.
