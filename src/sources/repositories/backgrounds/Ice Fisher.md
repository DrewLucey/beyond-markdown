You come from a proud line of ice fishers out of Ten-Towns in [Icewind Dale](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/frhof/a-guide-to-the-realms#IcewindDale). Catching knucklehead trout isn’t the most glorious trade in [the North](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/frhof/a-guide-to-the-realms#TheNorth), but it’s an honest living. You’ve trained your senses for the slightest tug on the line, wrestled big trout out of ice-covered lakes, and gutted enough knucklehead trout to feed your village many times over. These experiences have toughened your body and mind for a life of adventuring.
