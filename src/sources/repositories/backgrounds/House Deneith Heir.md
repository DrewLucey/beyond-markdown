You’ve learned the importance of duty, honor, and the laws used to govern society when duty and honor fail. As you established your name in the house, you might have worked as a bodyguard for some minor dignitary, as a mercenary, or as a marshal hunting fugitives. Now you’re prepared to claim your rightful standing in the mightiest of the dragonmarked houses.
