**Ability Scores:** Strength, Constitution, Wisdom  
**Feat:** [Healer](https://www.dndbeyond.com/feats/1789152-healer)  
**Skill Proficiencies:** Medicine and Survival  
**Tool Proficiency:** Leatherworker’s Tools  
**Equipment:** _Choose A or B:_ (A) Greatclub, Leatherworker’s Tools, Bedroll, Blanket, Healer’s Kit, Pole, Rations (3 days’ worth), Tent, Tinderbox, 5 Torches, Traveler’s Clothes, Waterskin, 32 GP; or (B) 50 GP

The dead magic zones of the [Anauroch](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/frhof/a-guide-to-the-realms#Anauroch) desert are anathema to spellcasters and monsters that rely on magic—which is exactly why you made your life there. Perhaps you’re on the run from [Red Wizards](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/frhof/factions-of-the-realms#RedWizards), or you ran afoul of a powerful djinni in [Calimshan](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/frhof/a-guide-to-the-realms#Calimshan). Whatever the case, you decided that living in Anauroch was your best option. After long months or years, you’re stronger, wiser, and armed with hard-earned knowledge of desert medicine and wasteland survival.
